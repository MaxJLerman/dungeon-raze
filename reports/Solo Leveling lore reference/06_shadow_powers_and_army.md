# 06. Shadow Monarch powers and the shadow army

**Covers:** how Sung Jinwoo's (Sung Jin-woo's) shadow powers work in Solo Leveling: extraction, storage, regeneration, grades, the place-swap, the domain buff, shared senses, army size and tactics, every named shadow, the army inherited from Ashborn, his other Monarch powers, and how shadows look and sound, across the novel, the manhwa, the anime, the sequel Ragnarok and the games. DungeonRaze dropped its shadow-raising class, so this chapter is background only.

**Date:** 2026-11-16. Episodes are given as season and episode (S2E13 is the season 2 finale); "ch." is a manhwa chapter unless it says novel.

**Source markers:** `[O]` official or licensed text and staff interviews. `[P]` press, explainer, recap or review site, which here includes Wikipedia and Baidu Baike. `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki. `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet. `[F]` fan site, forum or fan opinion. `[low]` added where the research marked the fact low confidence. A fact with `[W]` and another marker has independent support; one with `[W]` alone does not, and chapter references carrying only `[W]` are the wiki's own and unchecked. Two of the supporting outlets, Beebom and Game Rant, visibly lean on the wiki, so their agreement with it is a second published source and not always an independent one. The link after a marker goes to one non-wiki source. The wiki material was obtained through the wiki's API after the site refused automated reads, and the project owner chose on 2026-11-16 to keep it, labelled.

## At a glance

1. **Raising a shadow.** Jinwoo speaks a command word ("Arise") over a corpse and its soul rises as a loyal soldier. He gets three tries per target, and a third failure loses the soul for good. [W][P]
2. **What decides success.** The odds fall as the target's strength exceeds his and as time passes since death. No source gives a formula or a time limit. A boss stronger than he is can be beaten and still not taken. [W][P]
3. **What can never be raised.** Monarchs and Rulers (they have no body left to raise), demons of the Demon Castle (contaminated mana) and, until his full awakening, anything without mana. [W][P]
4. **Two early caps.** He could extract 30 but store only 20, so he had to choose whom to keep. Both caps grow with his level and mana and stop mattering late. The numbers are the wiki's alone. [W]
5. **Free to field, costly to lose.** Shadows wait inside his own shadow and, per the wiki, cost nothing to call out. Mana is spent rebuilding destroyed soldiers, and when it hits zero the army leaves the field. One very strong enemy drains him faster than a horde does. [W][P]
6. **Shadows do not die.** Any shadow re-forms after any damage while he has mana. Only a Monarch-level attack, his own release of the shadow, or his death ends one. [W][P]
7. **Grades.** Eight steps from Basic to the single Grand Marshal. A shadow can be named at Knight grade, can speak at General grade, levels by killing, and moves up a grade only with the Monarch's approval. A strong corpse can start high. [W][P]
8. **What carries over.** A shadow keeps its skills, gear and one signature look from life, and can use equipment handed to it. It loses anything that needed a living body. [W][P]
9. **The place-swap.** He swaps places instantly with any shadow at any distance, for no mana, on a three-hour cooldown that shortens with skill level. It needs a shadow already at the destination. After the full awakening it has no cooldown and needs no anchor. [W][P]
10. **The zone buff.** Monarch's Domain gives every shadow inside it 50 percent to all abilities, with no cost and no skill levels. Its radius and duration are never defined. [W][P]
11. **Planted shadows.** A shadow hidden in another person's shadow is a bodyguard, an alarm, a scout and a swap anchor at once, and almost no one can sense it. [W][P]
12. **Scale.** Twenty to thirty at the start, hundreds by the end of the anime, about 100,000 on inheriting Ashborn's army, about ten million at the end of the story. The last two figures have no chapter citation. [W][P]
13. **Structure.** A handful of named shadows with clear roles (duelist, taunting tank, charger and mount, caster, flyer, fast healer, commander) lead anonymous troop types. [W][P]
14. **Look.** A black silhouette with one glow colour (light purple in the manhwa, bluer in the anime), smoke at the edges, glowing eyes, and a kneel on being raised. [W][P]

## 1. Shadow Extraction

Shadow Extraction raises a dead enemy as a loyal shadow soldier. It is the skill the whole army is built on.

### 1.1 How it is gained and used

- Jinwoo gains it from the job change quest, with the Shadow Monarch title. In the anime the quest runs through S1E11 (24 March 2024) and S1E12 "Arise" (31 March 2024). [P] [Wikipedia S1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling_season_1)
- The skill first appears in novel chapter 52, manhwa chapter 45 and the anime's twelfth episode. [W]
- He stands over a corpse and speaks a command word. The soul rises as a shadow soldier that obeys him. [P] [ANIHK](https://anihk.com/en/blogs/characters/solo-leveling-sung-jin-woo-abilities-evolution-guide)
- The command word is the user's own choice. Jinwoo picks "Arise" (manhwa chapter 45). [W]
- The System's skill text calls it a "Shadow Power" that needs no mana. It draws the mana out of a lifeless body to make the soldier, and its failure rate rises with the target's stats and with the time since death. [W]
- It works over a wide area, so hundreds of corpses can be raised in one use. [W] CBR agrees there is no limit on the number of targets or the area. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-sung-jinwoo-major-abilities-skills-explained/)
- It works on monsters and humans alike. [P] [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/anime/solo-leveling-monarch-of-shadows-explained-2620753/) Only three humans become shadows in the story: Kim Chul, Min Byung-Gyu and Hwang Dongsoo. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-powerful-new-shadows-season-3/)
- When first unlocked it could extract up to 30 shadows "at his current level". [W]
- Jinwoo can cancel an extraction after it succeeds, which ends that shadow. He does this with Min Byung-Gyu and with the Ant Queen. No source describes how the cancellation works. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-why-jin-woo-released-byung-gu-shadow-jeju-island-explained/)
- Part of each shadow's power in life stayed sealed after extraction until Jinwoo became the true Shadow Monarch. [W]

## 2. Storage and its caps

### 2.1 Where shadows wait

- The storage skill arrives together with Shadow Extraction. It is Shadow Preservation in the manhwa and Shadow Storage in the anime. [W][S] [TVmao recap](https://en.tvmao.com/tv-shows/solo-leveling-season-1-episode-12.html)
- Shadows are kept inside Jinwoo's own shadow. He can call them out anywhere and pull them back in at will, from any distance. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-sung-jinwoo-major-abilities-skills-explained/)
- Most characters assume he summons from another dimension. The soldiers are in fact always inside his shadow, which is why calling them out costs nothing. Lennart Niermann works this out in manhwa chapter 142. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-shadows/)
- Stored shadows are held indefinitely in a stasis-like state (a search snippet of the wiki's own page). [S]
- The skill also lets him sense whether his shadows are all right and check their stats. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-a-complete-guide-to-all-the-abilities-of-jinwoos-system/)
- Stored soldiers cannot be sensed by almost anyone. Top S-rank hunters including Goto Ryuji and Cha Hae-In could not detect them (manhwa chapter 93). Esil's father and Lennart Niermann could. [W]
- After the full awakening the same shadow space doubles as an unlimited item inventory. [W]
- Ashborn's version is described as a dimensional storage that preserves and monitors the shadows. [P] [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/how-strong-is-ashborn-solo-leveling-powers-abilities-explained) ComicBook.com, reporting a fan theory, likens the fortress in the anime's season 2 ending sequence to Ashborn's pocket dimension where the army lives. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-ending-ashborn/)

- There are two caps early on, one on how many can be extracted and one on how many can be kept. The gap forces him to choose which soldiers to keep. [W]
- The anime's 11 released is consistent with the wiki's figures (30 raised, 11 released, Igris added, 20 kept), but that is arithmetic across two sources and neither states it. [P]
- Storage is finite, grows as his level and mana grow, and early on makes him release weaker soldiers. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-barca-shadow-army/)
- No normally read source gives a capacity number at any stage. The 30, 20, 820 and 155 figures are the wiki's alone.

### 2.3 Mana

- Stored shadows use no mana. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-shadows/)
- The wiki says calling them out is also free and that mana is spent only on rebuilding destroyed soldiers. [W] CBR speaks more loosely of mana going on repeated summons. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-sung-jinwoo-major-abilities-skills-explained/)
- Summoning and reviving shadows against strong opponents burns through his mana, which has a ceiling. No amounts are given. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ways-jinwoo-is-still-weak/)
- The mana needed to restore a soldier scales with its strength and grade (manhwa chapter 87). [W][low]
- In the army's first real fight an alpha ice bear wipes it out and Jinwoo's mana falls to zero (manhwa chapter 51). [W]
- Against Kargalgan, regeneration drains mana faster than potions restore it, and when the mana runs out the soldiers vanish from the field (manhwa chapter 74). [W] A recap snippet for anime S2E6 has Kargalgan counting on exactly this and Jinwoo answering with a mana potion. [S] [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Recap/SoloLevelingS02E06DontLookDownOnMyGuys)
- Pulling soldiers back into storage stops the drain when a squad is being destroyed over and over (manhwa chapter 81). [W]
- The economy is therefore "free to field, costly to lose". The army is weaker against a few very strong enemies than against many weak ones. This is a reading of the rules above, not a stated one. [W]

## 3. Regeneration and what can destroy a shadow

### 3.1 Regeneration

- The shadow army is described as an immortal race of soldiers. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Every shadow, down to the weakest, rebuilds its body after any damage, including being torn apart or reduced to nothing. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-shadows/)
- Regeneration is unlimited in count and runs until the master's mana is fully spent (manhwa chapter 165). [W][P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-why-jinwoo-cant-turn-monarchs-into-shadows-explainer/)
- With mana at zero the army leaves the battlefield but is not lost (manhwa chapters 51 and 74). Only the wiki states this. [W]
- Ordinary monsters and hunters, S-ranks included, cannot kill a shadow. [W]
- Shadows have no organs and do not tire, eat or age. [W]
- They cannot betray him. [P] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-monarchs-ranked/)
- After the black heart awakens, his mana is enough to regenerate the army almost without limit. The sequel calls it infinite (Ragnarok novel chapter 363). [W]

| The Beast Monarch | Rakan could have destroyed Beru for good had Jinwoo not stepped in. Jinwoo found he could not pull the restrained Beru back into storage and had to charge in to free him. | [W] |

| The timeline reset | Shadows made from monsters, such as Beru and Igris, persist with their memories. - Ashborn's own army was nearly wiped out early in the war, when Rakan and Baran ambushed him with their armies, and he went into hiding to rebuild it. [W]
- No source explains how a Monarch's attack gets past regeneration, how long a destroyed shadow takes to re-form, or where it re-forms.

## 4. The grade ladder

Two translations name the upper grades differently. The novel's Commander is the manhwa's General, and the novel's Marshal is the manhwa's Commander, so "Commander" means two different grades depending on the release. [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling) This chapter uses **General** for the lower of the two and **Marshal** for the higher, which is what most press coverage does.

- Game Rant's 2024 ladder follows the wiki's wording closely, so it is weak independent support for the wiki's grade descriptions.

### 4.2 Growth and promotion

- Unlike other hunters' summons, shadows grow. They level up by killing in battle and can surpass what they were in life. [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Moving up a grade needs the Shadow Monarch's direct authorisation. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-shadows/)
- A shadow made from a very strong being can start at a higher grade. Beru starts at General. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-shadows/)
- A shadow's strength depends on its master. Under Suho, veterans such as Iron and Greed are weaker, which Beru puts down to differences in Shadow Authority, the Intelligence stat and level. [W]
- Kills made by shadows still give Jinwoo experience, and the soldiers gained ten levels during the split-army phase of the Demon Castle climb (manhwa chapter 81). [W][low]
- Jima is set to level 13 when raised (manhwa chapter 122), about the level of an S-rank hunter. It is the only per-shadow level number found. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-powerful-new-shadows-season-3/)

### 4.3 What a shadow keeps from life

- Shadows keep their abilities: Min Byung-Gyu's shadow heals, Greed keeps his S-rank skills, Tusk keeps his spells. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-all-sung-jinwoo-shadows-ranked-by-powers-and-abilities/)
- Anything that needed a living body is lost. The Ant Queen cannot lay eggs as a shadow, which is why she is weak, and Tusk loses Kargalgan's telepathy. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-shadow-soldier-fights/)
- Shadows can use equipment handed to them. Igris receives the Demon King's Longsword (manhwa chapter 113) and Tusk the Orb of Avarice. An ordinary shadow mage used the orb before Tusk existed. [W][F] [Quote The Anime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/igris-wiki/)

### 4.4 Speech

- Speech starts at General grade, and only in the monster tongue. Jinwoo follows it because the System translates for him. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-shadows/)
- Beru also speaks human languages. The wiki states the cause as fact: his Gluttony skill gave him the languages of the hunters he ate. Game Rant thinks that likely, since he ate Min Byung-Gyu, but says the story never settles it, and notes Jinwoo himself credits the General grade. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-how-can-beru-speak-human-language-explained/)
- Human-derived shadows such as Iron and Min Byung-Gyu could not speak. [W]
- Igris speaks only late in the manhwa, after his promotion to Marshal. Beru was the only shadow able to talk until manhwa chapter 167. A fan page gives Igris's line as "My Liege", with no chapter. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-every-major-shadow-origins-explained/)

### 9.2 Notes on each shadow

**Igris**

- In life he wore blood-red (deep crimson) armour with a four-horned helmet. The anime's title for him is the Bloodred Knight Commander. [W][P] [Wikipedia S1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling_season_1)
- In the boss fight he drops his weapon and the two finish with bare strength. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/every-shadow-part-of-sung-jin-woos-army-solo-leveling-anime/)
- The scarred eye is the left in the manhwa and the right in the anime. [W]
- His telekinesis is "Ruler's Hand" on the wiki and one fan guide and "Dominator's Touch" on a fan page. [W][F] [Quote The Anime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/igris-wiki/)
- Under Ashborn he was one of the "Two Wings" of the army. [W]
- Personality: loyal and tactical, kneels after battles, dislikes Iron's foolishness. He brings Jinwoo the heads of enemies, which Iron copied until told to stop. Later he argues with Bellion about Suho's schooling. [P][F] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- A fan page lists more moments by manhwa chapter: beaten by Jinwoo after underestimating him (40), thrown aside by Baran in one blow (87), loses the title of strongest shadow to Beru at Jeju (101 to 107), loses to Cha Hae-In in the Ahjin Guild test (113), saves a girl from a giant in Japan (134). [F] [Quote The Anime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/igris-wiki/)
- He is a rival to Beru and second to Bellion. Netmarble's Karma material calls him the first shadow Jinwoo named. The name is a shortening, because the original was too long. [P] [iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/who-is-stronger-beru-or-igris-in-solo-leveling/)

**Iron**

- Named because "Chul" means iron in Korean. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- Personality: slow-witted, a loose cannon who goes overboard and is proud of his kills, and a copier of Igris. Friendlier than the man was. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-every-major-shadow-origins-explained/)
- High orcs resisted his taunt. [W]
- He reverts to the human Kim Chul after the timeline reset and becomes Iron again under Suho. [W]

**Tank**

- Named for his durability, like a war tank. He is named only on reaching Knight grade. [W][P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- The ordinary ice bear shadows are about B-rank. [P] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-strongest-shadows-sung-jinwoo/)

**Tusk**

- Named for Kargalgan's large tusks, because Jinwoo could not recall his name. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- As the living Kargalgan he could speak human language, cause paralysing fear and grow in size. As a shadow he loses the telepathy and works as a support mage. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-kargalgan-jin-woo-shadow-tusk-explained/)
- The orb gives 100 percent amplification, against a 50 percent ceiling for crafted items. [W]
- The wiki lists twelve hymns: Protection (shield), Fire Dragon (fire beam), Blazing Fire (fireball volley), Combustion, Agony (pain curse), Frenzy (lowers a target's defence), Blindness, Slumber (drains stamina), Lethargy (holds a target in place), Rage, Strengthening (buff) and Giants (grows huge). Its notes on Rage and Combustion are guesses. [W]
- Game Rant says each hymn is tied to a zodiac constellation. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-a-complete-guide-to-sung-jinwoos-shadows-in-the-anime/)
- Personality: obedient, quiet and shy, but competitive with the other shadows. [W]

**Kaisel**

- Named Kaisel because Jinwoo could not recall the full name. TechWiser says he was unsure between Kaisellin and Karkallin. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- Wikipedia's anime summary calls the living wyvern a white dragon. [P] [Wikipedia S2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling_season_2)
- The Escapist says he had earlier been Ashborn's, without explaining. [P] [The Escapist](https://www.escapistmagazine.com/sung-jin-woos-strongest-shadows-in-solo-leveling-ranked/) The wiki says Ashborn captured Baran's wyvern Kaisellin after killing Baran, which may be what is meant. [W]

**Beru**

- Named after Bernard Werber, author of Empire of the Ants. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- Netmarble's material for the game Arise calls him the original work's first General-rank shadow. [P] [iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/who-is-stronger-beru-or-igris-in-solo-leveling/)
- Personality: brutal and battle-hungry, condescending to other shadows, fiercely loyal, fond of Suho. He speaks in an archaic style picked up from Korean period dramas, calling the other shadows peasants and Jinwoo's mother the Empress Dowager. At first he struggles to hold back his urge to kill. [W][P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-beru-best-shadow/)
- The anime shows more of his ice magic than the manhwa. [W]

**Jima**

- TechWiser says Jinwoo was too lazy to think of a more meaningful name. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- The living boss fought with claws and tridents. Game Rant in 2026 calls his power unimpressive. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-shadows-season-3/)
- No source describes his personality.

**Greed**

- Named for his greedy, selfish nature in life. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- Personality: aware of his crimes in life, so humble and servile toward Jinwoo, unlike the living man, but bloodthirsty in battle. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-every-major-shadow-origins-explained/)
- Game Rant's text also calls him a "Knight Commander", which it does not reconcile with General. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-powerful-new-shadows-season-3/)

**Bellion**

- Not named by Jinwoo. [P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-meaning-of-names-jinwoo-gives-to-his-shadow-soldiers/)
- He arrives after the Beast, Frost and Plague Monarchs are beaten. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-strongest-shadow-bellion-explainer/)
- Personality: composed, loyal to both Shadow Monarchs, strict about discipline. Igris is noted as the better pure swordsman. [W]
- His only animated appearance so far is an opening A-1 Pictures made for Netmarble's game Karma, in a design CBR calls faithful to the manhwa. It is a game trailer, not the series. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-karma-opening-previews-bellion-anime-design/)

**The giants and "No. 6"**

- The giants are named by number in summoning order, No. 1 to 29 on the wiki. There had been 30 giants, but one crossed to China and was killed by Liu Zhigang. [W]
- They had no boss, so they were made to fight and the winner became leader. [W]
- "No. 6" is a giant's number, not a name. The wiki singles No. 6 out as the exception to its statement that each giant has S-rank power, without saying in which direction. Game Rant says the giants were numbered 1 through 6. No source profiles that giant. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-powerful-new-shadows-season-3/)

## 10. The inherited army

### 10.1 Ashborn

- Ashborn is the King of the Dead and Monarch of Shadows, the strongest Ruler and the Greatest Fragment of Brilliant Light. Tired of the ancient war, he chose Jinwoo as his host to end it. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- He was the Absolute Being's most loyal Ruler. He once had six wings and tore them off on becoming a Monarch. A power hidden in him surfaced after the Rulers defeated the Absolute Being and gave him the powers of death. [P] [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/how-strong-is-ashborn-solo-leveling-powers-abilities-explained)
- The Absolute Being had planted the shadow power in his most loyal servant as a way to destroy his creations if they rebelled (the wiki cites manhwa chapter 192; the main run ends at chapter 179, so this number is unchecked). [W]
- Two Monarchs betrayed him. Rakan and Baran ambushed him with their armies and nearly annihilated his own, and he went into hiding to rebuild it. He killed Baran; the other escaped. Epicstream calls the second traitor "Karan". [W][P] [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/how-strong-is-ashborn-solo-leveling-powers-abilities-explained)
- He absorbed Baran's corpse into his shadow under his cape (manhwa chapter 163) and captured Baran's wyvern Kaisellin. [W]
- The Rulers asked his forgiveness and he became neutral. He turned on the Architect when Jinwoo reached level 100 and gave Jinwoo his black heart. [P] [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/how-strong-is-ashborn-solo-leveling-powers-abilities-explained)
- His powers match Jinwoo's: Shadow Extraction, storage, Shadow Exchange, Monarch's Domain and Ruler's Authority. Beebom says Jinwoo surpassed him. [P] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-monarchs-ranked/)
- Only three characters ever hold Shadow Extraction: Ashborn, Jinwoo and Suho. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-sung-jinwoo-major-abilities-skills-explained/)
- As Shadow Monarch he is a large armoured figure with flame-like glowing purple hair, glowing purple eyes, two curved black horns, claws, jet-black armour, a smoky black cloak and a pure black sword. [W]
- The size of his army at its height is given only as "millions", in a search snippet with no chapter. [S] [Fandomwire](https://fandomwire.com/every-monarch-in-solo-leveling-ranked-by-power/)

### 10.2 What Jinwoo inherits and when

- When the Monarchs kill Jinwoo, Ashborn appears, revives him, hands over his remaining powers and enters eternal sleep. Jinwoo takes his place. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Shortly after Jinwoo kills the three Monarchs who attacked him, Bellion and the rest of Ashborn's army come through a gigantic gate over Seoul and pledge themselves to him (manhwa chapter 167). The Hunters Association had taken the gate for a threat needing every S-rank in the world. [W][P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-strongest-shadow-bellion-explainer/)
- Bellion had stayed at Ashborn's side from before Ashborn became the Shadow Monarch. He arrives once the System is gone and Jinwoo has full control of the power, recognises Ashborn in him and accepts him as successor. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-strongest-shadow-bellion-explainer/)
- With that army Jinwoo has over 100,000 shadows. Baidu Baike's Bellion page, citing the Chinese edition, gives 100,000; Game Rant says "hundreds of thousands". [W][P] [Baidu Baike](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Bellion/3372153)
- Baidu Baike also says the army lay dormant in the dimensional rift for a thousand years. No other source repeats this. [P] [Baidu Baike](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Bellion/3372153)
- The inherited army contains shadow heavenly infantry (former winged soldiers of the Rulers, each at least S-rank), a shadow steed, ancestral dragons, ice elves, high orcs, goblins, giants, a rock golem and a lizard. [W]
- Igris also served Ashborn long ago. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling) The wiki calls him one of the old army's "Two Wings". [W]
- On full inheritance the sealed part of every shadow's power is released, Igris and Beru rise to Marshal, Shadow Exchange loses its limits, targets without mana become extractable and Jinwoo stops ageing. [W][P] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-strongest-shadows-sung-jinwoo/)
- The "ten million" figure belongs to two other things: Antares's army (manhwa chapter 173) and Jinwoo's own army after the 27-year rewound war. It is not the size of Ashborn's army at the handover. [W][P] [Epic Dope](https://www.epicdope.com/is-solo-leveling-the-manhwa-finished/)
- The anime has not reached any of this.

## 12. How shadows look and sound

### 12.1 The manhwa's look

- The standard shadow is a black body or black armour with a light purple glow. Igris, Iron, Tank, Beru and Greed are all described this way. Kaisel and Jima are black-scaled with glowing purple parts. [W][F] [Quote The Anime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/igris-wiki/)
- Purple is the colour of Ashborn's power in general. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-ending-ashborn/)
- Exceptions: Tusk has glowing red skin and glowing blue eyes, and Min Byung-Gyu's shadow has bright glowing blue skin. [W]
- Smoke and flame at the edges: Beru's smoky wings, Greed's smoke-like hair, the smoky quality of Iron's upgraded axe, Tank's flaming strip of hair, Ashborn's smoky cloak and flame-like hair. [W]
- Eyes are the only face: Beru's are "neon", Greed's glowing red, Tusk's glowing blue, Bellion's and Min Byung-Gyu's blank white. [W]
- Shadows keep recognisable features and wounds from life: Igris's eye scar and plume, Iron's armour, Tank's scars. [W]
- Colours invert on extraction. Kaisellin goes from white scales to black with a purple glow, Kamish from red to black with purple horns, Igris from red armour to black. [W][F] [Quote The Anime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/igris-wiki/)
- Jinwoo's eyes glow purple when he uses his powers, and weapons take on a light purple edge in his hands. [W]
- Size: Iron's shadow rises larger than his living body. The giants, Jima and Tusk tower over buildings at full size. [W]
- Extraction is drawn as black mist rising from the corpse (Iron) or dark energy gathering round the body (Tusk, Beru). Summoning is soldiers rising out of darkness spreading on the ground. [W][low]
- Monarch's Domain is his shadow spreading across the floor under the soldiers (manhwa chapter 73). [W]
- A new shadow's first act is usually to kneel. Igris kneels after being summoned or finishing a fight. [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- The manhwa's shadows are expressive and often comic, using gestures and faces, with chibi panels. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-season-2-biggest-manhwa-changes/)
- Living Igris bleeds white in the manhwa and purple in the anime. [W]

### 12.2 The anime's look

- The anime leans blue. The wiki's summary of the Kargalgan episode describes "bluish-glowing" soldiers rising when he says "Arise". [W] Jinwoo's eyes glow blue in battle, and a close-up at the end of the season 2 ending theme shows them a startling blue instead of grey. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-ending-ashborn/)
- In S1E12, during the job change, purple light surrounds Jinwoo and his eyes shift from blue to purple. After Igris rises he kneels and the other shadows follow; Igris and the first 30 kneel together at the end of the episode. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-episode-12-review/)
- A fan post says Igris and Iron glow blue in season 2 until Jinwoo activates Monarch's Domain, when they turn purple. A snippet of another Fandom-hosted wiki says the same of the first shadow knights and admits it is unclear why the colours change. Neither is a staff statement. [S][F] [Threads post](https://www.threads.com/@hykalmahmood/post/DF4JvKwSr81/from-blue-change-into-purple-iron-igris-chance-colour-after-jin-woo-activating-m)
- No staff statement explains blue against purple. Three staff interviews were checked and none discusses the glow colour.
- Opinion: ComicBook.com finds the anime's Beru "way too blue" and plain in his debut next to the manhwa's glowing-eyed, menacing version. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-levelings-season-2-beru-baran-designs)
- For a non-shadow comparison, the anime makes Baran bright purple where the webtoon has him grey with a purple tint; the novel gives no colours. ComicBook.com guesses the saturation was raised so he would stand out from a bleak setting. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-baran-purple-change/)
- The army is 3D. Producer Atsushi Kaneko of A-1 Pictures says it was first planned in 2D and moved to 3D after tests, because the soldiers carry little visual information in colour or line and looked thin on screen in 2D. Named characters are hand-drawn, and the 3D army is composited into the 2D shots. [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/maximizing-the-strength-of-anime-for-solo-leveling/)
- In S2E6 a 26-second single cut of Igris and Iron fighting high orcs was animated by Yoshihiro Kanno. The background high orcs are CG models with varied tattoos and colours so the shared model does not show. [O] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/how-the-solo-leveling-anime-was-made-behind-the-scenes-at-a-1-pictures-ahead-of-the-season-2-finale/)
- A fan commentary argues the anime swapped the manhwa's smoky rendering for solid black designs to keep animation quality up. [S][F] [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/solo-leveling-i-cant-really-blame-a-1-pictures-for-nerfing-sung-jinwoos-shadow-soldiers-designs/)
- The anime plays the shadows as silent, emotionless soldiers, and drops the manhwa's chibi panels and the glow in Jinwoo's eyes before he finishes an opponent. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-season-2-differences-between-anime-manhwa/)
- The season 2 ending sequence shows an empty throne like the one Igris guarded, an alabaster fortress and a shot of the dormant black heart. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-ending-ashborn/)
- Official shadow visuals were released for Igris (with a battle clip, after S2E1) and later for Tusk, Tank and Iron on the official site. The articles reporting them do not describe them. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/igrit-from-solo-leveling-season-2-gets-shadow-visual-and-battle-video-following-episode-1-premiere/)

## 14. Where sources conflict

Each entry gives the competing claims, then the better-supported one.

**Extraction**

1. **Does extraction cost mana?** The wiki's skill text and CBR say none; mana goes on repeated summons and repairs. Game Rant says it uses mana, from the anime's S1E12 description of draining mana to make soldiers. Better supported: no cost. The anime wording can as easily mean the corpse's mana. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-sung-jinwoo-major-abilities-skills-explained/)
2. **How many tries on Baruka?** The wiki says all three failed. Game Rant, Animehunch, Epic Dope and Beebom give no count. Unconfirmed either way. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-barca-shadow-army/)
3. **Did Kargalgan's extraction fail first?** The wiki says the first attempts failed. No article that describes the scene mentions a failure. Unconfirmed. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-kargalgan-jin-woo-shadow-tusk-explained/)
4. **The Ant Queen.** TechWiser says her extraction was interrupted and her fate is uncertain. The wiki, ComicBook.com, Screen Rant and CBR say she was extracted on the first try and then dismissed. Better supported: extracted and dismissed. [W][P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-season-2-biggest-manhwa-changes/)
5. **Who kills Kim Chul?** Wikipedia's episode summary says Jinwoo; its character list and the wiki say Igris. Better supported: Igris. [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
6. **Kamish.** The chapter is 115 per Game Rant and 142 to 143 per the wiki; the wiki's number fits its other references to the same arc (Lennart Niermann at chapter 142).
