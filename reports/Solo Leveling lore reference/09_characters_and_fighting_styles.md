# 9. Characters and how they fight

**Covers:** how the human hunters of Solo Leveling fight, class by class and character by character, with their weapons, named skills, looks, fates and chapter references, plus the fights between hunters and the names used in other editions.

**Date:** 2026-11-16. The two aired anime seasons end at the Jeju raid, so everything later is manhwa and novel material.

**Source markers:** `[O]` official or licensed text; `[P]` press, explainer, recap or review site, including Wikipedia and MyAnimeList; `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki, whose chapter numbers are its own and unchecked; `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet; `[F]` fan site or fan opinion; `[low]` added where the fact is low confidence. A fact with `[W]` and `[P]` or `[O]` has independent support and one with `[W]` alone does not; the main fan site was found to be rewritten from the wiki, so `[F]` plus `[W]` is one source, not two. The wiki material was obtained through the wiki's API after the site refused automated reads, and the project owner chose on 2026-11-16 to keep it, labelled.

## At a glance

- **Six classes.** Fighter, Tanker, Assassin, Mage, Ranger and Healer. "Support" is a description (barrier mages, buffers), not a confirmed seventh class. A balanced party is a fighter, an assassin, a tanker, a ranger and a healer around a mage. [P] [F]
- **Class is a label, not a limit.** The protagonist is listed as a Mage and fights as an assassin with twin daggers. The world's top Tanker carries no shield and brawls bare-handed. A C-rank fire Mage fights with a sword. [P] [W] [F]
- **The protagonist's dagger kit** is passive dagger mastery, vital-point strikes that grow into rapid chained slashes, thrown daggers, a speed boost, invisibility, an intimidation skill that halves a target's stats, and telekinesis. Late on he wins bare-handed. [W] [F]
- **One signature set per exemplar.** Sword fighter: speed chains, a light blade, a ground-stab quake. Beast fighter: claws and a partial or full tiger form. Fire mage: spears, a wall, a dragon, a bow. Tank: shield, Taunt and a body-hardening or growing skill. Ranger: up to five conjured arrows. Healer: heal, buff and a stand-still invisibility. [P] [F] [W]
- **Every class has a stated weakness.** Mages and rangers lose at close range. Taunt only works on weaker opponents. The healer's invisibility roots him. The full beast form costs reasoning. The strongest elder can fight only briefly. [P] [W]
- **Power shows as a personal eye-glow colour and an aura.** Yellow is the most common; the protagonist's is purple. [F] [W] [P]
- **Healers and support hunters die first.** Human and monster attackers both open on the healer. [W] [P] [F]
- **Between hunters the rank gap decides.** Fights are short, happen inside gates or as sparring, and the stronger side often does not draw a weapon. Skill or a trick only delays the result. [P] [W] [F]
- **Seven humans carry a Ruler's power.** They share telekinesis and a transformation under a personal style (armoured grappler, twin swords, fire, bare hands, daggers), and overusing the power destroys the body. Four of the seven die. [P] [W]
- **The top tank's kit** is the clearest "armour up, pull, ground-slam" set: golden horned armour, a telekinetic pull likened to a black hole, a two-fist shockwave and a one-arm power punch. [P] [W]
- **Three bodies of material are kept apart here.** The original story (novel, manhwa, anime), the sequel *Ragnarok* and the games. One widely used guide mixes game material in unmarked. [P]
- **The Japanese edition renames the cast and moves the setting to Japan.** The names table lists both sets. [P] [O]

## How each class fights

Sources agree on six classes: Fighter, Tanker, Assassin, Mage, Ranger and Healer. "Support" appears only as a description. Hunters are first divided into combat and non-combat; some non-combatants still help in raids. Type says nothing about rank. [P] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-hunters-guilds-explained/), [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)

A balanced party is described as a fighter, an assassin, a tanker, a ranger and a healer around a mage. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Choi-Jong-In) The Korean S-rank roster at Jeju is that party almost exactly: sword fighter, fire mage, beast-shifter, healer, archer and shield tank.

### Fighter

- **Role:** melee damage dealer with high attack and fair defence. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-hunter-ranks-explained/)
- **Two types:** speed (Cha Haein, Goto Ryuji, Kanae Tawata) and strength (Baek Yoonho, Woo Jinchul, Kenzo Tanaka). [W]
- **Weapons:** swords, axes, knives, claws and gauntlets on the wiki's list; axes, maces, swords, spears or fists in the explainers. [W] [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)
- **Defining moves and looks:**
  - Cha Haein: a black-bladed sword and red armour, dance-like speed chains, a blade of light, a ground-stab quake for crowds. [P] [F]
  - Baek Yoonho: golden claws, a partial or full white-tiger form with white hair and fangs, eyes that read an opponent's power. [F] [P]
  - Goto Ryuji: a red katana, and a bare hand used as a blade. [F] [P]
  - Liu Zhigang: twin swords and wide energy slashes that explode on impact. [F] [P]
  - Woo Jinchul: gauntlets. [F] [P]
  - Kumamoto Atsushi: a berserk state when hurt. Tanaka Kenzo and Seo Jiwoo: unarmed brawlers. Kanae Tawata: a katana in each hand. [P]
  - Dongsoo Hwang: fists, no named skills. [F] [W]
- **Strengths:** speed or raw power in close. The speed types win on precision; the strength types tear through tough-bodied monsters. [P]
- **Weaknesses:** the Jeju fighters all fall to one faster enemy. Baek's full form costs him his reasoning, and Kumamoto's berserk state turns him on anyone nearby. [P]
- **Best shown by:** Cha Haein (speed), Baek Yoonho (strength and transformation), Liu Zhigang (the top of the class).

### Mage

- **Role:** ranged caster, weaker in body. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)
- **Types:** barrier, curse, elemental, spatial and summoning. [W] [P]
- **Tools:** staffs, rings, a magic lantern and spell books. [W]
- **Defining moves and looks:**
  - Choi Jong-In (fire): volleys of fire spears, a net or wall of fire, a small fire dragon, a bow of fire, steered fireballs. A red cape, eight rings, a cigarette as igniter. [P] [F]
  - Song Chiyul: low-rank fire magic mixed with swordsmanship. [F] [P]
  - Lee Eunseok: lightning that hits several enemies in a line. Kei: an instant wall of ice. Gina: telekinesis that throws objects. Park Heejin: a compact staff. [P] [F]
  - Lee Bora: summoned creatures that bind. Shin Seokjin: fire spirits from a lantern. [P] [W] [low]
  - Yuri Orloff: barriers (see Support). [F] [W]
- **Strengths:** area damage, crowd control and scale. Choi is rumoured to be able to bring down a building with one spell, and his mana lasted the whole Jeju raid. [P] [F]
- **Weaknesses:** fragile in melee. Choi cannot win a one-on-one close fight and needs a party in front of him. Gina is weak up close. [P] [F]
- **Best shown by:** Choi Jong-In. Jinwoo is listed as a Mage but fights as an assassin (see his profile).

### Assassin

- **Role:** the fastest class, with weak defence. Stealthy, agile close-quarters killers who try to finish a target before it can react. [W] [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)
- **Skills:** Stealth and Disguise. [W] **Ragnarok (sequel):** "Slaughter" (ch. 259). [W]
- **Defining moves and looks:** Kang Taeshik fights with daggers and throwing knives, vanishes with Stealth, opens on the healer and cripples a limb first. [F] [W] Jinwoo inherits Stealth from him and fights in the same idiom. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-9)
- **Strengths:** the first strike, invisibility, picking off support members.
- **Weaknesses:** weak defence. Taeshik is undone by a stat-halving intimidation skill and a self-heal he did not expect. [W] [F]
- **Examples:** Ippei Izawa (S-rank), Kang Taeshik (B-rank), Sukmin (D-rank). [W]
- **Best shown by:** Kang Taeshik, and Jinwoo's dagger style.

### Tanker

(Also "Tank".)

- **Role:** trades agility for heavy defence and uses aggro skills to pull monsters off weaker members. What tankers share is drawing the enemy's attention and stopping a charge. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-hunter-ranks-explained/)
- **Shared skills:** Taunt magically goads enemies into attacking the user without thinking, but only works on opponents weaker than the user (debut ch. 19). Strengthening wraps the body in a mana aura that hardens it like steel and raises strength. [W]
- **Defining moves and looks, by rank:**
  - Low rank: sword and shield plus Taunt (Hwang Dongsuk, Yoo Jinho). [F] [W]
  - A-rank: tower shield, axe or hammer, Taunt (Kim Chul); holding the front against a charge so that others can counter (Son Kihoon); a conjured energy shield that stops an ogre's full-power blow (Park Jongsoo); an unnamed anime tank in red plate with a magic shield and an oversized axe. [F] [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-strongest-tanker-class-hunters-ranked/)
  - S-rank: a ram-skull tower shield, a short spear, a body that grows and hardens (Ma Dongwook). [F] [P]
  - National Level: no shield at all. Golden horned armour, a telekinetic pull and a two-fist ground slam (Thomas Andre). [P] [W]
- **Strengths:** holding a boss alone (Ma against the Ant Queen), surviving what kills others.
- **Weaknesses:** Taunt fails on stronger opponents. Ma is beaten down at once by the Ant King.
- **Best shown by:** Ma Dongwook for the classic shield tank, Thomas Andre for the top of the class.
- **Game:** Go Gunhee appears on tanker lists only because *Arise* classes him as one. [P]

### Healer

- **Role:** mends wounds and buffs the party. At a severe disadvantage one on one. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)
- **Defining moves and looks:**
  - Min Byung-Gyu: regrows limbs, brings back the nearly dead, buffs, and hides himself with a stand-still invisibility skill. A mace and a spell book. [W] [P] [F]
  - Lee Joohee: heals, plus Haste and a strength buff. [F]
  - Han Semi (A-rank): can regrow limbs if she arrives in time. Shimizu Akari: Japanese S-rank healer. [P]
- **Strengths:** a healer decides whether a party survives. Min undoes the Ant Queen's poison and the Ant King's maiming.
- **Weaknesses:** enemies go for the healer first. Taeshik's first strike is at Joohee, Shimizu is the Ant King's first victim, and Min dies the moment he is found. [W] [P] [F]
- **Best shown by:** Min Byung-Gyu.
- **Game:** Antoine Martinez shows a healer reaching National Level without being a vessel. [P]

### Ranger

(Also "Archer".)

- **Role:** fights at range, mostly through archery magic, and covers the party's blind spots. Shares the Mage's weakness up close. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)
- **Defining moves and looks:** Lim Tae-Gyu is the only named ranger. A pale golden bow, mana arrows made at the fingertips, up to five at once, stronger ones from the bow, enchanted for speed and weight and able to pierce heavy armour. [F] [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-full-guide-korean-hunters-powers/)
- **Jobs:** sniping, covering fire and thinning crowds. [P]
- **Weaknesses:** badly exposed at close range, which is how Hoshino beats Lim. The Ant King catches his arrow. [P] [F]
- **Best shown by:** Lim Tae-Gyu. Game Rant's class explainer names no ranger at all. [P]

### Support

- **Status:** not a confirmed seventh class. It appears only as a description: healers support the party, rangers support from a distance. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)
- **Yuri Orloff** is the one hunter called "support-type". His barriers pin S-ranks, seal gates and buy time. [F] [W] [P]
- **Norma Selner's** upgrading and foresight are support powers held by a non-combatant. [F] [P]
- **Weakness:** physically vulnerable. Orloff dies the moment a giant gets through his barrier. [F] [W]
- **Game:** *Arise* recasts Gina as a support mage. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-full-guide-korean-hunters-powers/)

How the anime stages speed:

- Fast exchanges are drawn with fighters reduced to streaks or lines, many cuts and few clashes. Reviewers found the weightier, slower-read fights (Jinwoo against Igris, the second half of the Taeshik fight) more satisfying. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-10-review-some-fantastic-fights/)
- One reviewer reads the staging of the sparring as a nod to older fighting anime, with rapid punch flurries and repeated dodge cycles. The comparison is the reviewer's. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-10-review/)
- The Jeju episode adds short fights against the queen's royal guards that give each hunter a turn to show a skill, and trims the manhwa's scenes of onlookers. Reviewers called the ant battle some of the most fluid animation in the series. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-11-review-an-absolute-massacre/)

Readings the notes draw from the above (inference, not sourced fact):

- Classes blur at the top. The strongest Tanker fights as a grappler, the strongest "Mage" as an assassin, and a C-rank Mage as a swordsman. Class is a label assigned at awakening, not a hard limit on style.
- The higher a tanker's rank, the bigger the shield and the more exotic the extra: an energy shield, gigantification, golden armour.
- Trained skill partly offsets rank: a one-armed C-rank mage parries a B-rank assassin for a few exchanges with a borrowed sword and a buff.
- The human villains (Dongsuk, Taeshik, Kim Chul, Dongsoo) share one trait, contempt for weaker hunters, expressed through a different class each time.

### The protagonist: Jinwoo Sung

Jinwoo Sung (Sung Jinwoo, Jin-woo Sung; Mizushino Shun in the Japanese edition). Only his personality, appearance, dagger style and arc are covered here; the shadow army belongs to another chapter.

**Personality**

- Before his reawakening he is timid, insecure and privately ashamed of his weakness and his family's money troubles. Afterwards he is bolder and confident, polite and easygoing in person, indifferent to fame and money, and ruthless and disdainful toward enemies in battle. [P] [MAL](https://myanimelist.net/character/174185/Jin-woo_Sung)
- The turning point is the double dungeon (Cartenon Temple). Being left for dead by his party teaches him that kindness invites exploitation. After it he stops risking himself for noble causes and concentrates on survival and power. He stays ruthless toward anyone who threatens his family yet still acts selflessly at times, for example going to Jeju Island to rescue the remaining Korean S-ranks. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Sung-Jinwoo)
- Anime S1 ep 9: he tells Kang Taeshik he does not care about the murdered convicts, only about his two former party members. One reviewer read this as the first clear sign of his hardening. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-9-hunter-vs-hunter/)
- Anime S1 ep 9: after Kim Sangshik dies he is shaken, because he had wanted the man alive so he could keep blaming him for the double dungeon. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-9)

**Appearance**

- Before: small and slight, long bushy hair over his ears, a boyish face that looks younger than his teenage sister, a plain blue hoodie and jeans, and bandages on arms and legs as a matter of routine. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Sung-Jinwoo)
- After: tall, muscular, sharp-featured, grey eyes, a black fringe down to eye level. His eyes glow purple with power or strong emotion. He favours dark, sleek clothes, from tailored suits to long hooded coats. [F] [W] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Sung-Jinwoo)
- The change is dated to about ch. 18. After clearing his first instant dungeon alone (ch. 16) he trains and puts points into strength, and the System's effects make him far more muscular within a few weeks. [W]
- Anime S1 ep 9: former party members notice the change. Song Chiyul senses a different aura and Lee Joohee sees that he has almost no injuries. He passes it off as daily exercise. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-9)
- After the timeline reset he wears a black glove on his left hand to hide burn scars from the Antares rematch. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Sung-Jinwoo)
- After the reset he fights with black daggers that glow purple, made from his own power (ch. 178). [W]

**Class and dagger style**

- His listed class is Mage, with the job Necromancer and later Shadow Monarch. [P] [W] [MAL](https://myanimelist.net/character/174185/Jin-woo_Sung)
- He was first suspected of being an Assassin because of how he handles a dagger, and is then revealed as a Mage. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-six-hunter-types-explained/)
- Anime S2 ep 10: Goto Ryuji has heard that Korea's newest S-rank is a mage and wants to test him. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-22)
- He wields two daggers in the manhwa (Knight Killer, later the Demon King's Daggers), sometimes bare fists or a longsword. One round-up counts five distinct dagger sets across the story. [S] [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/anime/solo-leveling-all-9-sung-jinwoo-s-weapons-ranked-weakest-strongest)

How his fights progress:

1. Status effects over raw power (anime S1 ep 6). Kasaka's Venom Fang cannot pierce the spider boss's shell, so he drives it into the eye and lets its paralysis and drain effects finish the boss. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-6)
2. Self-heal, intimidation, one stab (anime S1 ep 9). Against an assassin who seems faster he uses an instant self-heal to undo a crippling ankle cut, then the intimidation skill to blind or freeze the opponent before a single stab to the heart. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-9)
3. Pure evasion (anime S2 ep 10). In the Goto spar he fights almost entirely by dodging, takes a nick on the cheek, then catches Goto's hand. [P] [F] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-10-review-some-fantastic-fights/)
4. Fists, telekinesis and daggers together (anime S2 ep 12). The Ant King fight opens as an exchange of punches in which he also uses telekinesis. He then calls his daggers to his hands in a burst of particles, and the blades are shown clashing with the Ant King's claws. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-12-review-straight-hands/) A CBR writer's opinion is that he would have lost without his upgraded Mutilation skill. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-characters-live-up-to-hype/)
5. Bare hands against a target his blades cannot cut (manhwa and novel only so far). He beats Thomas Andre to the edge of death bare-handed. Thomas's Reinforcement is described as tough enough that the Demon King's Daggers cannot cut his skin. [F] [P] [MAL](https://myanimelist.net/character/194373/Thomas_Andre)

- Stealth is used outside combat too: he slips into a hospital room unseen to give Yoo Myunghan the cure. [F] [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Yoo-Myunghan)
- Later kit milestones on the wiki's character page, listed here only for orientation: Shadow Extraction ch. 45, Monarch's Domain ch. 73, Shadow Exchange ch. 88, Shadow Preservation ch. 123, Dragon's Fear ch. 173. [W]

**Relationships**

- Family: father Sung Il-Hwan, who vanished in a dungeon about ten years before the story; mother Park Kyung-Hye, comatose with Eternal Slumber; sister Jinah, whom he supports and in effect raises. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Yoo Jinho: sworn brother, later vice-chairman of his guild. Jinwoo kills Dongsoo Hwang for torturing him. [P] [F] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- The deal with Yoo Jinho is 19 more raids together in return for a building worth 30 billion won and Jinho's silence about his real strength. It is struck about a day after the Hwang Dongsuk killings (ch. 26). [W]
- Cha Haein: she falls for him after he saves her three times and because he is the only hunter who smells pleasant to her. They marry and have a son, Sooho (Suho). [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)

### Korean S-ranks

The Jeju raid party is six Korean and ten Japanese S-ranks. [W] The Korean six are a sword fighter, a fire mage, a beast-shifter, a healer, an archer and a shield tank. Beside them stand two much stronger Ruler vessels (Go Gunhee and Sung Il-Hwan) and one defector (Dongsoo Hwang).

### National Level and international hunters

#### The category

- National Level Hunters ("National-Level Hunter" in Yen Press's edition [O]; also "Special Power Authority" and "Highest Class Hunters"; 国家権力級ハンター on Japanese Wikipedia) are S-ranks whose power outweighs a country's military. The title was created after five S-ranks killed the dragon Kamish (ch. 115). [P] [W] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-hunters-guilds-explained/), [Yen Press](https://yenpress.com/titles/9798400902536-solo-leveling-vol-10-comic)
- The four named ones, in order of Hunter Points (ch. 149): Thomas Andre (USA), Liu Zhigang (China), Christopher Reed (USA) and Siddharth Bachchan (India). [P] [W] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-hunters-guilds-explained/)
- The title is first mentioned in ch. 78, where Goto Ryuji says that clearing an S-rank gate is the minimum requirement. The top four owe their margin over other S-ranks to power the Rulers gave them over the past decade (ch. 143). [W]
- World rankings of S-ranks use a points system run by the US Federal Bureau of Hunters and based on achievements, not raw power. [S] [W]
- Jinwoo never formally joins them, although the wiki's page for him carries the epithet "6th National Level Hunter". [W]
- The fifth Kamish survivor is a healer who is not named in the manhwa or the novel and was never made a vessel. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-national-level-hunters-classes-and-abilities-explained/)
- **Game:** the name Antoine Martinez for that fifth survivor, and his face, were first shown in "Hunter Origin: Thomas Andre's Story" in *Arise*. Screen Rant calls him French; TechWiser lists his nationality as unknown. Beebom describes him as reaching National Level without being a vessel. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-national-s-rank-hunters-list/)
- **Ragnarok (sequel):** the title is reintroduced in the revised timeline (novel ch. 342), by Liu Zhigang after he sees Sooho's power in India. World leaders formally recognise it after Thomas Andre kills the Apostle of Conquest in New York. [W]

## Human-versus-human fights

Fights between hunters are short. They are decided by the rank gap and by who lands the first clean hit, and they nearly always happen where there are no witnesses (inside gates) or under a sparring pretext. Each fight below ends with what it shows, which is the notes' reading and not a sourced fact.

Dungeon & Lizards arc, ch. 18–24, with the fight in ch. 21–24; anime Episodes 5–6. [W] [F]

Episode 22, "We Need a Hero", aired on 8 March 2025 in Japanese and 22 March in English. [P] [W] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Solo_Leveling_episodes)
- Everything after Jeju is manhwa and novel material only. Thomas Andre appears in Episode 21 and Liu Zhigang in Episode 25, the last aired. Reed, Niermann, Orloff, Selner, Jonas and Adam White have no anime appearance. [F] [P]
- Crunchyroll dubbed the anime into several languages, Japanese, Korean, English and Hindi among them. [W]
- **Film:** *Solo Leveling: Beyond the System* was announced at Crunchyroll's Anime Expo 2026 panel, directed by Tao Tajima at A-1 Pictures. Wikipedia gives no release date and does not say what it adapts. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling) The announcement is dated 3 July 2026 and a 2027 release is reported but unconfirmed. [S] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-movie-beyond-the-system-2027/)
- **Season 3:** no premiere date. A D&C Media financial report is said to list it as confirmed and expected in 2027–2028. [S] [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/anime-shows/solo-leveling-season-3-finally-gets-release-window-and-our-worst-fears-have-come-true/) In May 2026 Screen Rant reported that no third season had been officially confirmed. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-season-3-arise-trailer-jinwoo-thomas-andre/)
- **Live action:** a Netflix series was announced in July 2025 with Byeon Woo-seok as Jinwoo and Han So-hee as Cha Haein. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- **English print:** volume 15 of the Yen Press edition was dated 17 March 2026. [P] Comic vol. 11 closes the Thomas Andre fight and came out on 18 February 2025. [O] [P] [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-11/)

### Novel against manhwa

- The four-against-four sparring is a webtoon addition that is not in the novel, and the Park Jongsoo and Hanekawa bout is webtoon-only. [W] [low]
- Yuri Orloff's barrier falls to one sword swing in the manhwa and takes five strikes in the novel. [W]
- Kamish's Wrath is light orange with blood-red edges in the manhwa and ice-white in the novel. [W]
- Park Heejin is a Mage with a staff in the manhwa and a fighter type in the novel. [F]
- Novel and webtoon chapter numbers differ; each profile gives both where the notes have them.

### Manhwa against anime

- **Lizards fight:** Jinwoo kills eight hunters in the webtoon and six in the anime. In the webtoon the crew blasts its way back in. [F] [W]
- **Taeshik fight:** the anime has Jinwoo hear screams where the webtoon has him sense killing intent, makes the Stealth rune a System reward instead of a stone on the body, has Taeshik kill all the convicts, and cuts the surviving convict, Joohee's buffs on Jinwoo and Song's remark about Jinwoo being armed. [F] [W] [S]
- **Added spar (S1 ep 6):** Go Gunhee against Woo Jinchul is anime only. [F] [W]
- **Jinwoo softened (S2 ep 10):** the anime drops a manhwa scene in which he rescues a woman in an alley and leaves a High Orc in the attacker's shadow with orders to kill the man unless he gives himself up. It also drops his thought that he must end the Goto bout before the System orders him to kill. CBR reads both cuts as a loss of his anti-hero edge, which is the reviewer's opinion. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-10-review-crunchyroll/)
- **Goto softened (S2 ep 10):** "kill" becomes "crush" and his fear is cut (see the fights section). [P] [W] [low]
- **Sparring (S2 ep 10):** bouts overlap across the arena in the anime and are cut between in the manhwa. Cha is still on her feet when Jinwoo steps in; in the manhwa she has been forced to the ground. The Park and Hanekawa bout is left out. [P] [W] [low]
- **Jeju (S2 ep 11):** the Ant King's first blow sending Cha spinning through the air into a rock pillar is an anime addition. The episode adds short fights against the queen's royal guards and trims scenes of onlookers. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-11-review/)
- **Ant King fight (S2 ep 12):** the anime adds abilities and camera angles the manhwa lacks. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-12-review-straight-hands/)
- **Min's shadow (S2 ep 13):** in the anime it goes and heals Cha at once without being told to. In the manhwa the extraction fails twice first and Jinwoo wonders whether Min's will is resisting. [P] [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/review/solo-leveling-season-2-arise-from-the-shadow/episode-25/.223056)
- **Skipped (S2 finale):** the ch. 109 meeting with Norma Selner. [P]
- **Liu Zhigang's slash (S2 finale):** judged more impressive in motion than in the manhwa. [P]
- **Recolours:** Lee Joohee's hair is brown in the anime and orange in the manhwa. Sung Jinah's is brown in the manhwa and purple in the anime. Han Song-Yi's eyes are green in the anime. [F]
- **National references thinned in the international anime:** a manhwa caption placing Dongsoo Hwang in Washington, D.C. is gone, Sung Il-Hwan's "I'm Korean" becomes "I'm human", and a manhwa phone call about ants washing ashore in Japan is replaced by a different conversation. CBR later noted that most of the Jeju material was kept. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-changing-jeju-island-manhwa/)

### The Japanese edition

- It gives the characters Japanese names and moves the setting from Korea to Japan. The country that is Japan in the original becomes "DFN", a fictional island nation in East Asia. [P] [Japanese Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/俺だけレベルアップな件)
- Seoul becomes Tokyo and Jeju Island becomes the fictional island of Kanan. DFN is glossed as "De Facto Nation" and its capital is "De Facto City". [P] [Anime Feminist](https://www.animefeminist.com/solo-levelings-adaptation-anti-korean-sentiment/), [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/solo-leveling-is-not-as-successful-in-japan-as-in-the-rest-of-the-world-and-has-even-been-altered-in-many-places,1402933)
- The changes began with the Japanese release of the novel, per an editor's note on the Anime Feminist piece. The wiki says they apply to the webtoon and the anime. [P] [W]
- In the Japanese-market anime, DFN's betrayal at the island is not premeditated, the scenes of Goto being intimidated by Jinwoo and the Hanekawa and Park Jongsoo bout are cut, and Korean and Japanese flags and décor are removed from the association offices. The essay's reading of the motive is its own. [P] [Anime Feminist](https://www.animefeminist.com/solo-levelings-adaptation-anti-korean-sentiment/)
- The Japanese broadcast used the Japanese names in voice and credits while the Crunchyroll stream of episode 1 used Korean names in the credits. The producer, Sota Furuhashi, is reported as saying that Japanese names were adopted for the domestic release and the Korean names kept for audiences abroad. [S] [IMDb news](https://www.imdb.com/news/ni64581053/)

## Touches in DungeonRaze

- `CLASSES` and `MOVES` in `06_game.js`: the class subsections and skill tables are the source's version of a class kit (chain, heavy, dash, tool, special).
- `useTool` and `useSpecial`, and the player states `guard`, `dodge`, `blink`, `heal` and `execute`: the evasion, stealth, intimidation and pull moves described in the profiles and fights.
- `makePlayerFig` in `05_figures.js` (`f.rim`, `f.tips`) and `posePlayer` in `07_render.js`: weapon silhouettes, armour looks and the eye-glow table.
- `EDEF`, `makeEnemyFig` (`f.rim`, `f.eye`) and `threatOf`: any rival hunter built as an enemy, and the human-versus-human fights.
- The effects calls `R.sprite`, `R.ribbon` and `R.ring`: auras, slash arcs and glow.
- The hall in `03b_hub.js` and its portal grades E to S: hunter ranks, the association staff roles and any non-player character.
- The recorded decision that the Healer class is left out, and backlog items 2 and 4 (grades, more enemy variety and a second boss).

Two project rules apply to everything above. Names, characters and signature moves from the series are deliberately not used in the game. The shadow-raising class was dropped by the owner and is not to be reintroduced unless asked.
