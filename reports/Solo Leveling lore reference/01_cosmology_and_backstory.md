# Solo Leveling lore reference, chapter 1: Cosmology and backstory

**Covers:** the setting's creation story, the war between Rulers and Monarchs, the Cup of Reincarnation and the erased timelines, why gates open, mana and magic beasts, dungeons and the gap between dimensions, vessels, the Architect and the System, the final war and the reset, a relative timeline, and how the versions differ.

**Date:** 2026-11-16. Chapter numbers are manhwa chapters unless marked "novel" or "Ragnarok".

**Source markers:** `[O]` official or licensed text (the English Ragnarok novel on Wuxiaworld, whose licence the notes confirmed only from a listing snippet; Netmarble's releases and developer Q&As); `[P]` press, explainer, recap or review site, with Wikipedia counted here; `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki, whose chapter references are its own and unchecked; `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet, which can be a snippet of the wiki and so is not independent support; `[F]` fan site, forum or fan opinion; `[low]` added where the notes mark low confidence. A fact with `[W]` plus `[O]`, `[P]` or `[F]` has independent support; `[W]` alone does not. The wiki material was obtained through the wiki's API after the site refused automated reads, and the project owner chose on 2026-11-16 to keep it, labelled. The notes do not identify any outlet as having copied the wiki, but they warn that single explainer sites contain plain errors, so a lone `[P]` is weaker than two.

## At a glance

1. **A creator god staged the war.** The Absolute Being split primordial light into eight Rulers and primordial darkness into eight Monarchs, set protectors against destroyers for its own amusement, and was killed on its throne by seven of the Rulers. `[P]` `[W]` `[O]` [Wuxiaworld prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
2. **The Shadow Monarch began as the loyal eighth Ruler.** Ashborn was cut down by his own kind, rose through a hidden power over the dead, fought beside the Monarchs as the ninth of them, was betrayed and withdrew. `[P]` `[W]` [QuoteTheAnime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/all-monarchs-and-gods-explanation/)
3. **Each Monarch owns a monster race.** Dragons, the dead, demons, beasts, snow folk, monstrous humanoids, giants, insects and demonic spectres (roster in section 2). `[W]` `[P]` [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-monarchs)
4. **The "good" side opens the gates.** About ten years before the story the Rulers opened gates on purpose, to soak Earth and its people in mana so the planet could survive a war of gods. Millions die of it. `[W]` `[P]` [Animehunch](https://animehunch.com/what-are-gates-dungeons-in-solo-leveling-why-did-they-appear/)
5. **Dungeon monsters are prisoners of war.** Magic beasts are soldiers of the Monarchs' armies whom the Rulers captured and fitted with essence stones, which force them to attack humans. `[P]` `[W]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
6. **Dungeon rules.** A dungeon is a pocket of the chaos world behind a gate, ranked E to S. Clear it within seven days or it breaks. The gate closes about an hour after the boss dies. Red gates trap hunters inside. `[W]` `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-gates-explained/)
7. **Mana is foreign to Earth and never defined.** Awakening is exposure to gate mana plus tolerance. A hunter's measured mana sets their rank and is mostly fixed. `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-origin-of-hunter-abilities-explained/)
8. **Higher beings need vessels.** A Ruler borrows a human who stays in control, at reduced power. A Monarch takes the body over at full power and dies with it. The Rulers had seven vessels, the world's strongest hunters. `[W]` `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/every-solo-leveling-race-explained/)
9. **The System is a vessel-growing machine.** The Architect built it, in the form of a game, to raise one human in steps until he could hold all of Ashborn's power. Neither Rulers nor Monarchs knew of it. `[W]` `[P]` [Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/comics-solo-leveling-ending-manhwa-manga-explained-jinwoo-monarchs)
10. **The Cup of Reincarnation rewinds ten years.** Rulers and Monarchs keep their memories; humans do not. Earth was destroyed and rewound more than once before the gates plan. No reliable count exists. `[W]` `[P]` `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 183](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-183)
11. **The ending erases the premise.** After the last rewind Jinwoo fights the Monarchs alone in the gap between dimensions for 27 years, two on Earth, and returns to a world with no gates, no hunters and no memory of him. `[P]` `[W]` [Epic Dope](https://www.epicdope.com/solo-leveling-chapter-179-release-read-online-spoilers/)
12. **Dates are relative.** First gates about ten years before the story, Kamish about eight, the Jeju gate four. No calendar years and no length for the old war. `[P]` `[W]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-gates-explained/)
13. **Version caveats.** The anime has not reached any of this. Ragnarok, by a different author, adds creator gods above the Absolute Being. The unreleased game Karma is the only official account of the 27 years. `[P]` `[W]` `[O]` [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/solo-leveling-karma-fills-original-story-gap-as-a-roguelite-39-qas-live-from-tgs)

## 1. The creator: the Absolute Being

### What it is and what it made

- Before creation there was only light and darkness. The Absolute Being split the primordial light into the Rulers and the primordial darkness into the Monarchs. `[P]` `[W]` [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-monarchs)
- Each was split into eight pieces. The Ragnarok prologue says this in its own text. `[O]` `[S]` [Wuxiaworld prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- A fuller version, known only from a snippet of the wiki: the Absolute Being found Primordial Light and Primordial Darkness in eternal conflict and created the cosmos to impose order on them. `[S]`
- The Rulers were made to protect the world and the Monarchs to destroy it. `[P]` `[W]` [QuoteTheAnime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/all-monarchs-and-gods-explanation/)
- The Absolute Being is described as covered in silver and gold. `[P]` [QuoteTheAnime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/all-monarchs-and-gods-explanation/)
- It could make life from nothing but was weak in battle. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/every-solo-leveling-race-explained/)
- The Statue of God in the double dungeon is a giant stone copy of the Absolute Being, made by the Architect. `[W]`

### Why it staged the war

- It told its creations that their war kept a balance of power. Its real motive was to watch them fight. `[P]` `[O]` [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-ashborn)
- The balance was built into the Rulers' armies. Their foot soldiers, the Heavenly Soldiers (silver soldiers in the Ragnarok prologue), were born from fruit of the World Tree and shaped by the Absolute Being. The Tree grew more whenever the Monarchs gained an advantage. The wiki cites novel ch. 229, manhwa ch. 168 and Ragnarok novel ch. 231. `[W]`
- It hid the power of death in Ashborn (Osborne in the Webnovel.com English novel), its strongest Ruler. Two reasons are given for this and they differ; see "Where sources conflict". `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ashborn-shadow-monarch-explained/)

### How it died

- After eons of war the Rulers asked it to end the fighting, or to give them the strength to finish the Monarchs. It did not answer. They concluded that the war was its entertainment and would never end while it lived. `[P]` `[W]` [QuoteTheAnime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/all-monarchs-and-gods-explanation/)
- Seven of the eight Rulers rebelled. Ashborn was the eighth and stayed loyal. `[P]` `[W]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-who-are-the-rulers/)
- They attacked together and by surprise. `[P]` [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-villain-explained/)
- It was killed sitting on its throne and did not attack the Rulers. It was found there pierced by countless spears. Ashborn, risen as the Shadow Monarch, found the corpse. `[P]` `[S]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-how-rulers-killed-absolute-being/), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/anime/solo-leveling-who-is-the-god-2474446/)
- In manhwa ch. 163 Ashborn says that no power is infinite and that the creator's power had a limit. This is the only in-story reason given for why a creator could be killed. `[P]` [QuoteTheAnime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/all-monarchs-and-gods-explanation/)

### What followed its death

- The rebels took its place as the new gods, with the stated purpose of maintaining the world without a creator. `[W]` `[F]` [MyAnimeList thread](https://myanimelist.net/forum/message/64159553?goto=topic)
- They took tools that carried fragments of its power, the Cup of Reincarnation among them (section 4). `[P]` `[W]` [QuoteTheAnime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/all-monarchs-and-gods-explanation/)
- The World Tree stopped bearing fruit, so the Rulers' soldiers can no longer be replaced. `[O]` `[W]` [Wuxiaworld prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)

### The Itarim (Ragnarok only)

- The Absolute Being was one of the Itarim, or Outer Gods: supreme beings who each create a universe. They do not age and can be killed only by their own creations or an outside force. Others among them also set their creations fighting for amusement. `[P]` `[W]` `[O]` [Beebom](https://beebom.com/who-are-itarim-outer-gods-solo-leveling-ragnarok)
- When a world loses its owner the Itarim race for its mana, and the first to claim it becomes its master. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- It took the power of all the rebelling Rulers together, plus surprise, to kill this one. `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-itarim-outer-gods-origin-explained/)
- The Itarim learned of the killing from the Architect, Kandiaru. In the reset timeline he escaped Jinwoo's purge, fled beyond the known universe, and bought his way into an Itarim's service by revealing that his home universe had no master. Other Outer Gods followed and the "Outer God War" began. The wiki gives no chapter. `[W]`
- The Itarim's Apostles were wearing the Rulers down, since the Rulers cannot replace losses, until Jinwoo's shadow army joined them. The wiki cites the Ragnarok novel prologue. `[W]`

## 2. The two created races

### Rulers

- Other names: Fragments of Brilliant Light; Messengers of God before the rebellion; Emissaries in the Webnovel.com English novel. `[W]` `[O]` `[F]` `[S]` [Wuxiaworld prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- They are winged, angel-like beings who protect humanity by questionable means. `[P]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- There were eight counting Ashborn and seven after he changed. Those seven later took seven human vessels (section 5). `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-who-are-the-rulers/)

- Together the seven were strong enough that Ashborn admitted he could not have fought them off had they tried to send him back to the "World of Nothingness". `[W]`
- Their goal after the rebellion is to end the war and keep creation running (the notes' inference from the sources, not a quoted statement).

### Length of the war

No source gives a number. The Ragnarok prologue says "since ancient times", "eons" and "an exceedingly long war". `[O]` The wiki says "eons" for the war before the rebellion and "centuries later" for the gap between the Monarchs' defeat and their move on the human world, though its Demons page says "centuries ago" for the first war. `[W]` GameRant says "eons" and that the war was expected to last for eternity. `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-rulers-vs-monarchs-explained/) One fan write-up gives figures; see "Where sources conflict".

### Ashborn

- Title: Greatest Fragment of Brilliant Light. He was the strongest Ruler. `[P]` [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/how-strong-is-ashborn-solo-leveling-powers-abilities-explained)
- He tore off his six wings when he became a Monarch. The wiki pages read do not mention this. `[P]` [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/how-strong-is-ashborn-solo-leveling-powers-abilities-explained)
- He alone could hold both Primordial Light and Primordial Darkness. `[S]`
- He stayed blindly loyal to the Absolute Being and kept fighting on its behalf after learning the truth. `[W]`
- He joined the Monarchs because he saw they would lose once Legia was taken, and wanted to steady the balance and keep the war going as his maker had wished. `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ashborn-shadow-monarch-explained/)
- Bellion, his lieutenant, was the first shadow he ever made and was born from World Tree fruit. Bellion survived the ambush with the rest of the original army and reached Earth after the Monarchs War (ch. 166 to 168). `[W]`
- He followed the Monarchs to the human world "out of his own desire to find his place in the universe", having never felt he belonged in the chaos world. `[W]`
- Commentators read his second alliance with Antares as a way to watch the Monarchs from inside. This is a reading, not text. `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ashborn-shadow-monarch-explained/)
- In the novel he wears Jinwoo's own face when they meet. Asked why he chose Jinwoo, he answers only that he wanted to. `[S]` [Webnovel ch. 220](https://www.webnovel.com/book/solo-leveling%28only-i-level-up%29_12507348206677105/chapter-220_36852240060826591)
- His end: after the Monarchs kill Jinwoo, Ashborn revives him, hands over the rest of his power and enters eternal sleep, returning to the "World of Eternal Slumber" (ch. 164). Ch. 162 has his line about being the record of Jinwoo's struggle. `[P]` `[W]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)

### How it was used before the story

- The Monarchs set out to conquer the human world in order to rebuild their armies. The Rulers tried to stop them to save the human race. The human world was destroyed whichever side won. `[W]` `[P]` [Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/comics-solo-leveling-ending-manhwa-manga-explained-jinwoo-monarchs)
- The first use came when the Rulers' first attempt to stop the Monarchs failed. They then rewound repeatedly, and humanity met the same fate each time. `[W]` `[P]` `[F]` [Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/comics-solo-leveling-ending-manhwa-manga-explained-jinwoo-monarchs)
- The number of uses is not established. One source says seven before the gates timeline; see "Where sources conflict". Every other source says "multiple" or "repeatedly" and gives no figure. `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-who-are-the-rulers/)
- The gates plan was the change of method, and also a way off the Cup: the Rulers saw it wearing out, expected the gates to saturate the world with mana within a few years, and reckoned that a win would mean never needing it again. `[W]`
- After the last rewind before the story the Rulers opened the gates so that mana would seep in slowly and some humans could survive the next war, which they expected in ten years. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- The notes infer two things from this, neither stated by a source. Because both sides remember every loop, each rewind helped the Monarchs as much as the Rulers, which is why repeating it changed nothing. And the whole age of hunters is one Cup interval: the gates open ten years before the story, which is exactly the Cup's reach.
- Baran is the one Monarch the final rewind does not restore. The notes' reading is that Ashborn killed him long before the ten-year window.
- As the world repeated, the Architect was able to watch humans closely, and built the System out of what humans enjoyed playing and were good at (novel, about ch. 218 to 220). `[S]` [Webnovel ch. 220](https://www.webnovel.com/book/solo-leveling%28only-i-level-up%29_12507348206677105/chapter-220_36852240060826591)

### What an earlier timeline looked like

- The only description: the Demon Castle is a burning, ruined Seoul, and Ashborn later reveals that this look is based on Seoul in an alternate timeline where the war devastated the human world and wiped out mankind. The wiki says the dungeon is modelled on that Seoul, not that it is that place, and gives no chapter. `[W]`
- A forum poster goes further and says the Demon's Castle was Earth in a past timeline. This is fan interpretation. `[F]` [MyAnimeList thread](https://myanimelist.net/forum/message/64159553?goto=topic)
- A side effect of the final reset: the Demon Castle became unstable and its surviving demons were given sanctuary in the Shadow Monarch's own realm, the World of Eternal Slumber. No chapter given. `[W]`

### Other tools

- No other tool is named in any source. "Instruments" is not a term the sources use. The wiki's phrase is tools containing fragments of his power; a fan write-up calls the Cup a "Power Fragment". `[W]` `[P]` `[F]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)

## 5. Why gates open: the Rulers' plan and the Monarchs' plan

### The Rulers' plan

- Gates to another dimension began to appear around the world about ten years before the story. This is the framing of anime episode 1. `[W]`
- The Rulers opened them on purpose. It was a collective decision and a last resort after rewinding failed. `[P]` `[W]` [Animehunch](https://animehunch.com/what-are-gates-dungeons-in-solo-leveling-why-did-they-appear/)
- The aim was to fill the human world with mana by exposing mankind to magic beasts. This would reinforce the earth itself so it could withstand the war, and keep humanity from being wiped out. `[W]` `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-gates-explained/)
- At the same time they gave their powers to seven human vessels, who became the strongest hunters in the world. `[W]` `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- Both Rulers and Monarchs can create gates and travel between worlds with them. The Rulers spawned the Tokyo S-rank gate on purpose. `[W]`
- The Rulers are indirectly responsible for the millions of deaths the gates caused. `[P]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Even so, humans stayed too weak. This is how fans read Ashborn's account in ch. 163. `[F]` [MyAnimeList thread](https://myanimelist.net/forum/message/64159553?goto=topic)
- Releasing beasts captured from the Monarchs' armies on Earth gave the Monarchs a reason to come. `[P]` [Animehunch](https://animehunch.com/what-are-gates-dungeons-in-solo-leveling-why-did-they-appear/)
- Neither side knew about the System. The Architect built it for Ashborn alone, so the Rulers could not copy it to grow stronger vessels, and the Monarchs did not see Ashborn's change of sides coming until they met Jinwoo. `[W]`

### Why Earth

- The Monarchs moved first. Centuries after their defeat they went looking for a new world in which to rebuild their armies, and Earth was that world. The Rulers discovered this and sent armies after them. `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-rulers-vs-monarchs-explained/)
- The Monarchs' surviving soldiers had gone into hiding as refugees, and their aim in coming to Earth is described as survival and a foothold. `[W]`
- No source says why this world and not another.
- Ragnarok only: Earth is the rear of the new war and the place with the weakest magical power, which is why the invaders strike there. The prize is the ownerless universe and its mana; Beebom calls Earth a source of unlimited mana that no god has claimed. The narration calls the Rulers the main culprits of the earlier invasion, and the Rulers describe their earlier aim as nurturing Earth to protect it. `[O]` `[P]` [Wuxiaworld prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-villain-explained/)

### Vessels

- The Rulers lack organic form and have a mana-based biology, so they cannot easily cross into the human world without human vessels. `[W]`
- A second reason: their mana pressure would crush ordinary humans, so they could not fight the Monarchs in the human world themselves. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- The Rulers' true bodies reach Earth only at the end, when Jinwoo's fight with Antares warps space far enough for them to enter in their raw forms (ch. 177). `[W]`
- Monarchs cannot enter the human world in their true form either, so the first to arrive possess human bodies. `[P]` [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-monarchs)
- Antares is the only Monarch who never used a vessel. No human could hold him, so he waited until conditions let him cross in his own form. `[W]`

## 6. Mana, essence stones and magic beasts

### Mana

- Mana is not native to the human world. It originates from gods such as the Absolute Being and from their creations, and it arrived through the gates. `[W]`
- No source defines it further. GameRant says the lore is incomplete and calls an origin in the Absolute Being "likely", not confirmed. CBR leaves the source and workings unexplained. `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-origin-of-hunter-abilities-explained/)
- It flows out of defeated monsters into the Earth, and hunters draw on it after awakening. `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-origin-of-hunter-abilities-explained/)
- A hunter's output can be measured and sets their rank. The amount is mostly fixed. An S-rank's is too high to measure. `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-origin-of-hunter-abilities-explained/)
- Rulers and Monarchs seem not to need mana for their authority skills. `[W]`
- The same mana makes some people ill. `[W]` `[P]` [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-origin-of-hunter-abilities-explained/)

## 8. Vessels, the Architect and the System

### How a vessel works

- First appearance: novel ch. 2, manhwa ch. 6. `[W]`

### Manhwa ch. 178: one day of peace, then the war in the gap

In order, from two recaps read in full: `[P]` [Epic Dope ch. 178 recap](https://www.epicdope.com/solo-leveling-chapter-179-release-read-online-spoilers/)

### The second war (the 27 years)

- Jinwoo killed the other Monarchs first and saved Antares for last. Yogumunt was the last to fall before Antares. `[W]` `[P]` [QuoteTheAnime](https://quotetheanime.com/solo-leveling/all-monarchs-and-gods-explanation/)
- Antares knew, did nothing to help the others, and waited. When Jinwoo reached his palace Antares took his true form at once. Jinwoo still killed him alone and came away with a badly burned left hand (ch. 178; burn shown in ch. 181). `[W]`

- It was a war of extermination. Jinwoo hunted every dragon until the species was extinct but for one egg. The giants were extinct after it. Most or all of the Monarchs' servants ended as his shadows. `[W]`
- Kamish's Wrath wore out over "nearly three decades" and one blade broke on Antares's skin, so Jinwoo had daggers made from Antares's fangs (Ragnarok novel ch. 244). `[W]`
- Because the Monarchs never reach Earth, humanity never meets the gates or the monsters. `[P]` `[W]` [Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/comics-solo-leveling-ending-manhwa-manga-explained-jinwoo-monarchs)

## Touches in DungeonRaze

- **Portal grades E to S in the base hall.** The source ranks gates, dungeons and magic beasts E to S, sets a hunter's rank by measured mana, and makes red gates B-rank at lowest (sections 6 and 7).
- **The hall's portal frames and the zone swap.** A gate is a join to a separate place with its own terrain, not a cave under the gate (section 7).
- **The cave setting.** Dungeon layouts run from multi-level caverns to open landscapes (section 7).
- **The boss and the boss chamber.** A dungeon ends with its boss; the gate closes about an hour after the boss dies, and an uncleared dungeon breaks after seven days (section 7).
- **Enemy `threat`, name tags and `PACKS`.** Beasts are ranked, some are tied to a Monarch and far stronger than their neighbours, and each monster race belongs to one Monarch (sections 2 and 6).
- **Kill rewards and the loot backlog.** Essence stones, mana crystals and rune stones are what the source's dungeons yield (section 6).
- **Classes, and where levels will plug in.** An ordinary hunter does not choose their ability and their mana is mostly fixed; only the System's one Player levels up (sections 5, 6 and 8).
- **"Return to base" and the extraction idea.** Red gates are the source's case of a dungeon that cannot be left until it is cleared (section 7).

Two project rules apply to everything above. Names, characters and signature moves from the series are deliberately not used in the game. The shadow-raising class was dropped by the owner and is not to be reintroduced unless asked.
