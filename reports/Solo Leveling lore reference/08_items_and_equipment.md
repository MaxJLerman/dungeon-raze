# 08. Items, equipment and loot

- **Covers:** the items, weapons, armour, accessories, consumables, keys, rune stones, currencies and materials of Solo Leveling (novel, manhwa, anime), how grades and drops work, the artifact market and what can be handed to other people. Items of the sequel Ragnarok are kept in their own section. Items that exist only in the games are left out.
- **Date:** 2026-11-16.
- **Source markers:** `[O]` official or licensed text (publisher word lists, licensed translations, the official anime site, staff interviews). `[P]` press, explainer, recap or review site. `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki; a chapter or episode reference that carries only `[W]` is the wiki's own and unchecked. `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet. `[F]` fan site or fan opinion. `[low]` is added where the research marked a fact low confidence. The wiki material was obtained through the wiki's API after the site refused automated reads, and the project owner chose on 2026-11-16 to keep it, labelled. A fact with `[W]` and `[P]` has a second publication behind it, but explainer sites repeat the wiki's numbers without naming a source, so this is not a check against the books. An `[O]` fact drawn from a Google Books word list shows only that a term is printed in that volume.

Names: the licensed English name is given first wherever one was found (Yen Press novel and print webtoon, and the licensed translation of Ragnarok). Where none was found, the wiki's name is used and the name table under Differences between versions says so. Stamina and Vitality are one stat under two translations. So are Sense and Perception.

## At a glance

1. **Grades are letters on an item card.** The licensed field name is "Acquisition Difficulty". Values seen are None (junk), E, C, B, A and S, plus "??" and "???" for things the System does not measure, which covers one-of-a-kind weapons and quest items alike. No D-grade item is named anywhere.
2. **Attack climbs steeply with grade.** +10 for an ordinary hunter's sword, +25 at C, +75 at B, +110 at A, +220 and +350 at S, +1500 at "??". Stat bonuses appear at A and named passive effects at S.
3. **Grade belongs to the item, not to the boss.** An A-Rank boss dropped an S helmet and S-Rank bosses dropped A-grade items beside S-grade ones.
4. **Jinwoo's weapon line has six steps and one purchase.** A found sword, a C boss dagger, a B dagger bought for 2,800,000 Gold, an A boss dagger, an S boss pair, then a "??" pair received as a gift. Loot that does not suit him goes to his shadows.
5. **Armour cuts physical damage by a percentage and accessories add flat stats.** 3% and 7% on B pieces, 15% on an S helmet, 19% for a four-piece A set. Jewellery gives +20 to two stats each, and a three-piece set adds +5 then +10 to all stats. Equipped armour is invisible.
6. **Healing has three separate limits.** A healing potion fails below some health floor, the S-grade Elixir of Life cures illness but not injury, and the full recovery is tied to finishing a quest. Basic potions are all E.
7. **Two currencies never meet directly.** Gold is earned and spent only in the System's Store. The hunter economy runs on won, from essence stones cut out of every monster, mana crystals mined from dungeon walls, rune stones and artifacts. Store-bought gear can be sold for won: a 2.8 million Gold dagger was appraised at about 100 million won.
8. **Drops follow monster tier.** Ordinary monsters leave junk to sell and now and then a utility item. Elites leave lower-grade armour. Bosses leave a named signature item with extras such as an accessory, an ingredient, a key or a skill stone. The only drop-rate wording in any source is "rare drop".
9. **Keys are tickets and boxes deliver them.** An E key opens the first System dungeon. The Demon's Castle needs an S key, an A door key from its gatekeeper and a permit per floor. Quest boxes come in two kinds: Blessed for what the Player wants, Cursed for what the Player needs.
10. **Rune stones teach one skill when broken** and are the one skill item ordinary hunters share with the Player. They can come from a kill or from a quest.
11. **Ownership is enforced by the System.** Jinwoo cannot hand a potion to anyone but can pour it into their mouth. Coats, weapons and a magic sphere change hands freely. An enemy who grabs his weapon loses it at once.
12. **Other hunters have almost no named gear.** They are described by class and weapon type. Named, statted equipment is a mark of the System or of the strongest monsters.
13. **The names in common use are mostly not the licensed ones.** The wiki says Demon King's Daggers, Baran, Baruka and Orb of Avarice where the Yen Press books print Demon Monarch's Dagger, Balan, Barca and Sphere of Avarice.

## Grades

### The grade ladder

- Each System item has a card with a grade field and a type field. The licensed English editions call the grade field "Acquisition Difficulty", with a letter as its value. The wiki calls the same field "Rarity". `[O]` [Google Books, comic vol. 6](https://books.google.com/books?id=GD2dEAAAQBAJ)
- The side-story volume of the print webtoon uses "ATK" and "Category" on item cards. `[O]` [Google Books, comic vol. 15](https://books.google.com/books?id=lJ2GEQAAQBAJ)
- Values seen on named items: None, E, C, B, A, S, "??" and "???". No D-grade item appears anywhere in the wiki's weapon, equipment and inventory tables. `[W]`
- Every System item has a formal name. Weapons looted from magic beasts are named after the monster they came from. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/weird-solo-leveling-system-facts-trivia/)
- Item types seen: Sword, Longsword, Dagger, Armor, Necklace, Accessory, Magic Item, Elixir, Consumable, Key, Junk, Material, Ingredient, Rune, Container and Heart. Potions and the Elixir of Life (wiki: Holy Water of Life) are typed Consumable. Only Kasaka's Venom Gland is typed Elixir. `[W]`

### Grade and price

- An ordinary hunter's sword with Attack +10 cost its owner ₩3,000,000. `[P]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)
- Knight Killer cost 2,800,000 Gold in the System's Store. It is the only weapon with a price in the wiki's table. `[W][P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-sung-jin-woo-strongest-weapons/)
- Other Gold prices in the wiki's tables: Mythical Restoration Potion 10,000, Cerberus' Molar 150,000, a Lycan fang 20, Warm Fur Coat 10, Warm Gloves 10. The tables do not say whether a price is for buying or selling, except that the Lycan fang's text says it can be sold in the Store. The four Truth Seeker's pieces show "Price: ?". `[W]`
- At the Korean Hunters Auction, evaluators put one System weapon at about 30 million won and Knight Killer at about 100 million won (manhwa chapter 79). These were appraisals, not sales. The 30-million weapon is not named. `[W][low]`
- The anime's version of the scene (episode 19) has daggers at the artifact store costing millions and Jinwoo being told that a dagger he bought in the System shop for about 2 million could sell there for upwards of 100 million. The review that reports this rounds the purchase price and does not name the dagger. `[P]` [Anime Rants](https://animerants.net/2025/02/18/solo-leveling-episode-19-review-i-have-a-bad-feeling-about-this/)
- 2,800,000 Gold against a 100 million won appraisal works out at about 36 won per Gold. This is one data point and the two currencies cannot be exchanged for each other (see Currencies and materials).
- An early English web translation of the novel has the auction's smallest price step in the hundreds of millions of won and the useful items costing billions, with Jinwoo holding about 1.7 billion won and expecting to have to sell the sphere. `[S][low]` [webnovel.com listing](https://www.webnovel.com/book/solo-leveling-(only-i-level-up)_21292432006103705/chapter-80_57161610858395451)
- System gear beats human craft. The Auction's custodian measured the Sphere of Avarice at a 100% magic amplification rate, where the best-known artifact craftsmen reach 50%. `[W][P]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)

### Notes on single weapons

**Before the System and the first dungeon**
- Before the System, Jinwoo carried a cheap, weak knife that broke on a goblin (anime episode 1). `[W]`
- In the first instance dungeon the manhwa has him unarmed and the anime gives him a dagger. The anime's item list for that dungeon has two Worn Daggers and a Traveler's cloak. Anime coverage says the daily quest's loot box gave him the dungeon key and, later, a dagger. `[W][P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-quest-log-training-stats-explained/)

**Kim Sangshik's Sword**
- After Kasaka shattered it, Jinwoo threw the broken blade through the eye of a D-rank Stone Golem during a dungeon break, which let the hunters there finish the golem. `[W]`
- It is two-handed in the source art and one-handed in the anime. `[F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Kim-Sangshik-s-Sword)

**Kasaka's Venom Fang**
- A compact black blade with a white edge and a bone hilt. `[S][F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Kasaka-s-Venom-Fang)
- The bleed can kill. One page words the 1% as 1% of "stamina", that translation's word for health. `[P]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)
- Its effects do not work on everything. Cerberus is described as completely immune and Barca as resisting the poison. The anime credits its paralysis and drain with finishing the spider boss Buryura after a stab through the eye (episode 6). `[W]`
- It was the weapon Jinwoo used against the B-rank hunter Kang Taeshik. `[P]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)
- One explainer says it is especially effective against humans. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-artifacts-explained/)

**Knight Killer**
- He bought it in the Penalty Zone because the Store is switched off inside the Job Change dungeon itself. `[W]`
- It is described as his first purchase from the Store and as the only dagger he ever bought. In anime episode 12 he calls it a little pricey. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-underutilizing-shop-system/); `[F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Knight-Killer)
- A black and orange blade with a grey grip. `[F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Knight-Killer)
- The Job Change dungeon's enemies are Knights, Magicians, Archers and Assassins. `[W]`

**Barca's Dagger**
- Barca fought with more than one dagger. One was destroyed when he blocked a dagger that Jinwoo pulled back by telekinesis. The shadow Iron killed him. `[W]`
- A bright silver curved blade with a white glow. `[F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Baruka-s-Dagger)
- One page calls the blade a tanto and says its magic lightens the wielder, which is why it adds Agility. `[P][low]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)

**Demon Monarch's Dagger**
- An explainer written from the anime's second season says "Two as One" applies when he wields both daggers. The wiki's table does not say whether both must be equipped. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-new-demon-king-daggers-explained/)
- Limits: the pair only scratched the face of the Statue of God and could not cut Thomas Andre's skin. `[W]` for the statue; `[P]` for both, [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)
- He first used the dagger-only skill Mutilation with this pair, against the Ant King. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-new-demon-king-daggers-explained/)
- The edges glowed blood-red under Balan and light purple under Jinwoo. `[P]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)

**Demon King's Longsword**
- The wiki's table does not say what triggers the storm. One explainer says it comes when the sword is swung. `[P]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)
- Igris received it when he was set to test Haein Cha (wiki: Cha Hae-In) for entry to the guild. His infobox lists it as his weapon, citing novel chapter 173. `[W]`
- Haein Cha wielding it is supported for the sequel only, where she kills an enemy with it. `[W]` A fan site calls it her main weapon. `[F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Demon-King-s-Longsword)
- Colour: the blade was light blue under Balan. Under Jinwoo the guard and hilt turned black, and the blade turned light purple after he received the Black Heart. `[P][F]` [OtakusNotes](https://otakusnotes.com/sung-jin-woos-all-weapons/)

**Kamish's Wrath**
- Origin: Thomas Andre got Kamish's largest and sharpest fang when the dragon bit his arm. The American government let him keep it and he had it made into a weapon. The fang was too short for a longsword, so it became two daggers. `[W]`
- They lay for eight years in the basement of the Scavenger Guild headquarters, because Andre is not a dagger user and would not let his guild members use them. He gave them to Jinwoo over a meal, as thanks for sparing him and his guild members. `[W]`
- Andre said his Reinforcement skill would have failed against them. Jinwoo killed lesser Monarchs with them (ch. 159). They could not scratch Antares, whom Kamish once served (ch. 176), and one blade broke on Antares' skin. `[W]`
- The wiki's weapons table words Mana Sensitivity as power that follows the user's Strength stat. The item's own page says it rises with the user's abilities. `[W]`
- In the novel Jinwoo makes them feather-light. `[W]`
- Look: in the webtoon they are large and light orange with blood-red edges, one thicker than the other, one with a black guard and one with a gold guard. The novel gives them an icy white tone. `[W]`
- The replacement pair after the reset is black with a purple glow. `[W]`

**Antares' Fangs**
- The wiki calls them the strongest magical weapons in the world and Kamish's Wrath the second strongest. It says Jinwoo had them made because nearly three decades of war had worn Kamish's Wrath down. `[W]`

**Sphere of Avarice**
- A red crystal sphere said to be formed from a high-ranking demon's petrified blood. It dropped with the Demon Monarch's Earring and the World Tree's Fragment. `[W]`
- After the final war dwarves mounted it as the head of a dragon-styled staff for Tusk, at Beru's suggestion. `[W]`
- Jinwoo took it to the Auction for valuation and kept it (ch. 79 to 80). `[W][low]`

**A passive beside the weapons**
- The skill Advanced Dagger Techniques raises the damage Jinwoo deals with daggers by 33% (ch. 36). `[W]`

## Currencies and materials

Two economies run side by side. System Gold is earned and spent only inside the System. The hunter economy runs on won and is built on essence stones, mana crystals, rune stones and artifacts.

### System Gold and the Store

- Gold comes from killing monsters, finishing quests and selling items to the Store. `[W][P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/weird-solo-leveling-system-facts-trivia/)
- Gold has no physical counterpart and can be spent only in the System's Store. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/weird-solo-leveling-system-facts-trivia/)
- The Store is scaled to the Player's current level. It stocks consumables, equipment and weapons (webtoon ch. 42). The wiki also names it the Shop, the System Shop and the Player Shop. `[W][F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/System)
- The Store buys as well as sells. The first thing Jinwoo sells is a wolf fang, for 20 Gold (anime episode 4). `[W][P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-quest-log-training-stats-explained/)
- A boss fight can pay Gold directly: the Igris fight paid a large amount on top of its items (ch. 40). `[W]`
- Gold becomes won only by way of goods. Items bought in the Store are accepted by the real artifact trade and can be sold there for ordinary money. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-underutilizing-shop-system/)
- The Store can be closed by a dungeon's rules (the Job Change dungeon) and opened from the Penalty Zone. `[W][P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-jinwoo-underutilizing-shop-system/)

### Essence stones

- The licensed print webtoon prints "Essence Stone". English coverage also says "Magical Core". `[O]` [Google Books, comic vol. 10](https://books.google.com/books?id=1GAVEQAAQBAJ); `[P]` [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-what-is-an-essence-stone-magical-core-how-worthy-is-it/)
- They are embedded in the bodies of magic beasts. Only hunters can get them, by killing the beast and cutting the stone out. Guilds employ Collecting Teams to recover the corpses. `[W][P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- They are the main income of hunters, and value depends on rank and quality. `[S][F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Essence-Stones)
- Low-rank stones fetch little because they are the commonest and S-Rank stones are "valued in the millions", with no currency named. No source gives a price per grade. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Uses: making weapons and gear, and energy. The government studies them as a clean energy source, and the American hunter bureau ran its headquarters on one. `[W][P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Colour by rank: E and D white and the most common, A red, S purple. Lighter stones are commoner and darker ones worth more. All are shaped like a triangular diamond. No colour is given for B or C. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Rare pure black stones come from monsters of extraordinary strength such as the Ant King and Kamish. Larger beasts generally yield larger stones. Kamish's stone powered the bureau's headquarters for close to ten years. `[F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Essence-Stones)
- Origin: the Rulers made the stones and embedded them in captured magic beasts to fill the human world with mana. CBR credits this to the manhwa. `[P][F]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Jinwoo's shadows can do the harvesting: they kill the beasts and collect the dungeon's essence stones for him (ch. 56, anime episode 15). `[W]`

### Mana crystals and glow stones

- Mana crystals are blue minerals fixed to the walls of some dungeons, giving off a bluish light. They are found in the dungeon, not in monsters. They hold less mana than essence stones and are worth considerably less, though they still sell "for solid prices" as a second income. `[P]` [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-stones-ranked-by-worth/)
- They are mined with equipment the party brings in, often by specialist members. `[P]` [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-stones-ranked-by-worth/) Electricity does not work in dungeons, so the mining is by hand with pickaxes, and guilds usually send lower-ranked hunters as mining teams. `[S][F]` [Daddy Jim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item)
- One won figure: a chamber in a C-rank dungeon held about 1 billion won of crystals (anime episode 6). The raid contract shared out only the essence stones and said nothing about other goods, which Yoo Jinho questioned on the spot. The party had to leave to fetch mining equipment before it could take them. `[W]`
- They are also burned in bulk: one S-Rank's barrier, built to seal an S-Rank gate, needed over 100,000 tons. `[W]`
- The anime adds a scene of the shadow Iron mining (episode 15). `[W]`
- Glow stones are a further kind: common, found in quantity in nearly every dungeon and used to light it, and usually missing where insect-type beasts live because those beasts dislike light. `[P]` [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-stones-ranked-by-worth/)
- One article ranks the four kinds of stone by worth as its own opinion: essence stones, rune stones, mana crystals, glow stones. `[P]` [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-stones-ranked-by-worth/)
- The wiki's summaries also say "Mana Stones", "magic gems" and "magic stones" without defining them. `[W]`

### Tax, treasure and gate rights

- Sales are taxed, and guild members pay lower rates than independents. The example given is a 40% rate for an independent hunter selling C-Rank essence stones. `[S]`
- Dungeons hold treasure such as artifacts and mana crystals. Lower-ranked dungeons have weaker monsters and less valuable loot. `[W]`
- Gate rights are traded for won. Yoo Jinho paid 150 million won for the rights to a batch of C-rank gates. Jinwoo resold three of them to the White Tiger Guild for 600 million won in total after asking 300 million each. Comparable gates on the market were 70 million won cheaper than what the guild paid (ch. 35 and 37, anime episode 10). `[W]`

### Crafting and crafters

- Human craftsmen make magic gear and are outclassed by System items (see Grade and price). None is named. `[W]`
- Dwarves do the notable crafting: they mount the Sphere as a staff after the final war and restore Kamish's Wrath in the sequel. The Yen Press novel prints "dwarves" in its last volume. `[W][O]` [Google Books, novel vol. 8](https://books.google.com/books?id=_BG6EAAAQBAJ)
- The only thing Jinwoo makes himself in the sources read is the Elixir of Life, from a System recipe. `[W]`

### The inventory

- The wiki lists it as "Unlimited Inventory" among the System's abilities. `[W]`
- It holds ordinary objects too. In the novel Jinwoo pulls a pen out of it to sign a notepad, and in the anime an umbrella he gives his sister was a System reward. `[W]`
- It also stores monster corpses and quest items. `[P]` [Anime Tiger](https://animetiger.com/solo-leveling-system-explained/)
- Ordinary hunters have a small counterpart. Mages' Spatial Magic lets most of them summon two or three low-ranking items, and Jinwoo's Red Gate teammates took his inventory for that magic (anime episode 13). `[W]`

## Other hunters' gear and the market

### Other hunters

- The story names almost no equipment for other hunters. The wiki's infoboxes give weapon types and never a name or a stat: Yoo Jinho a broadsword and round shield, Hwang Dongsuk a broadsword and heater shield, Kang Taeshik a dagger, Song Chi-Yul a sword, Kim Chul a battle axe or hammer with a tower shield, Min Byung-Gyu a mace and spell book, Goto Ryuji a katana, Sung Il-Hwan daggers. `[W]`
- A guide to Korea's S-Rank hunters names no piece of equipment for any of them. Most are described by powers alone. Haein Cha is given "a sword" and Lim Tae-Gyu a bow and arrows. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-all-10-s-rank-korean-hunters/)
- Weapon types by hunter class on the wiki: Assassins use daggers. Fighters use swords, axes, knives, claws and gauntlets. Mages use staffs, rings, magic lanterns and spell books. `[W]`
- Yoo Jinho is the hunter defined by bought gear. He wears very fancy, expensive and useless armour, turned up to one raid in a heavy suit from Italy that Jinwoo told him to take off because he could not move in it, and makes up for his low rank with an arsenal of high-grade equipment that boosts his stats. No piece is named or priced. `[W]`
- Other hunters treat that gear as loot and as an explanation. The raid team that meant to kill him eyed it as something to collect afterwards, and the staff who looked into the raid assumed he had survived because of it (anime episodes 6 and 7). `[W]`
- Haein Cha's own sword is never named. Beru crushed it in his bare hand. The anime's episode 18 item list gives a pickaxe as her weapon when she moves against the orc boss Kargalgan. `[W]` For her guild test she borrowed a training sword because her own had been left behind. `[W][low]`
- S-Rank armour is mentioned once and not named: Baek Yoonho and Ma Dongwook wore battle armour at Jeju and the Ant Queen's poison still injured them badly (ch. 98). `[W]`
- Thomas Andre's defence is a skill, Reinforcement. His page lists no weapon. He owned Kamish's Wrath for eight years without using it. `[W]`
- The United States government holds Kamish's Remains as state assets and uses them as bargaining chips. The bureau's headquarters was built over the corpse, which is kept in the basement behind mana-reinforced glass with the rune stone. `[W]`; `[O]` the term, [Google Books, comic vol. 10](https://books.google.com/books?id=1GAVEQAAQBAJ)

### Shadows and allies

- Shadows bring their own gear. Iron kept the armour and weapons he had in life, and when he rose to Elite Knight grade his axe grew larger and double-bladed. A shadow's equipment improves with its grade, not through purchases. `[W]`
- Jinwoo's surplus loot goes to shadows. The Longsword went to Igris (ch. 113). The Sphere went first to a shadow mage for a test fireball, a manhwa scene the anime cut, and then to Tusk. `[W]`
- With the Sphere, Tusk's fire attack cancelled Balan's breath and blew a hole through Hallasan. He carried it in his hand until it was made into a staff. `[W]`
- Every transfer on record is a hand-down of Jinwoo's own loot or a potion given by hand. No source shows him buying or commissioning gear for a shadow or an ally.

### The artifact market

- Artifacts are high-level gear that even experienced craftsmen cannot produce: unique items with special effects, some forming sets. They are extremely rare, are sold at auction, online included, and are very expensive, though an S-Rank hunter such as Hwang Dongsoo could afford one. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-artifacts-explained/)
- An anime page has Hwang Dongsoo planning to get artifacts for a rematch with Sung Il-Hwan while Jinwoo looks artifacts up online (episode 16). `[W]`
- The Korean Hunters Auction appraises and sells hunter goods. The Hunters Guild serves as its protector (ch. 79). `[W]`
- A chapter page describes two tiers: an online marketplace for ordinary equipment and a private collection of rare artifacts kept behind magically reinforced glass, which Jinwoo cracked with mana alone. `[W][low]`
- Before his re-ranking Jinwoo held back from selling System items, because a registered E-Rank selling a strong artifact would draw suspicion (ch. 65). `[W][low]`
- Anime episode 19: Jinwoo looks for heat-resistant equipment. A seller shows a cloak and a ring and a second offers to find him a weapon. He judges all of it too dear while noting that the items in his own Store cost even more. `[W]`
- The manhwa's chapter 79 page words the lesson differently: the appraisals showed him he could buy gear in the Store with Gold and resell it for won, so he no longer needed to sell the Sphere. `[W][low]`
- All price figures found are under Grade and price. No hammer price, auction fee or list of auctioned artifacts was found.

### How the System words its notices

- Only Jinwoo can see the System's windows. He checks whether his sister can see one floating in front of him and she cannot. `[W]`
- Lines quoted from the anime's first season: "The rewards have been delivered.", "Daily Quest: Strength Training has arrived.", "Failure to complete the daily quest will result in an appropriate penalty." and "You have acquired the qualifications to be a Player. Will you accept?". No item-drop line is quoted. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-quest-log-training-stats-explained/)
- The wiki only paraphrases: a message says the enemy is defeated and Jinwoo then looks over his rewards (anime episode 11). Unread notifications wait until he opens them (anime episode 3). The first dungeon key arrives as a "special reward". `[W]`
- In the anime's first instance dungeon the System told him of the kill, of the fang and that items can be sold. `[P]` [China Attila recap](https://www.chinaattila.com/solo-leveling-episode-4-recap-have-i-gotten-stronger/)
- Interface words printed in the licensed print webtoon: "Notification", "Accept", "Reject", "experience points acquired", "Item", "Acquisition Difficulty". These are vocabulary, not whole sentences. `[O]` [Google Books, comic vol. 12](https://books.google.com/books?id=IBEyEQAAQBAJ)
- Sequel only (Ragnarok, licensed translation): the item notice is a bracketed line that starts with "Item:" and gives the name in quotation marks. Three forms were seen. The item "has been obtained" (a key, at the end of a quest). "Obtained" followed by the name (an axe, straight after a goblin kill and just before the line saying the goblin "has been defeated"). The item "has been acquired" (a gem, and a stone handed over by another character). The System then opens an item window with Acquisition Difficulty, Type and a line of description. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld, Ragnarok ch. 4](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-4)
- Sequel only: quest rewards come as a line that the quest "has been completed", then "You've received rewards for completing the quest", then a yes or no prompt to confirm the rewards, and only then the list (in the example, 50 ability points and 3 Strength points). Other notices use the same bracket pattern: "Level up!", a skill "has been learned", "You have entered" a dungeon. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld, Ragnarok ch. 5](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-5)
- Sequel only: "can be stored in your inventory or sold at the store" is a standing sentence on item cards. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld, Ragnarok ch. 142](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-142)
