# 13. The official games and how they turn the source into mechanics

**Covers:** the licensed Solo Leveling video games (*Solo Leveling: Arise*, *Arise Overdrive* and the unreleased *Karma*) plus other licensed products: what exists, how each game turns the story into combat, classes, gates, bosses and progression, how it was received, and which of its mechanics are ordinary genre vocabulary.
**Date:** 2026-11-16. "Current", "live" and "as of" mean this date. The source pages were read through a summarising tool, so check any exact number, date or version against its link before building on it.
**Source markers:** `[O]` official text (publisher releases, store pages, patch notes, developer statements and interviews); `[P]` press reporting; `[C]` critic or player opinion; `[G]` community guide (usually right, version-dependent); `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet; `[low]` added when the notes mark the item unconfirmed. A line that starts "Inference:" is the research notes' own reading and has no source behind it.

## At a glance

- Netmarble holds the game licence and its studio Netmarble Neo made all three titles: *Arise* (free-to-play gacha action RPG, mobile and PC, 8 May 2024, still live), *Arise Overdrive* (buy-once, no gacha, PC 24 November 2025, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S 4 November 2026) and *Karma* (isometric roguelite, unreleased, now aimed at Q1 2027).
- Arise's combat is one loop: empty a boss's break gauge or land a perfect dodge to open a short defence-down window, then spend weapon skills and the ultimate inside it. The gauge refills, so the loop repeats all fight.
- The perfect dodge ("Extreme Evasion") slows time and gives brief invulnerability. Only the protagonist gets a counter from it: a teleport behind the target that deals damage and applies a defence debuff, on a 10-second cooldown. Dodges are a limited stock with a cooldown.
- Guard and parry are Overdrive additions; no source lists either among Arise's inputs. In Overdrive a timed parry (the game's word is "Deflect") briefly breaks the enemy and fires a QTE, a stat widens the parry window, and one skill tree is fuelled by parries. No source gives a window in frames or seconds, a guard meter or a stamina cost.
- Gates in Arise are a farming board with two independent axes: rank E to S (difficulty and yield) and type by colour (reward table). In 2026 the operators removed re-rolling, Red Gates and Special Gates, set difficulty automatically and let the player pick the reward, after naming "re-searching for S-rank Gates" as a pain point.
- Arise launched with six classes (Tanker, Fighter, Mage, Assassin, Ranger, Healer) and five elements (Fire, Water, Wind, Light, Dark), both fixed per Hunter. What a Hunter really does is set by skill tags (break, heal, buff, debuff, shield-break). "Breaker" is a function, not a class, and later units are sold by role label.
- Bosses are pattern fights: readable tells (glowing eyes, a stance, ground markers), a refilling break gauge, an elemental weakness, usually a timer, and one signature mechanic each. Igris opens every attempt with the same jump slash, which teaches the dodge-then-counter rhythm.
- Camera and lock-on are named weak points in both released games: weak on touchscreens in Arise, and patched on day one yet still criticised in Overdrive.
- Arise: about 70 on review aggregators, an estimated $100 million plus in three months, 70 million downloads by October 2026. Overdrive: OpenCritic 64, Steam 75 percent positive, an estimated 355,000 Steam copies. Praise for both is the moment-to-moment combat and faithful presentation. Criticism for both is repetition and grind; Overdrive adds reused maps, health-padded and recycled bosses, mobile-style menus, crashes and forced online play.
- The three games take three positions on automation: Arise has auto and semi-auto and is extending them, Overdrive's developers say it has no auto-battle, and Karma's producer calls manual control essential.
- Game-original Hunters, shadows and stories are made with the original author's feedback. No source has the author or publisher calling any of it canon.
- The notes' analysis is that the mechanics are common action-RPG vocabulary, while the names, characters, creature and boss designs, story, coined terms and the look of the "System" belong to the licence. That is design inference, not legal advice.

## Solo Leveling: Arise (mobile and PC, free-to-play)

### Combat loop

Arise is a stage-based third-person action RPG. The whole fight is built around opening a defence-down window and spending the big attacks inside it.

**Two modes of control**

- In Jinwoo mode the player controls the protagonist, who fights alongside called-in Hunters and summoned Shadows. In Hunter mode the player fields three Hunters and tags between them. `[P]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- The director said the two modes exist to balance Jinwoo's overwhelming power against the other Hunters and to vary play. He named two design pillars: faithful recreation of the source and making combat as fun as possible. `[O]` [Final Weapon interview](https://finalweapon.net/2024/04/23/solo-leveling-arise-interview-netmarble-seong-keon-jin-inho-kim)
- The director said the 2022 G-Star prototype "lacked depth and strategy", so strategic layers were added, and that players are meant to enjoy well-timed QTEs and counterattacks. The team aimed for the same feel on every platform, with Xbox and DualShock pads supported. `[O]` [Anime Corner interview](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-arise-seong-keon-jin-interview/)

**Attack chains and skills**

- Jinwoo's inputs: basic attacks, two basic skills, QTE skills, weapon skills, support skills, dodge and dash. `[P]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Core Attacks spend energy that basic attacks rebuild. The Intelligence stat shortens cooldowns. `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- Some QTE skills trigger only after a perfect dodge; a perfect dodge opens QTE skills or a tag-in. `[S]` [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/how-use-extreme-evasion-solo-leveling-arise); `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- Mashing the basic attack does not clear stages. Stages add optional mid-fight objectives, such as using a skill from a marked spot or landing two perfect dodges in one fight. `[C]` [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/solo-leveling-arise/review); `[C]` [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-solo-leveling-arise-presentation-impresses/)
- No source describes the length or cancel rules of Jinwoo's basic chain. Two unit kits show how basic attacks feed other things: one Hunter's ultimate unlocks after two full three-hit basic combos, and a Successor's loop is a skill, five basic attacks, a skill, a QTE and five more basics (see "Named examples" below). `[G]` [Inven, Charlotte](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/19000/solo-leveling-arise-core-features-of-dark-attribute-main-dps-charlotte); `[G]` [Inven, Miro](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/21653/the-successor-finally-arrives-the-first-protagonist-is-the-wind-attribute-dealer-miro)
- In September 2024 the developers said a new Skill Rune came out stronger than planned because of a bug and was kept because it demands more manual skill. `[O]` [Inven, developer Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/18996/solo-leveling-arise-developer-livestream-qa-for-20-update)

**The dodge and its counter**

- The perfect dodge is called Extreme Evasion: pressing dodge just before a hit lands slows time and gives brief invulnerability. An ordinary dodge has no slow-down. `[G]` [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/solo-leveling-arise/dodge-extreme-evade)
- Timing cue: the dodge button fills with red as an enemy winds up, and the player presses when it is fully red. `[S]` [Gamer Tweak](https://gamertweak.com/use-extreme-evasion-in-solo-leveling-arise)
- Dodges are a limited stock. The remaining ones show as segments in a ring above the sprint icon and a cooldown starts once they are spent. The guide gives no count or cooldown length. `[G]` [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/solo-leveling-arise/dodge-extreme-evade)
- A knocked-down character can press dodge to stand up sooner, also on a cooldown. `[G]` [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/solo-leveling-arise/dodge-extreme-evade)
- Dodge is Space on PC, X on a PlayStation pad and A on an Xbox pad. `[G]` [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/solo-leveling-arise/dodge-extreme-evade)
- Telegraphs: weak enemies telegraph plainly, stronger ones feint or delay to bait an early dodge. Named tells are flashing eyes and area markers. Plain basic attacks are the hardest to dodge because they carry the least warning. `[G]` [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/solo-leveling-arise/dodge-extreme-evade); `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- The counter is called Shadow Step: after an Extreme Evasion Jinwoo teleports behind the target, deals damage and applies a Defense Decrease debuff. Cooldown 10 seconds. `[G]` [Talk Android, Igris guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/455563-how-to-beat-igris-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Only Jinwoo has this counter. Other Hunters can perfect-dodge but get no counter. `[S]` [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/how-use-extreme-evasion-solo-leveling-arise)
- One unattributed snippet gave 3 seconds of slow motion and up to 60 percent defence reduction at maximum skill level. `[S][low]`
- Inference: Shadow Step is functionally "perfect dodge, then teleport backstab with a defence-down debuff on a cooldown". Reserving the dodge counter for the protagonist is how Arise says "Jinwoo is special" in mechanics.

**The break gauge**

- Bosses carry a Break gauge, shown as a white bar. Emptying it stuns the boss for a few seconds and applies Defense Decrease. The gauge then refills, so breaking repeats through the fight. `[G]` [Talk Android, Igris guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/455563-how-to-beat-igris-solo-leveling-arise/); `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- Break damage comes from skills tagged "Break", which carry light, medium or heavy break values, and from support skills of Hunters built as breakers. Hitting the boss's weak element raises break damage. `[G]` [Talk Android, Igris guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/455563-how-to-beat-igris-solo-leveling-arise/); `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- While the gauge is up the enemy takes reduced damage. On some bosses the reduction is heavy enough to make the break mandatory. `[G]` [Inven, Kargalgan guide](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/18942/guide-for-almighty-shaman-kargalgan-use-meilin-or-hwang-dongsoo-to-break-the-shield)
- Scale of the window: a 2026 unit's kit extends a target's Break state by 6 seconds when she tags in (and a separate "Overload" state by 15). `[G]` [Inven, Miro](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/21653/the-successor-finally-arrives-the-first-protagonist-is-the-wind-attribute-dealer-miro)
- Inference: Arise's break ends in a debuff and the gauge refills. It opens a window; it does not end in a kill animation.

**Ultimates and the burst window**

- Ultimates need energy and have long cooldowns. `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- The standard pattern is to hold the ultimate and weapon skills until the target has Defense Decrease from a Break or a Shadow Step. `[G]` [Talk Android, Igris guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/455563-how-to-beat-igris-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Manual ultimates in team fights were announced as coming in September 2024. `[O]` [Inven, developer Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/18996/solo-leveling-arise-developer-livestream-qa-for-20-update)

**Weapon swapping and elements**

- Jinwoo equips two weapons. Using a weapon skill also switches to that weapon. Hunters have no weapon skills. `[P]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Jinwoo's element comes from the weapon he holds: a Light bow against the Dark boss Igris, a Wind weapon or Dark sword against Kargalgan. `[G]` [Talk Android, Igris guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/455563-how-to-beat-igris-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Weakness is set per enemy. Kargalgan resists Fire and Light and is weak to Wind and Dark. `[G]` [Inven, Kargalgan guide](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/18942/guide-for-almighty-shaman-kargalgan-use-meilin-or-hwang-dongsoo-to-break-the-shield)

**Supports, tagging and shadows**

- In Jinwoo mode he brings up to three teammates whose skills are called in during the fight. `[C]` [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/solo-leveling-arise/review)
- In Hunter mode the support skill swaps the active character instead. `[P]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- From April 2026 a "Successor" can sit in Jinwoo's "Shadow Power" slot and tag in for about 30 seconds. `[G]` [Inven, Miro](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/21653/the-successor-finally-arrives-the-first-protagonist-is-the-wind-attribute-dealer-miro)
- The October 2026 update's Deep Containment Zone fields four Hunters with mid-fight tagging and combination attacks, and a "Union" combat system has been previewed. `[P]` [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Solo-Leveling-Aise-changes-combat-forever-in-2-5-update.1430041.0.html)

**Camera and lock-on**

- On mobile the player has no full control of the camera. Lock-on is a small crosshair icon that cycles targets, and it is inconsistent enough to send Jinwoo at the wrong enemy or at empty space. The view is narrow on a phone. `[C]` [Gamespace](https://gamespace.com/all-articles/news/solo-leveling-arise-review-reveals-a-story-that-is-all-about-power-creep/)
- A second reviewer calls the camera controls poor, says lock-on does not work well on a touchscreen and recommends a controller or the PC version, while still rating the combat among the best in mobile action RPGs. `[C]` [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-solo-leveling-arise-presentation-impresses/)

**Automation**

- Auto and semi-auto combat exist in both modes. Semi-auto is available from the start and is poor at avoiding attacks. Full auto unlocks at story chapter six and uses skills, support calls and dodges. Guides still advise manual play for hard content because auto does not time dodges or ultimates well. `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705); `[P]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- The August 2026 roadmap extends it: "full auto-play", faster cutscenes during auto-play and better auto-battle for Successors. `[O]` [Inven, August 2026 developer live](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25108/alternate-form-lee-joohee-teased-august-developer-live-outlines-future-update-direction)

**How critics placed it**

- Pocket Gamer found the combat engaging and compared it to *Punishing: Gray Raven*, but less forgiving. Gamespace called the dodge-and-counter feel *Devil May Cry*-like. Siliconera called it the best mobile action combat it had played outside *Genshin Impact*. `[C]` [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/solo-leveling-arise/review); `[C]` [Gamespace](https://gamespace.com/all-articles/news/solo-leveling-arise-review-reveals-a-story-that-is-all-about-power-creep/); `[C]` [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-solo-leveling-arise-presentation-impresses/)
- Inference: this is the mobile "character action" template (perfect dodge with slow motion, tag-in supports, break gauge, ultimate meter). The Solo Leveling flavour comes from who does it and what it is called, not from new rules.

### Progression

**Jinwoo**

- Each level gives five stat points. Strength raises attack. Vitality raises HP and defence. Agility raises critical rate and critical damage. Intelligence raises MP and shortens cooldowns. Perception raises Precision, which sets the floor of the damage range. Points can be reset for gold. Guides recommend Strength, Perception and Agility and skipping Vitality and Intelligence. `[G]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Job (class) change is unlocked through the story or by winning job-change battles. `[G]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Completing a job change grants new skills, stat points and a costume. `[S]` [EarlyGame](https://earlygame.com/guides/mobile-gaming/solo-leveling-arise-stats-guide)
- The October 2026 update raised the level cap from 160 to 170 and added a job change called "Requiem". `[P]` [Inven, 70 million](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27393/solo-leveling-arise-surpasses-70-million-downloads)
- Skill runes modify a skill, sometimes changing how it works and what damage type it deals. `[S]` [ItemLevel](https://itemlevel.net/solo-leveling-arise-complete-skills-runes-guide/)
- Weapons are advanced with duplicates and upgraded with gold and materials. A fully advanced low-rarity weapon can beat an unadvanced top-rarity one. `[G]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)

**Gear and later growth systems**

- Artifacts are the gear pieces, in Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary rarity, dropped from Gates. `[S]` [ItemLevel](https://itemlevel.net/solo-leveling-arise-complete-artifact-guide)
- Artifact sets give 2-piece and 4-piece bonuses. Example: +25 percent ultimate damage at two pieces and a 40 percent shorter ultimate cooldown at four. `[G]` [Talk Android, Igris guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/455563-how-to-beat-igris-solo-leveling-arise/)

**Hunters and gacha**

- Draws produce Hunters, Player Weapons and Hunter Weapons. Hunters are levelled with gold, upgraded with elixirs and "advanced" with duplicate copies. **Daily loop and keys**

- At launch, entry was limited by separate keys: Gate Keys, Encore Mission Keys and Instance Dungeon Keys refilled daily, Hunter Archive keys at one per day. `[G]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- An "Activity Fund" accrues idle rewards (gold, experience, crystals, upgrade materials) that lapse after 24 hours. `[G]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/)
- The game's "daily quests" are an ordinary mission checklist: clear six Gates, run three mining jobs, try ten artifact upgrades, claim the Activity Fund, level a Hunter. Filling the daily bar pays 100 Essence Stones. The weekly bar pays 250 in one guide and 300 in another. `[G]` [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/solo-leveling-arise-beginners-guide)
- The April to May 2026 revamp replaced the separate keys. Gates, Instance Dungeons, Encore Missions and a new Trial of the Abyss share one "Dungeon Entry Key": 100 a day, capped at 200, or 400 with the paid Hunter Association Subscription. Sweep (instant clear) works in all four, with 100 free sweep tickets a day and 500 more from a paid Daily Growth Support. `[G]` [Inven, revamped farming guide](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/21692/entry-ticket-consolidation-a-complete-guide-to-the-revamped-farming-system)
- The same revamp brought a faster run to level 150, one "Monarch's Milestone" guide meant to carry a beginner to about 2.5 million combat power, merged currencies, a monthly rotation for challenge modes and a large batch of free draws (the article's headline says 500). `[O]` [Inven, 2nd anniversary preview](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/21303/2nd-anniversary-update-preview-streamlined-growth-content-compression-and-500-free-draws)
- Inference: stats are mostly damage multipliers; the one distinctive stat is Perception as a minimum-damage floor. The split into Jinwoo-only and Hunter-only content makes the gacha roster necessary even though the protagonist is the star.

### Gates by rank and type

Gates in Arise are a repeatable resource-farming board, not a difficulty ladder for the story. The board described first is the launch design. Most of it was removed in 2026 (see "The 2026 revamp").

- Rank runs E, D, C, B, A, S. Higher ranks are harder and yield more. S is the hardest with the best rewards. `[G]` [Talk Android beginner's guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/460555-beginners-guide-solo-leveling-arise/); `[S]` [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/solo-leveling-arise-dungeon-breaks-gates-guide)
- Type is shown by colour and decides the reward table:

**Entry, re-rolling and difficulty**

- One Gate Key is consumed per successful clear. Keys come free daily or from the shop. `[S]` [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Solo-Leveling-Arise/archives/457471)
- "Rescan" re-rolls the board and can change each gate's type, rank and difficulty. Harder ranks become available as Jinwoo gets stronger. `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- Rescanning is also how the board's difficulty tier is raised, to "Difficulty 5" and beyond. `[S]` [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/solo-leveling-arise-gate-types-explained)
- A run is won by finishing inside its time limit with health left. No source gives the limit per rank. `[G]` [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/solo-leveling-arise-beginners-guide)
- Stages are tuned as checks against a timer and a recommended power number. The story's Igris fight suggested level 35 and 50,000 team power and is described as a damage check that can be beaten under-powered with good evasion. `[G]` [Talk Android, Igris guide](https://www.talkandroid.com/455563-how-to-beat-igris-solo-leveling-arise/)
- Launch-era criticism called the level and power gating of story progress close to a disaster. `[C][S]` [SpielTimes](https://www.spieltimes.io/news/solo-leveling-arise-game-review)

**What rank changes besides difficulty**

- Rank A and above can pay Mana Crystals and Essence Stones (premium currency). `[G]` [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/solo-leveling-arise-beginners-guide)
- Rank B and below can be auto-cleared once the player's level is high enough. `[G]` [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/solo-leveling-arise-beginners-guide)
- Clearing 3, 6 and 10 gates in a day pays bonus experience. `[G]` [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/solo-leveling-arise-beginners-guide)
- Sweep (auto-clear) unlocks at story chapter six and needs a large enough power lead over the gate. Jinwoo gates and Hunter gates draw on separate daily sweep points. `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)

**Mining**

- A cleared gate becomes minable. Up to three mining teams work cleared gates at once, extra teams cost Essence Stones, and rewards must be collected by hand to free a team. `[G]` [Level Winner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=111705)
- Yield depends on gate rank, gate type and a Mining Team Level. An S-rank gate mines for up to three hours. Guides suggest only bothering with B rank and above. `[G]` [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/solo-leveling-arise-beginners-guide); mining level `[S]` [dot.gg](https://dotgg.gg/solo-leveling-arise/gates-guide/)

**The 2026 revamp**

- At the 2nd anniversary (April 2026) the developers named "having to keep re-searching for S-rank Gates" as a pain point. `[O]` [Inven, 2nd anniversary preview](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/21303/2nd-anniversary-update-preview-streamlined-growth-content-compression-and-500-free-draws)
- The April to May 2026 revamp removed difficulty adjustment, rescanning, Red Gates and Special Gates. The gate menu now shows blue, purple and green gates. Difficulty is set automatically from Jinwoo's level. The player picks a target reward per gate (gold, elixirs, experience, skill scrolls, shadow materials, weapon or artifact materials) instead of rolling for one. `[G]` [Inven, revamped farming guide](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/21692/entry-ticket-consolidation-a-complete-guide-to-the-revamped-farming-system)
- Whether the letter ranks survive the revamp is not stated. See "Where sources conflict".

**Inferences from the notes**

- Inference: rank (how hard, how much) and type (which reward table) are two independent axes. The only per-rank numbers found are indirect, and they show rank gating convenience and reward type as well as difficulty.
- Inference: in Arise "red gate" and "dungeon break" are colours and reward tables. The fiction's stakes for them are not modelled in the rules.
- Inference: difficulty is mostly stat scaling plus a timer, and reviews of both games show players read that as grind.

### Boss design

**The shared grammar**

- Bosses are pattern fights with readable tells, a refilling break gauge, an elemental weakness and usually a timer. Each named boss adds one signature mechanic. Most attacks show circular or linear ground indicators; some lift or stagger the player and remove control. `[G]` [Inven, Kargalgan guide](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/18942/guide-for-almighty-shaman-kargalgan-use-meilin-or-hwang-dongsoo-to-break-the-shield)
- Asked about hard, grindy bosses, the producer said Boss Dungeons exist to create tense moments and tied the challenge to Jinwoo's growth through levelling. `[O]` [Beebom interview](https://beebom.com/exclusive-solo-leveling-arise-dev-interview)
- Presentation: the Igris and Kang Taeshik fights cut between 2D and 3D. The giant temple statue is shown in 2D animation because it conveyed the smile better. Kasaka and the centipedes were the hardest creatures to animate, and the centipede legs were hand-drawn. `[O]` [Final Weapon interview](https://finalweapon.net/2024/04/23/solo-leveling-arise-interview-netmarble-seong-keon-jin-inho-kim)
- Inference: because a boss's gimmick is often answered by owning the right gacha Hunter or element, the fight becomes a roster check.

## Solo Leveling: Arise Overdrive (PC and console, premium)

Overdrive is Arise rebuilt as a buy-once action RPG. The gacha is removed, Hunters and weapons are earned and crafted, guard and parry sit beside the perfect dodge, Jinwoo gets class skill trees, and four-player co-op raids are added. Netmarble calls it "an original take" on the story, and the stores say it holds the webtoon story plus adventures exclusive to the game. `[O]` [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2373990/); `[P]` [Inven, out now on consoles](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28147/solo-leveling-arise-overdrive-is-out-now-on-ps5-and-xbox-series-xs)

### What differs in combat

**Guard and parry**

- Guard and parry "block or deflect" attacks. A parry leaves the enemy briefly broken and a QTE fires in that window. The publisher frames these, with Overdrive skills and Chain Smash, as console-style mechanics. `[O]` [GameMeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/mv.php?gid=1768475)
- The game's word for a timed parry is "Deflect". A basic attack right after one gives a "Deflect Combo Attack". `[G]` [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/duelist-build-guide-for-solo-leveling-arise-overdrive/)
- The Perception stat widens the Deflect timing window. No window length in frames or seconds is published. `[G]` [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/duelist-build-guide-for-solo-leveling-arise-overdrive/)
- For the Duelist tree perfect Deflects are a main damage source. A node called "Swift Shadow: Counterattack" turns a Deflect into a combo, and the Duelist's Overdrive charges from Deflects and releases as a heavy counterstrike on a perfect one. A second site calls it the hardest of Jinwoo's four Overdrives to use because the payoff needs a deflect after activation. `[G]` [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/duelist-build-guide-for-solo-leveling-arise-overdrive/); `[S]` [AlcastHQ](https://alcasthq.com/slao-duelist-build/)
- A perfect parry or an Extreme Evasion banks a "retaliation" that can be spent on a skill or ultimate follow-up. It expires and the game does not make clear when. `[C]` [PlayDay.One](https://playday.one/2026/01/02/solo-leveling-arise-overdrive-review)
- A parry, counter or timed dodge triggers a skill that hits everything nearby or a counter-kick that stuns. `[C]` [PlayStation Universe](https://www.psu.com/reviews/solo-leveling-arise-overdrive-ps5-review/)
- Reviewers found the dodge responsive and the parry window generous. `[C]` [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/solo-leveling-arise-overdrive-review-shadowfall)
- On a pad, attacks are on two face buttons and one button (A or LB) serves as both dash and parry. One reviewer found the timed dodges and counters present but lacking finesse. `[C]` [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/?p=116435)
- Inference: Overdrive's parry is tuned by build, not fixed. A stat widens the window and one tree's special is fuelled by it.

**Other combat rules**

## Solo Leveling: Karma (unreleased)

**Premise and format**

- An isometric (quarter-view) hack-and-slash roguelite set in the 27 years Jinwoo spends fighting alone in the dimensional gap after he uses the Cup of Reincarnation, a period the original only summarises. The story's central object is a "Broken Cup of Reincarnation" restored through Jinwoo's fragmented memories. `[O]` [Netmarble release, Anime Expo](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/netmarble-unveils-major-solo-leveling-karma-reveals-at-anime-expo-2026); `[P]` [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/05/solo-leveling-karma-combat-trailer/)
- It is designed as a mobile roguelite. The producer denied that PC had been deprioritised but said a PC or console version is not confirmed. `[O]` [Inven, TGS Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/solo-leveling-karma-fills-original-story-gap-as-a-roguelite-39-qas-live-from-tgs)

**Combat**

- Controls are dash, basic attack and special attack. Dashes are not only for dodging: movement is built into skills to cut control fatigue. Effects trigger on conditions such as hits, critical hits and crowd control. `[O]` [Inven, TGS Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/solo-leveling-karma-fills-original-story-gap-as-a-roguelite-39-qas-live-from-tgs)
- Five weapon types are planned for launch, among them daggers, greatswords, handguns, bows and gauntlets. Each has its own basic-attack animations and special skills. Weapon-growth passives combine with Blessings so the same weapon can be built as one heavy hit or as a fast set-up tool. `[O]` [Inven, TGS Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/solo-leveling-karma-fills-original-story-gap-as-a-roguelite-39-qas-live-from-tgs)
- The G-STAR 2025 demo showed daggers, scythes and bows, each with "responsive evasion and counters". The July 2026 combat trailer shows weapon switching during combat. `[P]` [But Why Tho?](https://butwhytho.net/2025/11/solo-leveling-karma-demo-g-star); `[P]` [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/05/solo-leveling-karma-combat-trailer/)
- The general producer said he insisted on manual controls as essential to a roguelite action game, acknowledged that the team studied well-made quarter-view roguelites, and said competitive or co-operative content would be added to keep a single-player genre going as a service. `[P]` [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025111316463614623)
- Difficulty is meant to be "challenging but fair", with bosses beatable through build choices. `[O]` [Inven, TGS Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/solo-leveling-karma-fills-original-story-gap-as-a-roguelite-39-qas-live-from-tgs)

**Runs and builds**

- A run starts with a weakened Jinwoo and builds toward Monarch-level power. Buffs are offered at random and the player picks one at each level-up. They come in two families, weapon-specific and "Monarch's Authority". "Blessing Cores" let the player absorb Monarch powers. The TGS demo had more than 200 buffs. `[P]` [Saiganak, TGS report](https://saiganak.com/en/event/tgs2026-sololeveling-karma-for-the-media-event-report/)
- Meta-progression: a "Recording System" saves a finished Blessing build for use in seasonal endgame content, and "Challenge Dungeons" are asynchronous clear-time races run with saved builds. `[O]` [Inven, TGS Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/solo-leveling-karma-fills-original-story-gap-as-a-roguelite-39-qas-live-from-tgs)
- Modes shown: a Story Mode and a wave-based Survival Mode at TGS 2026; a Time Attack Dungeon of fast boss kills and an Event Dungeon of large wave battles at G-STAR 2025. `[P]` [Inven, TGS 2026](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26122/solo-leveling-karma-unveils-key-systems-and-content-at-tgs-2026); `[P]` [But Why Tho?](https://butwhytho.net/2025/11/solo-leveling-karma-demo-g-star)

**Shadows and characters**

- Shadows work two ways. Some are full playable characters, controlled directly and not summoned. Separately, a "Shadow Summon" is timed against a boss attack to counter it and trigger a joint skill with Jinwoo, leaving the boss incapacitated for a while. Enemies of the new "aberrant" type cannot be made into shadows. `[P]` [Saiganak, TGS report](https://saiganak.com/en/event/tgs2026-sololeveling-karma-for-the-media-event-report/)
- The TGS build had eight playable characters. Five are named: Sung Jinwoo, Beru and three new characters, Azalea, Kali and Nemel. Cha Hae-In, Choi Jong-In, Igris and Kaisel appear but were not confirmed playable. `[P]` [Saiganak, TGS report](https://saiganak.com/en/event/tgs2026-sololeveling-karma-for-the-media-event-report/); `[P]` [Inven, TGS 2026](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26122/solo-leveling-karma-unveils-key-systems-and-content-at-tgs-2026)
- Enemies include "Mutated Hunters" created by the Monarchs, with familiar faces returning as foes, and a new group called "Corrupted Hunters", which includes Cha Hae-In and Go Gunhee. Antares appears, voiced in English by Troy Baker. `[O]` [Netmarble release, Anime Expo](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/netmarble-unveils-major-solo-leveling-karma-reveals-at-anime-expo-2026); `[P]` [Saiganak, TGS report](https://saiganak.com/en/event/tgs2026-sololeveling-karma-for-the-media-event-report/)
- Bosses shown: Go Gunhee in an "aberrant" form in the Episode 1 story demo, and a Forest Guardian Centaur. `[P]` [Saiganak, TGS report](https://saiganak.com/en/event/tgs2026-sololeveling-karma-for-the-media-event-report/)

**Business and release**

- Characters are to be earned through play, not gacha, and the main paid products under discussion are flat-rate passes or funds. Not final. In November 2025 Netmarble's business head said the game centres on Jinwoo instead of releasing a new character every few weeks, with a monthly subscription or battle pass being considered. `[O]` [Inven, TGS Q&A](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/solo-leveling-karma-fills-original-story-gap-as-a-roguelite-39-qas-live-from-tgs); `[P]` [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025111316463614623)
- Pre-registration rewards include an outfit, cosmetics, boxes and a "Support Card". `[P]` [Inven, TGS 2026](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26122/solo-leveling-karma-unveils-key-systems-and-content-at-tgs-2026)
- Some coverage expects Korea and Japan first, with the West possibly later in 2027. `[S]` [Critical Hits](https://criticalhits.com.br/games/solo-leveling-karma-pode-chegar-ao-ocidente-apenas-em-2027/)
- No press hands-on was found. The pieces that read like previews are event reports: at TGS the live demo was played on stage by actor Kanata Hongo. `[P]` [GameMeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1780648)
- Inference: Karma is likely free-to-play (mobile-first, pre-registration rewards and boxes, treated as a live-service launch in earnings commentary), but the model is not final. Making shadows playable characters is the franchise's own answer to "how do you make an army fun to control", and it sidesteps pet AI.

- Inference: game-original characters are the property of Netmarble and the rights holders just as much as the source cast. "Invented for the game" does not make a name free to reuse.

**Genre vocabulary, in the notes' judgement** (usable under other names and presentation)

- Light attack chains, cooldown skills, an ultimate meter, weapon swapping.
- Perfect dodge with slow motion and a follow-up; parry and guard; a break, stagger or posture gauge that opens a damage window; back-attack bonuses; elemental weaknesses.
- Class archetypes (fighter, mage, assassin, tank, ranger, healer) and skill trees.
- Letter-graded difficulty tiers from E to S. The notes' reason for calling the scale a convention is unverified memory (see "Not stated or still open"). On that reading the risk lies in combining the letters with other distinctive elements, not in the letters.
- Dungeons entered through portals, instanced runs with a boss at the end, a hub with a blacksmith and mission board, crafting from boss parts, co-op raids, rarity-tiered gear and set bonuses, roguelite buff drafting.
- Boss-design grammar: a fixed opening attack, body-language tells, ground indicators, a mid-health escalation, one signature mechanic.

**Tied to the licence, in the notes' judgement** (only the parts with a source in this chapter; the parts the notes hold from memory are in "Not stated or still open")

- Every character, monster, place, guild and item name and its visual design. That includes game-original characters such as Emma Laurent, and bosses recognisable by silhouette such as a giant grinning stone statue in a temple or an ant king.
- The shadow army: the hero absorbs defeated enemies into a personal force that fights for him.
- The "System" as a premise and presentation (the term appears in reviews of the games).
- The plot beats: the weakest E-rank hunter who rises, the job change to necromancer, the Monarch of Shadows.
- Coined skill and system names: Shadow Step, Extreme Evasion, Monarch's Awakening, Chain Smash, and the others in the table above.
- The combination of "Gate", "Red Gate", "Dungeon Break" and "Hunter's Association" as a set. Individually these are ordinary words; together with ranks and hunters they read as this series.
- Trade dress: logos, typography, the comic-panel cut-in style, voice lines and music.

**Automation, three positions**

- Arise shipped with auto and semi-auto and in 2026 is adding "full auto-play". Overdrive's developer note says it has no auto-battle. Karma's producer says he insisted on manual controls as essential to the genre. `[O]` [Inven, August 2026 developer live](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25108/alternate-form-lee-joohee-teased-august-developer-live-outlines-future-update-direction); `[P]` [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025111316463614623)
