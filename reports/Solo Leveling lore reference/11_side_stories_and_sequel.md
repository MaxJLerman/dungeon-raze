# Side stories and the sequel Ragnarok

**Covers:** what Solo Leveling lore exists beyond the main story: the side stories and epilogue, the sequel *Solo Leveling: Ragnarok* (novel and webtoon), and other official material (art book, light novel extras, games, spin-offs).
**Date:** 2026-11-16. Publication status is as of this date.
**Source markers:** `[O]` official or licensed text; `[P]` press, explainer, recap or review site, including Wikipedia; `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki; `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet; `[F]` fan site, personal blog, forum or fan opinion; `[low]` added where the research marked a fact low confidence. A fact with `[W]` and another marker has independent support; one with `[W]` alone does not, and chapter references carrying only `[W]` are the wiki's own and unchecked. The wiki material was obtained through the wiki's API after the site refused automated reads, and the project owner chose on 2026-11-16 to keep it, labelled.

Names follow the licensed English editions where one is established. The sequel's hero is "Suho" in the licensed English novel and "Sooho" in the licensed English comic; this chapter uses Suho. "Novel" and "webtoon" in the sequel sections mean the Ragnarok novel and the Ragnarok webtoon unless the text says otherwise.

## 1. The side stories

The side stories are Chugong's own continuation after the final battle. They show Sung Jinwoo living in the reset timeline, which has no gates, after a 27-year war fought alone against the Monarchs. They end by handing the story to his son, which is where the sequel starts.

### 1.1 Publication

**Novel**

- The side story ran as 27 web chapters on KakaoPage from August 7, 2017 to March 13, 2018. `[P]` [MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net/manga/155115/Solo_Leveling_Side_Story)
- The whole novel was serialised from July 25, 2016 to March 13, 2018. `[P]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- The side-story chapters are novel ch. 244 to 270, and the novel has 270 chapters in all. `[W][P]` [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-solo-leveling-arcs-in-order/)
- In print, D&C Media (Papyrus imprint) issued the side story as its own volume on April 18, 2018 ("나 혼자만 레벨업 외전"). The Korean first edition is 13 main volumes plus that volume. The 2019 revised edition has 8 volumes and folds the side story into volume 8. `[P]` [Korean Wikipedia](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/나_혼자만_레벨업)
- MyAnimeList lists the printed side-story book as 12 chapters. `[P]` [MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net/manga/155115/Solo_Leveling_Side_Story)
- In English, Yen Press novel vol. 8, the last volume, came out on July 18, 2023. Its blurb covers only the war with Antares and does not mention the side stories. `[O]` [Yen Press](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975319410-solo-leveling-vol-8-novel)
- That volume prints the material after the main text as ten "Bonus Stories" and six "Epilogue" parts, without web chapter numbers. `[F]` [Yonocruz book notes](https://marloyonocruz.com/2025/08/04/book-notes-solo-leveling-vol-8-by-chugong/)

**Webtoon**

- The main webtoon ended on December 29, 2021 with chapter 179. `[P]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- The artist DUBU (Jang Sung-rak, also spelled Jang Seong-rak, chief executive of Redice Studio) died on July 23, 2022 of a cerebral haemorrhage. `[P]` [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-webtoon-to-get-side-story-in-honor-of-dubu/)
- The side-story webtoon was announced on January 9 to 10, 2023 as a tribute to him, to be drawn by a group of his colleagues, with Chugong back as writer. It launched on KakaoPage on January 21, 2023 with weekly Wednesday updates. `[P]` [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-webtoon-to-get-side-story-in-honor-of-dubu/)
- Kakao Entertainment's press release of January 26, 2023 dates the global release: Tapas in the United States on January 27, 2023 at 5 a.m. Pacific, Kakao Webtoon in Taiwan and Indonesia the same day, Thailand on February 6, weekly after that. `[O]` [Kakao Entertainment](https://newsroom.kakaoent.com/news/kakao-webtoon-unlocks-global-hit-webtoon-solo-leveling-side-story-with-3hr-wait-till-free/)
- The same release describes the episodes as "side stories and an epilogue" written after the web novel ended. It says the artists, a group called Disciples, largely trained under Jang, that Jang had begun work on the side stories before he died, and that his family endorsed the continuation. It advertises a battle between "the Giants" and a shadow army of ten million, and the romance of Jinwoo and Cha Hae-In (Haein). `[O]` [Kakao Entertainment](https://newsroom.kakaoent.com/news/kakao-webtoon-unlocks-global-hit-webtoon-solo-leveling-side-story-with-3hr-wait-till-free/)
- Credits: story Chugong, art DISCIPLES (REDICE STUDIO), adaptation h-goon. Tappytoon's copyright line gives DUBU for the main series and DISCIPLES for the Epilogue. `[O]` [Yen Press vol. 14](https://yenpress.com/titles/9798400904646-solo-leveling-vol-14-comic)
- The wiki credits the adaptation of the whole webtoon to Gi So-Ryeong (2018 to 2019) and H-Goon (2019 to 2023), and gives the webtoon as running from April 23, 2018, with 200 chapters. `[W]`
- The run had 21 episodes. D&C's editorial team said it "concluded with Episode 21" on June 1, 2023. The wiki gives May 31, 2023 and Wikipedia says January to May 2023 (see section 5). `[P]` [Animehunch](https://animehunch.com/solo-leveling-side-story-ends-with-chapter-21/)
- The 21 episodes are webtoon ch. 180 to 200, so the series is usually counted as 179 chapters plus 21 side stories, 200 in all. `[F][W]` [SoloHunterHub](https://solohunterhub.com/solo-leveling-ending-explained/)
- Platform names for the same 21 episodes: "Special Episodes" on Tapas, "Solo Leveling Epilogue" on Tappytoon. AniList credits DISCIPLES for ch. 180 to 200. Tapas lists 202 episodes in all, which the guide reporting it does not explain. `[P]` [ChapterBrief](https://www.chapterbrief.net/blog/solo-leveling-reading-guide)
- Chapter number equals 179 plus the special-episode number. No source states this rule. It is the research's own reading, and it holds at the four points that can be checked: Special Episode 21 is ch. 200, Special Episode 11 holds the case the wiki marks at ch. 190, a "Side Story 17" video title is attached to ch. 196, and a "Side Story 3 Part 2" caption to ch. 182. `[P][W][S]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-episode-71-change-original-manhwa-ending/)
- Numbering can differ by one or two between platforms (whether the finale is 178 or 179, whether a prologue is counted), so "ch. 180 to 200" is the common English numbering and not a universal one. This is also the research's reading. `[P]`
- Print collections of the webtoon side stories: vol. 14 "Side Stories 1" (Korean June 9, 2025; English from Ize Press December 30, 2025, 312 pages) and vol. 15 "Side Stories 2" (Korean October 20, 2025; English March 17, 2026, 272 pages; translator Hye Young Im, letterer Abigail Blackman). Vol. 15 is the last volume of the comic. `[O]` [Yen Press vol. 15](https://yenpress.com/titles/9798400904660-solo-leveling-vol-15-comic)

### 1.2 The bridge from the main story: the reset, the 27-year war and the return

- After Antares falls (webtoon ch. 177), Jinwoo asks the Rulers to use the Cup of Reincarnation one last time. They warn him that the Monarchs will come back with their memories and that the Cup cannot be used again. Webtoon ch. 178 and 179 are the epilogue. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-new-final-arc-release/)
- He returns to his younger body, lives for a short time as a schoolboy, then enters the gap between dimensions and kills the Monarchs one by one over 27 years, leaving Antares for last. Only about two years pass outside, so afterwards he ages his appearance gradually. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-ending-explained/)
- Gates, magic beasts and hunters no longer exist. Only the Rulers' former vessels know what he did. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-ending-explained/)
- Every Monarch except Baran comes back with their memories, and Jinwoo fights them in the "Dimensional Crack" (webtoon ch. 178). `[W]`
- Webtoon ch. 178 mentions him fighting Yogumunt, Monarch of Transfiguration, before Antares. The wiki adds that Yogumunt dies two years in, when only he and Antares are left. `[P][W]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-ending-explained/)
- Antares is last. The fight is in his palace and he opens it in his transformed state. He leaves Jinwoo a burn on the left hand, which Jinwoo covers with a black glove (webtoon ch. 181). `[W]`
- In the novel, Jinwoo's one wound from the last fight with the Dragon Monarch was taken shielding Igris. The source does not say where the wound is, so it may or may not be the left-hand burn. `[F]` [Yonocruz book notes](https://marloyonocruz.com/2025/08/04/book-notes-solo-leveling-vol-8-by-chugong/)
- One blade of Kamish's Wrath broke on Antares' skin and the pair wore out over nearly three decades, so Jinwoo had Antares' fangs made into six daggers. The wiki cites the origin to Ragnarok novel ch. 244 and the design to webtoon ch. 183. `[W]`
- Shadows that existed before the reset point stay with him. The ones made from monsters, such as Beru and Igris, kept their memories. The ones raised from humans, such as Iron and Greed, are gone because those people are alive again. `[P][W]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ending-jinwoo-fate-explained/)
- The size of the shadow army after the war is given three ways: ten million, ten thousand and 100,000 (see section 5). Ten million has the official source. `[O]` [Kakao Entertainment](https://newsroom.kakaoent.com/news/kakao-webtoon-unlocks-global-hit-webtoon-solo-leveling-side-story-with-3hr-wait-till-free/)
- The main webtoon never shows the 27-year war or the second fight with Antares, and its epilogue skips how Jinwoo regained his strength. CBR calls the webtoon ending a more rushed form of the novel's. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ending-jinwoo-fate-explained/)

### 1.5 The webtoon's special episodes (ch. 180 to 200)

No chapter-by-chapter synopsis was found. What follows is every event that a source ties to a volume or chapter, then the events with no chapter mark.

**By volume**

- Vol. 14 (Side Stories 1): Woo Jinchul, president of the Hunter Association in the erased timeline, is now a violent-crimes detective. Criminals keep turning themselves in, terrified of shadow ant creatures. His only lead is a track coach who saw the star athlete Sung Jinwoo with them. When the two meet, a touch from Jinwoo gives Jinchul knowledge of the erased timeline, and he keeps the secret. `[O]` [Yen Press vol. 14](https://yenpress.com/titles/9798400904646-solo-leveling-vol-14-comic)
- Vol. 14: Jinwoo joins the school track team with no experience because he wants to meet someone at competitions: Cha Hae-In, a track star in this timeline. At a meet he also runs into Kim Chul, the former shadow Iron, who feels a strong urge to kneel to him. `[P]` [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-14/)
- Vol. 14: Jinwoo reunites with Yoo Jinho, who calls him "Boss" without knowing why. `[P]` [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-14/)
- Vol. 14: the Rulers send an envoy to thank Jinwoo. Someone in the United States reports the sky cracking. The Rulers explain that the Titans, who had invaded the Rulers' own world in the erased timeline, have sensed Jinwoo's power and are heading for Earth. Jinwoo goes to America and he and the shadow army deal with them quickly. `[P]` [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-14/)
- Vol. 14: Jinwoo accepts Jinchul's offer and becomes a police officer, nicknamed "The Phantom". The volume closes on an ominous dream showing another version of the event that started everything. `[P]` [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-14/)
- Vol. 15 (Side Stories 2), official blurb: Jinwoo and Hae-In are surprised when their toddler son shows signs of inheriting the Shadow Monarch's powers. The volume then follows him as a teenager who begins dreaming of being trapped in a dungeon full of monsters. `[O]` [Yen Press vol. 15](https://yenpress.com/titles/9798400904660-solo-leveling-vol-15-comic)
- Vol. 15, from a review: people from the old timeline still feel drawn to Jinwoo, some take the feeling for love and are told it is not, and in some cases he gives their memories back. He is the only awakened person and uses the shadow army against criminals and new gates. The boy's parents erased his memories and sealed his powers to protect his childhood. `[P]` [Anime UK News](https://animeuknews.net/2026/04/solo-leveling-volume-15-review/)

### 1.6 Lives in the reset timeline

- Sung Il-Hwan is alive again, a firefighter. As a former Ruler's vessel he keeps his memories, then asks the Rulers to erase them so he is not a burden on his son. `[P][W]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-every-character-fate-explained/)
- Park Kyung-Hye never contracts Eternal Slumber. `[P]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Go Gunhee is terminally ill. Jinwoo heals him with the Holy Water of Life, which buys ten years. When it wears off he declines a second dose and dies peacefully. `[P][W]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-every-character-fate-explained/)
- Yoo Jinho founds a game company, Ahjin Soft, instead of the Ahjin Guild, and marries Jinwoo's sister Jinah. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-every-character-fate-explained/)
- Thomas Andre is a wrestler and a friend of Norma Selner, who is a fortune-teller. Baek Yoonho is a firefighter, Choi Jong-In (Jongin) a businessman, Ma Dongwook a judoka, Lim Tae-Gyu (Taegyu) a chauffeur, Lee Joohee a nursing trainee and Song Chi-Yul a kumdo teacher. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-every-character-fate-explained/)
- Min Byung-Gyu is a doctor. The wiki adds that he was a history instructor first, marries Oh Seonjoo and has nightmares about the ant. `[P][W]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-every-character-fate-explained/)

### 1.7 What the side stories set up for the sequel

Four things from the side stories are reused by Ragnarok. The list is the research's summary, not a statement by any source. `[P]`

- A shadow army that persists in a world without mana and is used covertly.
- The Titans, a second outside race, which had preyed on the Rulers' world.
- Rulers who are grateful to Jinwoo and afraid of him.
- A Shadow Monarch power that passes to a child.

## 2. The sequel: Solo Leveling: Ragnarok

Ragnarok is a sequel web novel by Daul, not by Chugong, and a webtoon adapted from it by Redice Studio. The novel is complete. The webtoon is running and has covered roughly the first third of the novel's chapter count while changing a good deal of it.

### 2.2 Premise

- **Both.** Official synopsis: "the Absolute Ones", creators of the universes outside our own, seek to fill the power void left when one of their number died. Gates reopen and monsters pour through. Sung Suho must conquer a shadow dungeon and learn his father's legacy. `[O]` [Tapas](https://tapas.io/series/solo-leveling-ragnarok/info)
- **Novel.** The licensed English synopsis names the enemy: the Itarim, gods of other universes, aim to fill the void left by the Absolute Being. Jinwoo sends Beru, the shadow ant king, to awaken Suho's powers. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok)
- **Both.** Jinwoo leaves Earth to join the Rulers against the Itarim and seals Suho's memories and powers so he can live normally. Cha Hae-In disappears when Jinwoo departs. When the Itarim reach Earth, Beru unseals Suho, who receives the System Jinwoo used and sets out to defend Earth and find his parents. `[P]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- **Webtoon.** Suho is an ordinary college student with a talent for drawing. His parents vanished shortly before gates began appearing three years earlier. He dreams of fighting monsters and levelling up as in a game. During a monster outbreak on campus he tries to save a classmate and receives the message "[Congratulations on becoming a Player.]" `[O][P]` [Yen Press vol. 1](https://yenpress.com/titles/9798400904776-solo-leveling-ragnarok-vol-1-comic)
- **Novel.** The "Great Cataclysm", when gates opened worldwide, was two years ago. Suho was about 20 and a first-year student then, and is now a third-year painting student at Hanguk (Hankuk) University. His parents disappeared in the summer of his first year of high school. As a child he dreamed of armoured knights, ant soldiers, dragons and a final boss who looked like his father, levelling up as he won. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 1](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-1)
- **Both.** Beru arrives on Earth the day Suho awakens, tells him the truth about his father and becomes his trainer. Wikipedia places the gates' return in Suho's third year of university. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-suho-monarch-explained/)
- **Both.** People are hunters again. Woo Jinchul, whose memories Jinwoo unwittingly restored, controls the gate openings in the reset timeline and prepares for the war with the Itarim. Awakened criminals are classed as "Villains". He is chief of the Hunter's Association and an A rank. `[P]` [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- **Webtoon.** Beru is weakened on Earth by the story's rules for travel between dimensions. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-sequel-better-than-the-original-manhwa/)
- How long after the original the sequel is set is given four ways (section 5).

### 2.4 The power system and how it differs from the original

**The System**

- **Both.** Suho is the second Player after Jinwoo. The System was revised for him and trains him faster than it trained his father. Beru trains him through it, and in the novel Antares does too. `[P]` [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-are-there-other-players-in-the-system/)
- **Webtoon.** CBR describes it as a unique form of the System that Jinwoo made for his successor. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/sung-suho-solo-leveling-ragnarok-jinwoo-comparison/)
- **Both.** Jinwoo removed the System's "malevolence". It now adapts itself, adding pet and companion features, and it held off an attack by an Itarim's Apostle. `[W]`
- **Webtoon.** The enemy side has a System too. Arsha, the Plague Monarch's successor, grows through one, and at least two other Monarch heirs are shown as Players with Systems. Who built it is disputed (section 5). `[P][W]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-chapter-64-monarch-system-architect-kandiaru/)
- **Novel.** Quests appear as named tasks with a timer ("Survival", four hours) and a reward prompt, and there are secret quests ("The Courage of the Helpless"). `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 6](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-6)
- **Both.** The System shop unlocks at level 20. A "Cursed Random Box" and the Shadow Dungeon Key appear early. `[W]`
- Because villains and enemy heirs also level, the sequel treats levelling as a general property of the setting and no longer unique to its hero. This is the research's reading. `[P]`

**Stats and levels**

- **Webtoon.** Stats use the original five attributes. At level 75 (ch. 67): Strength 195 (+34), Stamina 126 (+29), Agility 143 (+39), Intelligence 179 (+30), Sense 107 (+21). The bonuses come from Shadow Creation armour. With gear he beats Jinwoo's figures at level 81 (186, 145, 175, 189, 126; original ch. 80). CBR estimates about 1.1 levels per chapter. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-chapter-67-suho-higher-stats-than-jinwoo/)
- **Webtoon.** Level 42 at the end of Season 1 (ch. 47) and level 75 at ch. 67. `[P]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-season-2-everything-to-know/)
- **Novel.** Early numbers from ch. 6 to 8: level 3 to level 7 inside the Shadow Dungeon; maximum HP 190, refilled on each level-up; Strength raised from 10 to 15 with five free stat points. The Association measures his mana at 46. Level 16 comes at ch. 18. `[O][W]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 7](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-7)

**Job and class**

- **Webtoon.** After the Job Change Quest Suho is "Irregular: The White Shadow", a forced merge of "The Heir of the Shadow" (Shadow Monarch powers), "Soul Striker" (his abilities from the epilogue) and a hidden custom class. The quest also grants the title "Champion of Adversary". `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/sung-suho-solo-leveling-ragnarok-jinwoo-comparison/)
- **Novel.** He is registered as an E-rank summoner. Beru, who is his father's creature and not his own summon, goes about with him calling himself a "nurse ant". His later job change yields the title Monarch of Transcendence; no class name is reported. `[O][P]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 8](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-8)
- **Version not stated.** The wiki lists Suho's class as Fighter. `[W]`
- The three job options map onto three play styles: summoner (Heir of the Shadow), duellist (Soul Striker) and crafter. This is the research's reading. `[P]`

**Skills, webtoon**

- Kandiaru's Blessing ("The Great Spellcaster"): received when the seal breaks. A health and longevity buff, immunity to disease and poison including "Stardust", and faster recovery. The wiki lists Longevity at ch. 2. `[P][W]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-suho-powers-explained/)
- Ruler's Authority: inborn telekinesis, unlocked again with the seal. It moves objects, weapons, people and himself, and can form an invisible striking hand. `[P]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-suho-powers-explained/)
- Tenacity: physical defence +20%, rising to 40% and then 60% at level 3 by the end of Season 1. `[P][W]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-suho-powers-explained/)
- Slashing Storm (Storm Slash on the wiki, ch. 9): active, 50 mana, a blade attack on several targets. `[P][W]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-suho-powers-explained/)
- Hand-to-Hand Combat (Martial Arts on the wiki, +33%, ch. 13): a boost to physical damage. `[P][W]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-suho-powers-explained/)
- Strength Booster (Increased Strength on the wiki, ch. 36): +20% strength, from Ammut's training. The wiki's skills subpage says +30%. `[P][W]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-suho-powers-explained/)

**Shadow powers and their limits**

- **Webtoon.** Shadow Extraction works as before, but the number of soldiers Suho can maintain is "quite limited", which ComicBook.com puts down to his lacking the Shadow Preservation skill. Whether he has that skill is disputed (section 5). `[P]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-suho-powers-explained/)
- **Novel.** When Suho first learns Shadow Extraction the skill window carries a counter, "Number of Extractable Shadows: 0 / 3", and he raises exactly three Shadow Goblins from goblin corpses (ch. 7). The chapter does not say how the limit grows. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 7](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-7)
- **Novel.** The skill costs no mana. Its chance of failure rises with the target's remaining ability points and with the time since death. Each soldier is shown as "Shadow Goblin, Level 1, Normal Grade", and Beru remarks that they are weaker than the goblins they came from. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 7](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-7)
- **Original, for comparison.** Jinwoo could store only 20 shadows at first and the limit rose with his mana. `[W]`
- **Both.** Suho's shadows are weaker than his father's (Iron and Greed are weaker under him) because of his shadow authority, intelligence stat and level, on Beru's word. `[W]`
- **Novel.** Shadow Extraction allows three attempts per target and could not take demons until Suho worked round it with Antares' power (ch. 242). `[W]`
- **Webtoon.** Shadow Manipulation or Transformation (Extraction level 2): shapes shadows into gauntlets, fuses shadows into chimera-like creatures and makes shadow-enhanced weapons. Jinwoo used a cruder form against Antares. During the fight with Lee Minsung Suho also learns to pass skills to shadow soldiers. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-chapter-63-sung-suho-shadow-explained/)
- **Novel.** The wiki calls the level-2 form "Shape Transformation" at ch. 157 on one page and "Form Change" at ch. 8 on another, and says Suho mastered it early. `[W]`
- **Webtoon.** Shadow Creation (the Job Change reward, ch. 47): converts a shadow into a System item with its own grade, stats, buffs and sometimes a unique skill. Its strength depends on the source shadow. Items can be stored and level up with use. They cannot be turned back into soldiers. Beru says not even Jinwoo could shape shadows this far. `[P][W]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-sequel-manhwa-jinwoo-suho-shadow-power/)
- **Webtoon.** Shadow Contract (a job skill): lets him name items of Heroic grade or higher for a 30% stat boost. A named item levels up and its wearer gradually gains the stats of the shadow it was made from. Once named it binds to its first wearer and can otherwise be equipped only by Suho. Example: "Baekgu", Heroic-grade gauntlets with the skill "Surge", given to Baek Yoonho in ch. 62 and levelling while he uses them. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-chapter-63-sung-suho-shadow-explained/)
- **Webtoon.** The result: Suho's companions are allies who grow with him, where Jinwoo's army was subservient, and Korea's S ranks are powered up by his items. Because shadow equipment levels up in someone else's hands and raises the wearer's stats, people without a System can now level, which Game Rant and CBR both say ends the premise of the title. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-over-leveling-up/)
- The shift, as the research reads it: from one player who hoards an army to one player who crafts and buffs for a party. Shadows become a resource spent on permanent, levelling gear that others can wear, and guild S ranks stay relevant. CBR and Screen Rant both treat this as the sequel's defining mechanical idea. `[P]`

### 2.6 The gods of other universes and their agents

**The Itarim**

- Four terms name the same class of being: "Absolute Being" (the original's single creator), "the Absolute Ones" (the official English synopsis for the group), "Itarim" (the name used in the story) and "Outer Gods" (the common English gloss). `[O][P]` [Tapas](https://tapas.io/series/solo-leveling-ragnarok/info)
- **Novel.** In the licensed English prologue they are supreme beings who create universes, indifferent to the death of one of their own and drawn to mana that has no owner. The rule is that whoever claims an ownerless power first becomes its master. The conflict that follows is named the "War of the Outer Gods". `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 0](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- **Both.** They are ancient beings who existed before time and made their own universes and species, then set their creations to fight endless wars for amusement. That they did so out of boredom is the article writer's reading. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-itarim-outer-gods-origin-explained/)
- **Both.** None is named and no count is given. The prologue gives neither, and Game Rant on November 2, 2026 says the same of the whole story: only the Absolute Being is identified. The wiki lists two members, the Absolute Being and one "Itarim of Dimension-1", with no domains. `[O][P][W]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-itarim-god-level-villains/)
- **Webtoon.** They are drawn as gigantic humanoids in black armour with crowned helmets, first seen in ch. 1. `[W]`
- **Novel.** At the end of the novel they are gods "with nebulae for skin", whose breath melts dimensional walls and who throw galaxies as weapons. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-jinwoo-powerscaling-series-ending/)
- **Both.** The Itarim are Chugong's idea and not only Daul's. CBR and Game Rant report that in The Art of Solo Leveling (August 2026) Chugong says they were part of his lore for the original novel and were never put into the story outright. Neither article quotes the passage. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-karma-azarea-itarim-theory/)
- One blog describes the early invasion waves as Titans and blue-winged, angel-like giant humanoid Apostles, with later waves appearing as monsters and alien creatures. No stronger source confirms that the Titans of the side stories are Itarim forces. `[F][low]` [AnimeFocusNetwork](https://www.animefocusnetwork.net/post/the-itarim-explained-solo-leveling-s-most-powerful-beings-beyond-monarchs-and-rulers)
- That several Itarim factions are invading Earth at once, in a kind of battle royale, is a fan theory and is labelled so by the article that raises it. `[F]` [AnimeFocusNetwork](https://www.animefocusnetwork.net/post/the-itarim-explained-solo-leveling-s-most-powerful-beings-beyond-monarchs-and-rulers)

### 2.7 How the sequel changes the cosmology

- **Novel.** Backstory in the licensed English prologue: the Absolute Being made this world long ago and split the primordial light and the primordial darkness into eight pieces each, the Fragments of Brilliant Light (the Rulers) and the Monarchs. It kept their war going for its own amusement, which was found out only after ages had passed. It died, as did all the Monarchs, and peace held until the Itarim came. Jinwoo is styled both Monarch of Shadows and "Greatest Fragment of Brilliant Light". `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 0](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- **Both.** The original's Absolute Being is one creator among many. After its death the other gods did not avenge it but turned to its world's masterless, unlimited mana. It stays dead; no source reports a revival. `[P][W]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-itarim-outer-gods-origin-explained/)
- **Novel.** Ch. 344, as Game Rant reports it: the Itarim came into being when only primordial darkness and light existed, each formed from fragments of both. The Itarim of this universe, the Absolute Being, divided that source, making Monarchs from the darkness and Rulers from the light. Ashborn was born a Ruler with a fragment of light and was later given a fragment of darkness along with the shadow power, so he held both, and the chapter is said to call him the "next Absolute Being". Jinwoo inherits that double nature after first becoming the strongest human through the System. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-jinwoo-power-secret-explained/)
- **Both.** Strength is framed as the amount of Primordial Light and Darkness a being holds. Jinwoo is exceptional because he has both. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-apostles-vs-monarchs-rulers-explained/)
- **Both.** Ashborn has a place in the backstory only. No source shows him acting in the present of the sequel. `[P][W]`
- **Both.** The Rulers have destroyed many Apostles but cannot replace their own losses, so the conflict is a war of attrition. The wiki gives the mechanism: their Heavenly Soldiers grew as fruit on the World Tree, which stopped bearing after the Rulers' rebellion (novel ch. 231 and prologue). `[P][W]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-apostles-vs-monarchs-rulers-explained/)
- **Novel.** In the prologue the Rulers are at the front with Jinwoo and pay homage to him. His army is described as millions strong, made up of angels of light as well as shadows. He and the shadow legion are the only forces that keep rising after death, without which the war would already be lost. The Rulers vote against sending help to Earth, and own up to having been chiefly responsible for the earlier invasions of Earth. No Ruler is named. `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 0](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- **Both.** Jinwoo is away from Earth, "in outer space", fighting the Itarim with his shadow army beside the Rulers. He can fight off several at once but cannot defeat them. He has protected Earth this way for years and left Suho as its guardian. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-suho-monarch-explained/)
- **Both.** Only two of Jinwoo's shadows are on Earth, Beru and Kaisel. Beru, handed to Suho, loses most of his power and appears as a floating head in the webtoon and a small chibi figure in the novel. `[W]`
- **Both.** Travel between dimensions has costs and rules. This is why Jinwoo cannot simply come home, why Beru is weak on Earth, and why the Apostle arrives in pieces. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-sequel-better-than-the-original-manhwa/)
- **Both.** The Cup of Reincarnation's reset did not touch the memories of Monarchs or Rulers. `[W]`
- **Webtoon.** Other universes exist as places with their own versions of people: the Job Change Quest pits Suho against other-universe selves, one of them the novel's Suho. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-manhwa-light-novel-multiverse/)
- **Webtoon.** CBR argues from ch. 45 to 46 that the franchise is a multiverse in which novel, webtoon, anime and games are all separate real worlds, and that there may be many Absolute Beings. This is the writer's interpretation; no creator statement is cited. `[F]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-manhwa-light-novel-multiverse/)
- **Novel.** Dead Monarchs persist in a Realm of Death, where they can be bargained with and given a new body. The wiki's "World of Nothingness" looks like the same place under another translation. `[P][W]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-suho-monarch-explained/)
- **Novel.** Succession: a dead Monarch's seat passes to a successor chosen by the Absolute Being or by that Monarch's priest, who must cut a head from the eight-headed serpent Nidhogg (ch. 248). Suho acts as "Priest of the Monarchs" and Guardian of the World Tree, and a Monarch's "Primordial Darkness" can be handed on. `[W]`
- **Novel.** A Monarch cannot pass on power until dead, which is why an enemy takes Suho's Monarch's Domain for a trick (ch. 169). `[O]` [Wuxiaworld ch. 169](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-169)
- **Novel.** The name Suho is glossed as "one who protects and guards the stars", and Antares wonders how far ahead Jinwoo saw. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-suho-name-explained/)

### 2.8 How the novel ends

- **Novel.** North Pole arc (ch. 350 to 374): Orloff's "Void Gate" project at a North Pole base (ch. 352). Thomas Andre smashes Moscow's barriers (ch. 354 on the wiki, ch. 357 in press coverage). An Itarim breaks through the sky over the North Pole and Beru confronts it (ch. 359). The eight-headed Shadow Nidhogg, launched by seven of the eight new Monarchs, is caught in Orloff's barriers. Orloff proves to be a puppet with Kandiaru hiding in his body. Humanity's souls are evacuated into the Tower through the game capsules, and the pyramids' beams empower Shadow Nidhogg, which kills Kandiaru, wounds the Itarim's eye and drags the fight into its own domain (ch. 362). `[W]`
- **Novel.** Ch. 374: Suho, with Beru, the successors' powers and the Shadow of the dead World Tree, kills one Itarim after a hard fight, takes in the divine power it leaves, and with it revives the World Tree and saves Earth. The wiki places the fight in "Dimension-1", with a sword named Ragnarok, while Jinho feeds him the players' experience. `[P][W]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-jinwoo-final-fate-revealed/)
- **Novel.** Judged fit to join the Outer Gods' war, Suho reaches the battlefield and finds Jinwoo at its centre with billions of shadow soldiers, Titans among them, fighting three Itarim at once without strain while galaxies thrown at him break up before they arrive. Suho's reinforcements open "Ragnarok", the war of the gods, and the text skips it. `[P][W]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-jinwoo-powerscaling-series-ending/)
- **Novel.** Jinwoo's army has grown from about ten million after the Monarchs' war to billions, still rising with every enemy felled. Antares notes that Jinwoo keeps growing without the System. CBR elsewhere says that by the last chapter Jinwoo is slaying multiple Itarim. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-jinwoo-powerscaling-series-ending/)
- **Novel.** Ch. 375 jumps a year. The family is home, Jinwoo and Antares settle their rivalry over a PC game, Beru and Igris suggest date ideas, and Suho goes to deal with a sudden threat so his parents can keep their anniversary. The wiki's version of the epilogue says Suho brought down that Itarim "and several other entities like it". `[P][W]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-author-sung-jinwoo-new-ending/)
- **Novel.** Daul's afterword, as reported: Suho's journey is over but Jinwoo's story goes on. Daul calls himself a "humble guardian" of the series, not its creator, and says the Shadow Monarch may "return again" in another form. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-jinwoo-final-fate-revealed/)
- **Novel.** A search snippet describes the ending as abrupt, with the expected "Ragnarok" confrontation skipped and replaced by a one-year jump. That fits the above: the duel with one Itarim is shown and the larger war is skipped. `[S]`

### 2.9 Standing with the original author, and reception

**What Chugong has said**

- Chugong's note at the novel's launch read "It's going to be a whole new world of Solo Leveling with writer Daul!" `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-canon-status-creator-comments/)
- CBR's report of October 7, 2025 gives his later remarks, from an interview with LitRes, a large Russian e-book and audiobook service, relayed in English by a fan account on X. As CBR prints them: "Ragnarok was a spin-off created for fanservice"; "I was curious to see how another author would write Solo Leveling"; "This story is part of the Solo Leveling universe, but it isn't a direct continuation of the main plot"; it is "more like one of many possible scenarios, similar to Marvel's parallel universes"; and he is "also considering the possibility of writing a sequel myself". `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-chugong-fanservice-spinoff/)
- He did not use the word "non-canon". CBR (February 5, 2026) argues the interview was misreported on that point. Russian outlets headline it as him placing Ragnarok outside canon. Game Rant's wording moves between "not canon to his story" (September 2026) and "largely canon" in an alternate universe (November 2026). The safest statement is the quotation: part of the universe, not a continuation of the main plot. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-canon-status-creator-comments/)
- Every English quotation rests on one fan account's translation. The interview itself, its date, its title and its original language were not found (section 6). `[P]` [Kanobu](https://kanobu.ru/news/sozdatel-podnyatie-urovnya-v-odinochku-nazval-spin-off-ragnarok-fanservisom-506881/)
- A later CBR piece adds that he said he had left some scenes of the original unexplained on purpose and would clear them up in a rewrite. His own sequel is unconfirmed as a project. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-creator-light-novel-sequel-ragnarok-manhwa-connection/)
- D&C's official art book of August 2026 files Ragnarok under the heading "spin-off", beside the anime and the game Arise. `[O]` [Yes24](https://www.yes24.com/product/goods/193851192)

**The case each way on "is Ragnarok canon?"**

- For: it is officially licensed, published by the same publisher (D&C Media) and made by the same studio (Redice), and built directly on the epilogue. Wikipedia says Chugong expressed support, and a March 2024 explainer calls it canon and says he supervised. `[P]` [Yahoo / ComingSoon](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/solo-leveling-ragnarok-plot-canon-133323306.html)
- Against: Chugong's own description above. `[P]`
- A middle position, one blogger's opinion: the Itarim concept is canon even if Ragnarok's plot is not. Chugong's art-book remark that the Itarim were part of his original lore (section 2.6) bears on this. `[F]` [AnimeFocusNetwork](https://www.animefocusnetwork.net/post/the-itarim-explained-solo-leveling-s-most-powerful-beings-beyond-monarchs-and-rulers)
- A working order of authority, which is the research's synthesis and not an official statement: (1) Chugong's novel including its side stories; (2) the webtoon and its side stories, where they differ in presentation only; (3) the Ragnarok novel and the Ragnarok webtoon, as two separate licensed alternate continuations that disagree with each other; (4) Netmarble game stories; (5) explicit what-ifs. `[P]`

**Reception of the side stories and the original ending**

- The finale divided readers: an anticlimactic first killing of Antares by the Rulers, the Cup of Reincarnation erasing consequences, the second Antares fight and the 27-year war left off the page, and the Rulers' ultimatum to Jinwoo seen as hypocritical. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ending-jinwoo-fate-explained/)
- A reviewer of Side Stories 1 found it fun to watch Jinwoo move through the world he remade, thought two epilogue volumes excessive in principle but working in practice, and wanted more of the detective material. `[P]` [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-14/)
- Anime UK News gives comic vol. 15 a score of 8 out of 10. `[P]` [Anime UK News](https://animeuknews.net/2026/04/solo-leveling-volume-15-review/)
- Reader reviews of vol. 15 average about 3.9 out of 5 and split between enjoying the family scenes and calling the volume a thinly disguised set-up for the sequel with an overpowered son. `[S]`

**Reception of the sequel**

- Positive: Screen Rant (December 2025) argues the sequel beats the original on worldbuilding, focus, use of the supporting cast, creative powers, pacing of growth and depth of its lead, and says it is "already quite a hit" with webtoon readers. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-sequel-better-than-the-original-manhwa/)
- Mixed: CBR argues Suho "does not belong in Solo Leveling" because he levels with allies, while conceding he may be the better character. Its commenters split over Suho against Jinwoo. `[P][F]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/sung-suho-solo-leveling-ragnarok-jinwoo-comparison/)
- Negative, on the novel: readers called late chapters bland and low in quality and singled out a "dream sequence". `[S]`
- The licensed English novel's listing shows reader reviews marked "Not recommended" that fault short chapters and writing below the original's. `[O][F]` [Wuxiaworld](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok)
- Game Rant (August 2025) says readers welcomed the novel's last chapter for bringing Jinwoo back, while its commenters complain that he is now too strong to be interesting. `[P][F]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-author-sung-jinwoo-new-ending/)
- A short, interrupted Season 2 and JIN's departure fuelled fears of cancellation in January 2026, and false rumours that the webtoon had ended circulated when the novel finished. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-season-2-manhwa-ending/)
- SensaCine sums up reception as mixed: a fun extension with callbacks and spectacle for some, lacking the original's weight for others. `[P]` [SensaCine México](https://www.sensacine.com.mx/noticias/noticia-1000170606/)
- Readings built on Chugong's remark and offered without evidence: CBR's theory that the novel's sudden ending cleared the way for Chugong's own sequel, and a Russian outlet's conclusion that Suho "will no longer be a relevant character". `[F]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-creator-light-novel-sequel-ragnarok-manhwa-connection/)

**Common confusions**

- Explainers often mix the sequel's novel and webtoon, for example quoting novel ch. 348 titles beside webtoon events. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-suho-power-compared-jinwoo-theory/)
- "Season 3" in headlines can mean the Ragnarok webtoon's third season (running since November 2026) or the anime's (not released). `[P]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-ragnarok-confirms-a-major-update-amid-hiatus/)
- Names vary by translator and outlet: Suho or Sooho, Tiel or Tielle, Gray or Grey, Sirka or Sarca, Snowfolk or Ice Elves. `[O][P]` [Yen Press vol. 15](https://yenpress.com/titles/9798400904660-solo-leveling-vol-15-comic)
- Headlines in November 2026 say the sequel changes or overwrites the original ending. What webtoon ch. 71 does is replay the last scene of the original side stories from Cha Hae-In's side and show that it was the start of a long separation. Nothing in the original is altered. Game Rant's line that Jinwoo's epilogue "is going to be overwritten" by the webtoon's eventual ending is a forecast. `[P]` [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-ending-retcon-jinwoo-cha-hae-in)
- Much English-language "lore" about the Itarim circulates through fan wikis and power-scaling blogs. Lists of named Itarim or of all Apostles should be checked against the licensed text before use. `[F]`

### 3.3 Games and what they add to the story

**Solo Leveling: Arise** (Netmarble, action RPG, released May 8, 2024)

- It adds original hunters and "Hunter Origin" one-shot backstories published on Netmarble's official site, 27 of them by February 2026. Examples: Meri Laine, a Finnish orphan brainwashed by a guild master called Zero and stopped by Germany's top S rank, Lennart Niermann; Lennart's own story (June 2025); Myro, a pianist offered revenge by the Demon King Baran in exchange for becoming his successor; and Antonie (May 2026). `[P]` [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-returns-with-a-new-story-ahead-of-season-3/)
- On January 15, 2026 Arise added Igris's human identity, Sian Halat, as a playable character, with a new one-shot comic chapter on his backstory. The name and outline come from Chugong: a knight called "Igris of the battlefield" who switched from silver to red armour because blood was hard to clean. Game Rant later says the Arise backstory itself is not canon. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-arise-sung-jinwoo-side-story-igris-sian-halat/)
- Arise has also published what-if one-shots, such as Sung Jinah being recruited by the White Tiger Guild. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-season-2-hwang-dongsoo-villain/)
- **Solo Leveling: Arise Overdrive** is a separate four-player co-op action RPG announced in June 2025, available on PC (Steam, Microsoft Store) with PS5 and Xbox versions planned. `[S]`

**Solo Leveling: Karma** (Netmarble; announced July 2025; PC, iOS and Android in the announcement)

- An isometric roguelite hack-and-slash telling the "untold 27-year war" in the Dimensional Gap with an "original version of Sung Jinwoo". Executive producer June Sung Kim describes it as Jinwoo's journey to rediscover his humanity. `[O][P]` [Netmarble press release](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/netmarble-unveils-major-solo-leveling-karma-reveals-at-anime-expo-2026)
- Status: not released. Netmarble's third-quarter 2026 results, published November 5, 2026, move it to the first half of 2027 for Korea and Japan, with other regions to follow. Game Rant's mid-2027 for the United States is its own guess. Earlier targets were "2026" and the second half of 2026, and an App Store listing showed December 31, 2026. Pre-registration opened in Korea and Japan around Tokyo Game Show in September 2026. `[P]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-karma-cancels-release-2026/)
- Events: a panel at Anime Expo on July 4, 2026 and a cinematic trailer at Tokyo Game Show in September 2026. Troy Baker is Antares's English voice. A-1 Pictures worked on the opening animation. `[O][P]` [Netmarble press release](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/netmarble-unveils-major-solo-leveling-karma-reveals-at-anime-expo-2026)
- Chugong's role: at Tokyo Game Show (September 17, 2026) business head Lee Jong-hyuk and producer Jin Sung-gun said the original author supervised the story, checked worldbuilding, lore and dialogue, and worked with the team from early planning. Screen Rant adds that Jinwoo's skills, the shadow army's mechanics and the Monarchs' relative power were verified with him. Whether the game is "canon" is worded differently by different sources (section 5). `[P]` [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/addressing-the-originals-gap-with-a-roguelite-39-tgs-qas-for-solo-leveling-karma)
- Enemies: Mutated Hunters, which the Monarchs create to kill Jinwoo and which have familiar faces; "Corrupted Hunters", altered forms of familiar hunters, among them Cha Hae-In and Go Gunhee; "aberrant" enemies that cannot be made into shadows. Antares keeps his memories from before the rewind and uses corrupted versions of Jinwoo's allies and family. `[O][P]` [Netmarble press release](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/netmarble-unveils-major-solo-leveling-karma-reveals-at-anime-expo-2026)
- New characters: a villain called Nemir, read by CBR as a demonic version of the Shadow Monarch from another timeline, which is the writer's reading. Azarea, shown in key art in July 2026 and named on August 28, 2026, officially described as a graceful, merciful figure who appears before Jinwoo during the war, sometimes as an ally and sometimes as someone to be rescued, and whose smile hides something. Outlets call her Goddess of Benevolence, Goddess of Deceit, or both. An unnamed "Messenger of Darkness" is said to have been sent by an Itarim to help Jinwoo. `[P]` [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-karma-azarea-itarim-theory/)
- That Azarea is an Itarim, or an Itarim's avatar or emissary, is the writers' theory and is not stated officially. `[F]` [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-karma-new-god-azarea/)
- The Itarim in the design: the original plan had Jinwoo draw power from "Emissaries of the Itarim", who were to make covenants with him. Netmarble confirmed in its September 2026 Q&A that these were dropped in favour of the Monarchs' own powers, because that fitted what fans expected. Game Rant lists the Emissaries' attributes as Radiant Light, Magic Bullet, Meteor, Flame, Tempest and Frost, and says they are "mostly removed". `[P]` [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/addressing-the-originals-gap-with-a-roguelite-39-tgs-qas-for-solo-leveling-karma)
- Systems: a quarter-view mobile roguelite action RPG. Dash, basic attack and special attack. Five weapon types at launch: daggers, greatswords, handguns, bows and gauntlets. Random skills, weapons and "Blessings" per run. "Blessing Cores" through which Jinwoo absorbs the Monarchs' powers. A "Recording System" that saves a finished build for challenge dungeons. A shadow summon command that briefly disables bosses. Playable hunters and shadows; Igris, Beru, Tank and Bellion are named. Asynchronous competition only. Passes considered in place of gacha. The team would not comment on PC or console. `[P]` [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26161/addressing-the-originals-gap-with-a-roguelite-39-tgs-qas-for-solo-leveling-karma)
- Karma is the only project positioned to make the 27-year war concrete. Until it ships, the war is a gap in every version of the story. This is the research's reading. `[P]`
