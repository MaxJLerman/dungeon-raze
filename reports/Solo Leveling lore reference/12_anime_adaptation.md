# 12. The anime adaptation

**Covers:** the Solo Leveling television anime by A-1 Pictures (25 episodes, 2024 to 2025): who made it, what each episode adapts, how it stages combat, colour, the System interface and sound, what it changed from the manhwa, its two localised versions, cast, reception and what comes next.

**Date:** 2026-11-16.

**Source markers:** `[O]` official text (official site, studio and distributor announcements, staff and producer interviews, soundtrack listings from the label or a store) · `[P]` press, recap or review site, including Wikipedia · `[S]` known only from a search-result summary · `[F]` fan site, forum, fan opinion, or critic opinion where it is opinion and not fact · `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki (not used in this chapter) · `[low]` added where the fact is low confidence or unconfirmed.

Episodes are numbered 1 to 25 across both seasons. Ep 13 is Season 2 Episode 1. Names are in the form the English release uses. Statements labelled as the notes' reading or inference are interpretation, not sourced fact, and carry no marker.

## At a glance

1. **Who made it.** A-1 Pictures, director Shunsuke Nakashige, action director Yoshihiro Kanno, music Hiroyuki Sawano. Season 1 ran 12 episodes from January to March 2024 (ch. 1 to 45). Season 2, "Arise from the Shadow", ran 13 from January to March 2025 (ch. 46 to about 108, with a closing scene from ch. 110). [P] [Wikipedia: episodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Solo_Leveling_episodes)
2. **Choreography grows with the hero.** He starts clumsy, bloody and grounded. After his reawakening the style moves away from realism into fast dagger work, dashes and midair clashes. The staff say the action must keep evolving. [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/maximizing-the-strength-of-anime-for-solo-leveling/)
3. **Top speed is drawn as coloured light.** In the Igris and Ant King fights the fastest exchanges show only blue and red trails, set off by slow motion and impact frames. [P] [Game Rant, S2E12](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-12-review/)
4. **Finishers change the rendering.** The killing blows on Igris (Ep 11) and Baran (Ep 20) switch to a sketch-like, white-on-black look, a choice made early in production. [O] [Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/anime/from-light-novel-to-anime-sensation-solo-leveling-producer-and-action-director-discuss-the-breakout-hit-242140.html)
5. **Three colours carry meaning.** Blue is Jin-woo, red is the enemy and danger, purple is shadow power from the class change in Ep 12 on. The System display follows him from blue to purple. Colour drains to grey on a death. [P] [Vida Extra](https://www.vidaextra.com/series/solo-leveling-cambia-cositas-secreto-durante-eventos-importantes-puede-que-siquiera-te-hayas-dado-cuenta)
6. **Drawn heroes, 3D crowds.** Named characters are hand drawn. Mass enemies and the shadow army are 3D, because the shadows' simple dark forms did not read in 2D. Close-ups of CG enemies are the main craft complaint. [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/maximizing-the-strength-of-anime-for-solo-leveling/)
7. **Bosses have visible phases, and the hero's tools are taken away.** Igris goes from sword to fists to a desperate stab. Kargalgan grows giant. The Ant King transforms and the music changes with him. Weapons bounce off, stealth is revealed, potions lock, the army is wiped out. This is the notes' reading of the fight accounts in section 3.
8. **Sound makes room for hits.** Music is thinned in crowd fights and heavy exchanges so impacts cut through, a new theme marks a boss's phase change, and Kanno planned the silences between blows in the Ant King fight. [P] [SensaCine México](https://www.sensacine.com.mx/noticias/noticia-1000147954/)
9. **The interface is a story device, and its detail is poorly documented.** System text is in English in every version, enemy names are coloured by threat (Igris in red), and windows arrive as offers, timers, shops and reward cards at turning points. How the windows open, their typeface and their alert sounds are not described anywhere and need direct viewing. [O] [Cocotame, part 1](https://cocotame.jp/en/series/110047/)
10. **Against the manhwa: longer fights, fewer jokes, more quiet scenes.** The comic faces are gone, major fights are lengthened or restaged, and side characters get added scenes. The Japanese broadcast is a second version with Japanese names and setting. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-director-original-cartoon-expressions-anime-why-cut/)
11. **Praised for craft, criticised for stakes.** It won Crunchyroll's Best Action two years running and Anime of the Year for Season 1. The recurring complaints are foregone outcomes, wins on raw stats, thin side characters and a rushed Season 2. [P] [Wikipedia: series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling_(TV_series))
12. **What comes next.** The film "Solo Leveling: Beyond the System" was announced on 3 July 2026 with no date. A third season has not been announced. The Netflix live-action series has a cast and no date. [O] [Official site news](https://sololeveling-anime.net/news/?id=70715)

## 3. Combat staging

### 3.1 Principles stated by staff

- Jin-woo's choreography is staged to improve with his power. He begins weak and clumsy. After his reawakening the style moves a little away from reality. The producers say the action must keep evolving. [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/maximizing-the-strength-of-anime-for-solo-leveling/)
- Kanno describes Jin-woo's style as brutal yet amateurish. He studied live-action fight footage for flow. [O] [Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/anime/from-light-novel-to-anime-sensation-solo-leveling-producer-and-action-director-discuss-the-breakout-hit-242140.html)
- The environment is part of the fight, most of all in city settings. Characters throw, break and smash into the background, which costs extra drawing. [O] [Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/anime/from-light-novel-to-anime-sensation-solo-leveling-producer-and-action-director-discuss-the-breakout-hit-242140.html)
- The long-legged, realistic character design made running and jumping hard to animate. The team's answer was to deform and stretch bodies and faces to carry momentum. [O] [AWN](https://www.awn.com/animationworld/faith-trust-and-drinking-friends-secrets-solo-levelings-success)
- Violence is meant to have consequences. Furuhashi's example: a sword cut to the arm removes the arm. The series opens on the hero's death. [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/maximizing-the-strength-of-anime-for-solo-leveling/)
- Several animators work on each scene, which gives fights varied texture. Key animators had room to improvise beyond the storyboard. [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/maximizing-the-strength-of-anime-for-solo-leveling/)
- Density: Ep 4 had four major fight scenes in 21 minutes, and almost every other episode was heavy with action. Animators were rotated constantly. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-anime-director-casting-backlash-controversy/)
- The working effects vocabulary, from the tags on Kanno's and Torii's clips: background animation, debris, impact frames, smears, lightning, sparks, smoke, wind, fabric, liquid, ice. [F] [Sakugabooru](https://www.sakugabooru.com/post?tags=solo_leveling)
- Season 2's shadow army scenes, with many characters fighting at once, needed substantial production time. [O] [Cocotame, part 2](https://cocotame.jp/en/series/110054/)
- Kanno's stated aims for the Ant King fight: it had to feel like a final boss. Every blow, pause and change of rhythm was planned from storyboard to edit. Silence between attacks was deliberate, to build tension. The aim was inevitability, not flash. The hardest part was keeping the action readable while making the world seem to shake, because too much speed or too many effects would lose the emotion. [P] [SensaCine México](https://www.sensacine.com.mx/noticias/noticia-1000147954/)
- Kanno names the Igris fight as his own favourite action sequence (CCXP México 2025). [S] [Vandal](https://vandal.elespanol.com/noticia/r36008/anime-el-director-de-solo-leveling-confirma-cual-es-la-mejor-escena-de-accion-del-anime-hay-muy-pocas-que-sean-asi)

The use of 2D and 3D in fights is in section 4.7.

### 3.2 Cartenon Temple statues, Ep 1 to 2 (ch. 1 to 10)

Credits: Ep 1 directed by Shunsuke Nakashige, storyboards by Nakashige and Hirotaka Tokuda. Ep 2 directed and storyboarded by Yūya Horiuchi, written by Fūka Ishii, chief animation director Chiaki Furuzumi. [P] [Wikipedia: Season 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling_season_1)

The sequence is a rules puzzle, not a duel. The recap spells the temple "Cortenon". [P] [Cinemaholic, Ep 2](https://thecinemaholic.com/solo-leveling-episode-2-recap)

1. Panic in the hidden chamber. Hunters who run are killed at once.
2. The first rule found is "do not move": the statues execute anything that moves. The leader, Song Chi-yul, has lost an arm and Jin-woo stops the bleeding.
3. Song recalls the tablet's three commandments: revere God, praise God, prove your faith.
4. A fast hunter sprints for the door. A statue's beam burns him away and leaves only his feet.
5. Revere: Jin-woo reasons that bowing to the enthroned statue stops the attacks. It works briefly. Then the enthroned statue smiles, stands and walks among them, killing, including one hunter who is bowing in front of it.
6. Praise: Jin-woo notices that some statues hold instruments and sends everyone to stand before one. Each plays when someone reaches it. He is the last to arrive and nearly dies. Once the music plays, the giant returns to its seat.
7. Prove your faith: an altar rises in the middle of the hall and everyone must stand at it. The exit opens but the statues advance, and they stop only while someone is looking at them.
8. One hunter bolts and leaves unharmed, which shows that people may leave so long as someone remains. The rest rush out.
9. Jin-woo stays so that Joo-hee and Song can leave. The statues kill him.
10. A screen appears offering him the chance to grow strong.

How it plays:

- Ep 2 stays almost wholly in the one room. Deaths are instant and varied (beams, bodies cut in half), there is blood everywhere, and more than half the party is gone within moments. [P] [Anime Corner, Ep 1 to 2](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-premiere-episodes-1-2-review-a-strong-but-slow-start/)
- The sound is ethereal singing over the heavy stomps of statues drawing closer. [P] [Anime Corner, Ep 1 to 2](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-premiere-episodes-1-2-review-a-strong-but-slow-start/)
- The viewer is shown the same clues as the characters. [P] [Anime Corner, Ep 1 to 2](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-premiere-episodes-1-2-review-a-strong-but-slow-start/)
- That review describes the last stage a little differently from the recap: the survivors must stand together in the middle and stare at the statues, and the door closes a little each time someone leaves. When too many flee, the badly wounded Jin-woo stays. His last thoughts are anger at those who ran, not noble sacrifice. [P] [Anime Corner, Ep 1 to 2](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-premiere-episodes-1-2-review-a-strong-but-slow-start/)
- Ep 2 uses first-person shots, which Ep 11 echoes. [P] [Game Rant, Ep 11](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-episode-11-review/)
- The enthroned statue's smile is singled out as the episode's signature image, a long-awaited manhwa panel. [S] [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/solo-leveling-episode-2-jin-woo-faces-statue-god-system-reveals)
- ANN's reviewer calls the violence brutal and says it shows the danger of dungeons visually. [F] [ANN, Ep 1 to 3](https://animenewsnetwork.com/review/solo-leveling/episodes-1-3/.206770)
- A critic of Ep 1 liked the chamber of statues and inscribed laws as real fantasy, and noted that the action distorts forms aggressively on a low drawing count, which gives speed and impact. [F] [Wrong Every Time](https://wrongeverytime.com/2024/02/16/solo-leveling-episode-1/)
- A critic who dislikes the later fights holds up Ep 2 as the show at its best, because Jin-woo survives by solving a problem. [F] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-fights-big-problem-no-stakes-coreography/)
- Later bosses call back to it. A reviewer links Kargalgan's growth to giant size to the temple, and his vision of his own death mirrors Jin-woo's on the altar. [F] [Game Rant, S2E6](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-6-review/)

### 3.4 Wolves, Kasaka and the city golem, Ep 4 (ch. 14 to 17)

Credits: directed by Tōru Hamazaki, written by Shingo Irie, storyboards by Ikurō Morimoto and Yoshihiro Kanno, chief animation director Hirotaka Tokuda. Craft notes:

- The snake is animated in drawn sections of its body, not as one whole length. The reviewer says the episode avoided CG for it. [P] [Anime Corner, Ep 4](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-4-review-true-strength/)

- Criticism: a reviewer who scored the episode 8 out of 10 says every threat in it, the boss included, is solved by overpowering it. That drains the fights of engagement and undercuts the game premise, since bosses are normally beaten by finding a weakness. ### 3.5 Giant spider and the Hwang Dong-suk party, Ep 6 (ch. 21 to 24)

Credits: directed by Takashi Sakuma, written by Norimitsu Kaihō, storyboards by Sakuma and Yoshihiro Kanno, chief animation director Tomoko Sudo. [P] [Wikipedia: Season 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling_season_1)

The spider. [P] [Game Rant, Ep 6](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-episode-6-review/); [P] [Anime Corner, Ep 6](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-6-the-payoff/)

1. Jin-woo, level 18, faces a C-rank giant spider with a metallic shell that spits corrosive acid. Neither side can land a decisive hit, so it becomes attrition.
2. He darts around it looking for a weak point. Point-of-view shots of his dashes alternate with shots of him winding up from above. Normal speed is mixed with slow motion so the fast passages feel faster.
3. His Fatigue stat climbs toward the critical level and the boss starts hitting him.
4. Cornered, he spends a stored daily-quest reward for a full recovery, which resets Fatigue.
5. He stabs an eye with Kasaka's Venom Fang, a C-rank weapon whose listed effects are an attack bonus, paralysis and a health drain of one per cent a second.
6. He stabs the paralysed spider's eyes again and again until it dies. During the fight his eyes darken and his voice drops to a rasp.

The killings. [P] [Game Rant, Ep 6](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-episode-6-review/); [P] [Anime Corner, Ep 6](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-6-the-payoff/)

1. Dong-suk's party returns, surprised to find both alive. They tell Jin-ho to kill Jin-woo and join them, or die for his gear. Jin-ho refuses.
2. They draw weapons. The mage's blast catches Jin-woo in the face and throws him, and they take him for dead. The screen blurs and the sound turns directional while his thoughts race.
3. An urgent quest tells him outright to kill the six attackers. He reasons that the System is keeping him alive for its own ends and decides to use it as it uses him.
4. To a solemn score, which the reviewer identifies as Sawano's "DARK ARIA", he kills each of Dong-suk's men with a single blow. After the first he reacts for a moment and the reaction is cut short. His eyes are hidden for almost the whole sequence.
5. Dong-suk tries to power up. Jin-woo pins him by the face, asks how a man can beg mercy from someone he has tried to kill three times, and takes his head off while he is still talking about his younger brother.
6. Aftermath in the rain: an Association official accepts a false account. The episode cuts to the brother, Dong-soo, abroad.

Reception:

- Game Rant called the spider the series' best fight to that point and Anime Corner its best animation to that point. [F] [Game Rant, Ep 6](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-episode-6-review/)
- ANN and Game Rant both say the cutaways to side characters are mistimed and blunt the killings. ANN calls the killings brutal and efficient, and the episode the metaphorical death of the good-natured boy. [F] [ANN, Ep 6](https://animenewsnetwork.com/review/solo-leveling/episode-6/.207513)

### 3.8 Igris, Ep 11 (ch. 37 to 41)

Credits: Kanno storyboarded and animated most of the sequence, working with Nakashige. Drawing digitally let him deform lines and watch over the whole shot. ### 3.16 Patterns across the fights

These are readings of the material above made in the research notes. They are not statements by the anime's staff, and they carry no marker of their own.

- Speed is shown by taking things away. At low speed the anime draws full bodies and damage to the surroundings. At top speed it removes the bodies and leaves colour-coded trails (blue for the hero, red for the enemy) and impact flashes.
- Finishers change the rendering style. Igris and Baran both end on monochrome, sketch-like frames.
- Boss fights have phases with a visible trigger. Igris: sword, then fists, then a desperate stab. Kargalgan: curses, then giant form. Ant King: brawl, then winged retreat and transformation with a change of music.
- The hero's tools are denied before they are rewarded. Weapons bounce off, stealth is revealed by mages, potions are locked, the escape item is knocked away, the army is wiped out, he is disarmed. The tension comes from disabled tools, because he rarely loses outright.
- Scale is sold from the hero's eye line: first-person shots, pans from behind him, storyboards that set his size against the boss, and bystanders bracing against shockwaves.
- Stealth reads as absence and ambush. The one stealth duel is an attack from nowhere and a crippling first strike, resolved by a debuff aura.
- Army fights are made of small vignettes. Each summon gets a readable fight of its own while the hero duels the boss in the middle.
- A long continuous shot is used as the showpiece of a big episode (the 26-second cut in Ep 18).

## 7. Changes from the source

The anime keeps the plot and key images of DUBU's manhwa. It adds connecting scenes for side characters, plants the Jeju arc from the first minute of Ep 1, removes the comic faces, and lengthens or restages most major fights. In Season 2 it also cuts comedy and some foreshadowing lines for pace.

### 7.1 Stated intent

- The team set out to follow the webtoon closely, above all DUBU's art and action. They judged that its compact chapters would feel repetitive if adapted one to one, so the anime adds the inner lives of other characters to vary the structure. [O] [Cocotame, part 1](https://cocotame.jp/en/series/110047/)
- Nakashige said the manhwa moves too fast to adapt as it stands, so he added material to carry across how entertaining the original was. D&C Media approved and supervised the changes. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-director-original-cartoon-expressions-anime-why-cut/)
- Author Chugong helped with several anime-only changes. [P] [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/anime/solo-leveling-director-breaks-silence-on-major-anime-change-2625150/)
- The anime also uses scenes from the novel that the manhwa lacks. Ep 1 alone has around ten changes. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-differences/)
- Lead actor Taito Ban suggested the monologue after Jin-woo's first kill in Ep 6 and queried lines that differed from the manhwa. [P] [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-anime-director-casting-backlash-controversy/)
- The guide that maps episodes to chapters lists no filler episodes. That label hides the anime-original scenes inside canon episodes. [P] [Anime Filler Guide](https://www.animefillerguide.com/?p=25)

### 7.5 Foreshadowing of Monarchs and Rulers

- The title "Shadow Monarch" is granted in Ep 12 without explanation. Around the middle of Season 2 a commentary piece noted that "Monarch" and "Rulers" had still not been introduced as terms. [S] [IMDb news index](https://m.imdb.com/news/ni65120946)
- Ep 14, the "glitch": Baruka tells Jin-woo that he does not seem human and that monsters hear a voice ordering them to kill humans. The picture and his speech distort and his previous sentence replays. An article reads this, from the source, as the System cutting him off. The episode does not state that. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-glitch-red-gate-jinwoo-secret)
- Ep 18: the manhwa's interrupted question from ch. 74, cut off by the System, is dropped. The anime puts in its place an original flash in which the dying Kargalgan remembers two figures. The article takes them to be the original Shadow Monarch and Igris. A verbal hint became a visual one. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-scene-cut)
- A second outlet says the Ep 18 flash teases the original Shadow Monarch earlier than the manhwa does. [S] [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/anime/solo-leveling-season-2-teased-major-sung-jin-woo-secret)
- Ep 25: in the last minutes a clean-up crew on Jeju meets two beings. One looks human and speaks the monsters' language. The other resembles the ice elves. They leave through gates. One outlet identifies them as the Frost Monarch and the Monarch of Fangs, the first Monarchs to appear in the anime. Another names them Sillad and Rakan. Both identify the figures from the source. Neither says the anime names them on screen. [P] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-season-2-ending-explained/)
- No source describes a Ruler shown or named in Seasons 1 and 2. This is a statement about the sources, not a viewing check. [P] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-finale-monarchs-explainer)
- Fan theory: the Season 2 ending sequence shows a seated figure with blue eyes, an empty throne, a pale fortress and a black heart, which some fans read as the original Shadow Monarch. [F] [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-2-ending-ashborn)

### 7.6 Tone and gore

- The producers say they did not hold back on gore, within Japanese broadcast limits, and want violence to carry consequences. [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/maximizing-the-strength-of-anime-for-solo-leveling/)
- Individual episodes go further than the manhwa: Ep 9 shows a decapitation on screen and Ep 18 adds gore. [P] [Game Rant, Ep 9](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-episode-9-review/)
- Game Rant's view of the series as a whole is the opposite: the manhwa is gorier, and the anime tones down both the darkest and the lightest moments. [S][F] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-things-manhwa-does-better-anime/)
- The pattern across all the changes: quiet human scenes are added around fights, jokes are removed from inside them, and fights run longer than their page count. Comparison articles mostly judge the fights improved and the emotional and comic beats weakened. [F] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-scenes-better-than-the-anime/)

### 8.3 What was softened

These points come from one opinion site's analysis of the Jeju arc. The descriptions of what is on screen are reported as fact there. The explanation that they stem from the history between Japan and Korea is the site's commentary. The only reasons the producers give for the two versions are audience familiarity and agreement with the rights holder. [F] [Anime Feminist](https://www.animefeminist.com/solo-levelings-adaptation-anti-korean-sentiment/)

- In the webtoon the chairman of the Japanese association states in ch. 100 that the goal is the annihilation of the Korean S-ranks. In the anime the betrayal is not planned from the start: the foreign side abandons its allies when the operation goes badly. [P] [Anime Feminist](https://www.animefeminist.com/solo-levelings-adaptation-anti-korean-sentiment/)
- Goto's scenes of private intimidation are cut, so he appears to have misjudged Jin-woo. [P] [Anime Feminist](https://www.animefeminist.com/solo-levelings-adaptation-anti-korean-sentiment/)
- National flags and Japanese decor are removed from the association offices, which are drawn as generic. [P] [Anime Feminist](https://www.animefeminist.com/solo-levelings-adaptation-anti-korean-sentiment/)
- A Brazilian outlet reported the Season 2 changes as "censorship" in the Japanese version, with the rival hunters belonging to DFN. [S] [Critical Hits](https://criticalhits.com.br/anime/solo-leveling-sofre-censura-na-segunda-temporada-do-anime/)
- A German outlet's headline says the series is less successful in Japan than elsewhere. No figures were seen. [S][low] [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/solo-leveling-is-not-as-successful-in-japan-as-in-the-rest-of-the-world-and-has-even-been-altered-in-many-places,1402933)

Because the picture is shared between versions, country-specific detail such as flags, signage and readable text had to come out of it. That is the practical reason the System windows are in English and the offices look generic. This is the notes' inference from the facts above, not a staff statement.

## 11. Status as of 2026-11-16

In short: a sequel film is officially in production with no date. A third television season is expected in the rights holder's investor material but has not been announced by the companies that make and distribute the anime. The live-action series has an announced cast and no date. Any specific year for any of the three is an outlet's estimate.

### 11.1 The film "Solo Leveling: Beyond the System"

Officially announced:

- Announced at Crunchyroll's Anime Expo panel on 3 July 2026 (4 July in Japan). English lead Aleks Le presented it. Attendees saw a teaser visual and a concept video. [P] [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-07-03/solo-leveling-series-gets-solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-anime-film/.239268)
- The official Japanese site's news item of 4 July 2026 says production of the theatrical film (劇場版 俺だけレベルアップな件 -Beyond the System-) has been decided, describes it as the sequel to Season 2 made as a film, shows a special trailer and a teaser visual, and promises further news. It names no staff, no release timing and no television season. [O] [Official site news](https://sololeveling-anime.net/news/?id=70715)
- It is an original theatrical film that continues the story after Season 2, in production at A-1 Pictures. [P] [Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/anime/crunchyroll-solo-leveling-movie-ghost-of-tsushima-anime-expo-264149.html)
- Credits shown on the concept trailer: Tao Tajima (director, director of photography, editor, colourist), Tomoko Sudo (character design, key animator), Atsushi Kaneko (animation producer), Tomotaka Yokoo (CG supervisor), with animation production and CG modelling by A-1 Pictures and a separate live-action production credit. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-new-anime-film-announced/)
- Co-producers: Crunchyroll, Aniplex, Netmarble, D&C Media and Kakao Piccoma. [P] [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-07-03/solo-leveling-series-gets-solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-anime-film/.239268)

Not announced:

- No release date or window. A page last updated on 9 July 2026 says the date and plot are withheld, and nothing newer than the July coverage was found on 16 November 2026. [P] [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-release-date-what-it-means-for-season-3-1.500602203)
- No arc, story, cast or composer is named. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-new-anime-film-announced/)

Only reported:

- It is Tajima's first time directing an anime series or film. [S] [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-feature-film-crunchyroll-in-production/)
- Years such as "2027" in some outlets are their own estimates from production timelines. [S] [CinemaView](https://cinemaview.in/en/blog/solo-leveling-beyond-the-system-movie)
- The notes infer from Tajima's combined credits (director, photography, editing, colour) that the film will lean further into the heavily graded, compositing-led look of section 4.1. That is a reading of the credits, not a stated plan.
- Some outlets framed the film as coming "instead of" Season 3. That is the outlet's reading, not a statement by the producers. [F] [Animehunch](https://animehunch.com/solo-leveling-gets-sequel-anime-film-instead-of-season-3/)

## 13. Not stated or still open

### 13.1 Needs direct viewing

The notes conclude that some visual and audio detail cannot be had from written sources at all. No review, recap or interview describes the following. Anyone who needs them must watch the episodes (Ep 2, 3 and 12 for the interface) or find an art book.

- How a System window opens, types on and closes.
- The windows' typeface and layout, and whether the status screen (stats, titles, skills) follows the manhwa's.
- The sound of a notification, a quest alert and a level-up.
- Whether the System has a voice.
- How a gate is drawn, and how a red gate differs on screen.
- How mana measurement is shown.
- How killing intent looks, beyond its effect as a stat reduction.
- How dodges are drawn (afterimages, blur). Sources say only "dash skill" and "ducks the slash".
- Eye glow beyond the three points in section 4.4.
- How the extractions of Tusk, the healer and Beru look.
- Which scene is last on screen in Ep 25.
- Whether a Ruler appears or is named anywhere in the background of Seasons 1 and 2.
- Whether any shot differs between the Japanese and international pictures, as opposed to audio and subtitles only.
- The official "shadow visuals" for Igris, Tank, Iron and Beru, and the official "making of" video for Ep 18. Both exist and neither was viewed.

### 13.2 Marked unverified in the notes

These come from general knowledge of the series, a single weak source or an unchecked summary. Do not rely on them without checking.

- The shadow "smoke" at extraction: hands rising from black ground and blue-black flame. No source describes it beyond "shadows burst out". Check against Ep 12.
- The interface is private to Jin-woo, floats in world space in front of him and is lit as a light source. No source describes this. It is only consistent with the first-person shots in Ep 2 and Ep 11.
- "Ruler's Hand" is a telekinesis skill. The reviews of Ep 11, 20 and 24 name it and none says what it does.
- A low-authority page claims "industrial" sounds were used for the System. Nothing corroborates it.
- Sawano is composing the film's score. One unnamed report says so. No official source names a composer.
- The film will start from around ch. 108 to 111. No source names the arc.
- Sony's mocopi motion capture was used "for most of the animation". The extent was not cross-checked.
- The readings "Ryu", "Geo" and "Wing" for the Japanese broadcast (section 12.4).

## 14. Touches in DungeonRaze

This chapter relates to the following parts of the game. It points only; it proposes nothing.

- Combat timing and feel: `MOVES`, `playerUpdate`, `hitEnemy` and `hurtPlayer` in `06_game.js`, with hit-stop (`G.hitstop`) and slow motion (`G.slowT`) in the frame loop. See sections 3.1 and 3.16.
- Finishers and executions: the `execute` state and posture `stagger`. See section 4.2 on finishing blows.
- Boss and enemy behaviour: `EDEF`, the `wind`, `act` and `rec` cases of `enemyUpdate`, `unb` attacks and their red warning. See the fight sequences in section 3.
- Effects: `R.sprite`, `R.ribbon`, `R.ring` and `R.blob` in `02_gl.js`, and the slash arc fields `tilt`, `dir` and `lance`. See sections 3.1, 4.2 and 4.3.
- Lighting and colour: `buildLights()` in `07_render.js`, `uToon`, `f.rim`, `f.eye`, crystal glow, and `ENV_HUB` and `ENV_CAVE`. See sections 4.2 and 4.6.
- Camera: lock target, camera collision and the resting camera angle. See the camera notes in sections 3.8, 3.11 and 3.16.
- HUD: name tags and their threat classes (`.tag.t0`, `.t1`, `.t2`), the overhead bars and the boss bar in `08_hud.js` and `index.tpl.html`. See section 5.
- Portals: the graded frames in `hubPortals()` and the fade in `startTrans`. See the gate note in section 4.6 and the open questions in section 13.1.
- Sound and music: any audio work, which must start from a click or key handler. See section 6.

Two project rules apply to everything above:

- Names, characters and signature moves from the series are deliberately not used in the game.
- The shadow-raising class was dropped by the owner. Do not reintroduce it unless asked.
