# Monarchs, Rulers and their armies

**Covers:** the nine Monarchs of Solo Leveling and the monster races they rule, the Rulers and their soldiers, the human vessels both sides use, Spiritual Body Manifestation, relative strength, and the dragon Kamish. Version tags: N = original web novel, M = manhwa/webtoon (numbers are webtoon chapters), A = anime, R = the sequel *Solo Leveling: Ragnarok*, G = the game *Solo Leveling: ARISE*.
**Date:** 2026-11-16.
**Source markers:** `[O]` official or licensed text; `[P]` press, explainer, recap or review site (Wikipedia is counted here); `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki; `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet; `[F]` fan site or fan opinion, which covers every strength ranking; `[low]` added when the research marked the fact low confidence. A fact with `[W]` and another marker has independent support; `[W]` alone does not, and chapter numbers carrying only `[W]` are the wiki's own and unchecked. Several press outlets probably repeat the wiki, so `[P]` beside `[W]` shows a claim is widely repeated, not that it was checked against the chapters. ## At a glance

1. There are nine Monarchs, each the king or queen of one monster race, made from darkness to destroy the world. The Rulers are eight winged beings made from light to protect it. Ashborn, the Shadow Monarch, began as the strongest Ruler. [W][O]
2. Power has two tiers. Antares (dragons) and Ashborn (shadows) stand far above the other seven. The story fixes that top and little else; any order for the seven lesser Monarchs is fan opinion. [W][P][F]
3. Each Monarch has a human-shaped form and a true form released by Spiritual Body Manifestation. Five true forms are described (Antares, Rakan, Querehsha, Sillad, Tarnak). None is described for Legia, Yogumunt or Baran. [W][P]
4. Transforming does not save anyone: every lesser Monarch who does it on the page dies in that fight or the next. This is a reading of the fight records, not a sourced statement. [W]
5. Monarchs take over a human vessel completely, get full power and die with it. Rulers lend power to a vessel who stays in control, get limited power and outlive it. Seven humans were Ruler vessels; four were killed. [W][P]
6. Each Monarch's race appears long before the Monarch does, as the ordinary monsters of earlier dungeons. Gate monsters are soldiers of the Monarchs' armies, captured in an old war and released by the Rulers through the gates. [W][P]
7. Antares is the final enemy and is not beaten in a fair fight the first time. Jinwoo freezes his army of ten million with a fear roar, pulls him to an island, survives a phase in which nothing hurts him, and the Rulers arrive and finish him. The solo win comes only in the rewritten timeline, 27 years later. [W][P]
8. The Rulers act as one faction with no individual names. Their foot soldiers are Heavenly Soldiers, spear-carrying winged warriors whose wing count shows rank (two, four, six for a Ruler), each at least as strong as an S-rank hunter. [W][O]
9. Kamish was one of Antares' dragons, released through the first S-rank gate. More than a hundred S-rank hunters went in and five lived. The raid itself is never shown in the novel or manhwa. His fang became the story's best daggers and his rune stone holds Dragon's Fear. [W][P]
10. The demonic spectres, Yogumunt's race, are the least documented: three named members, no description of looks or tactics, no known appearance as ordinary dungeon monsters. [W][O]
11. The anime's 25 episodes show only a copy of Baran and a brief first look at Rakan and Sillad. Every appearance detail in this chapter is the manhwa's design. *Ragnarok* is by a different writer and adds successors to the dead Monarchs. The game *ARISE* adds scenes of its own. [P][W]

## Origin and shared traits

- The Absolute Being made the Monarchs from darkness, born to destroy the world, and the Rulers from light to protect it. [W]
- The sequel's prologue, in its official English translation, says the world's primordial light and darkness were each split into eight pieces, giving the Fragments of Brilliant Light (the Rulers) and the Monarchs, and that when one dies the primordial power picks a replacement so the war goes on. This fits eight Rulers with Ashborn among them, and eight born Monarchs with Ashborn as the ninth. [O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- Each Monarch rules one monster race and seeks to wipe out humanity. [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Every Monarch can open gates to travel between worlds, and every Monarch except Jinwoo has Spiritual Body Manifestation. [W][P] [Game Rant: Monarch abilities](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-strongest-monarch-abilities-ranked/)
- Each Monarch is said to command millions of soldiers. No other source repeats this. [W]
- Monarchs and Rulers are mana-based beings without organic bodies, so neither can easily enter the human world without a suitable vessel. [W][P] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/how-many-rulers-monarchs-are-there-in-solo-leveling-what-are-they/)
- Because they are spiritual organisms, Monarchs cannot be turned into shadow soldiers, and their bodies dissolve into light or dust at death. [W][F] [QTA](https://quotetheanime.com/all-9-monarchs-gods-in-solo-leveling-ranked-explained/)
- Monarchs speak to each other by telepathy (listed for Antares and Tarnak). [W][P] [Game Rant: Strongest Monarchs](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-strongest-monarchs/)
- Sung Jinwoo is the second Shadow Monarch. [W]
- Reading of the research, not a sourced statement: the Monarchs split by temperament. Rakan, Sillad and Querehsha attack early and die first. Tarnak and Yogumunt refuse to move without Antares and fall in the invasion.

### Names and spellings

- The names Rakan, Sillad and Tarnak are standard in English coverage from 2025 on (Wikipedia, CBR, Screen Rant, ComicBook.com, Game Rant). Earlier pages differ: Fiction Horizon (November 2023, updated January 2024) lists the Beast, Frost and Iron Body Monarchs as unnamed, and Game Rant, Critical Hits and Dafunda (all January 2024) and Beebom call the Frost Monarch "Hochwan". The wiki lists "Hockwan" as an epithet of Sillad. No page says where or when each name was first given. [P][W] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/how-many-rulers-monarchs-are-there-in-solo-leveling-what-are-they/)
- A review of the licensed English manhwa volumes refers to Rakan as the "Fang Monarch" and "Beast Monarch", to Sillad as the "Frost Monarch", and to a "Fragment of Luminosity" hidden in "President Go" (Go Gunhee). [P] [The Outerhaven: vol. 11](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-11/)
- The Tappytoon English webtoon (ch. 172) renders Tarnak's title as "King of Monsters, Monarch of Steel", which the wiki calls a mistranslation. Antares is "King of Berserk Dragons" in some translations. [W]
- The official English *Ragnarok* novel renders demonic spectres as "demonic spirits" and the Rulers' troops as "silver soldiers". [O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 236 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-236)

## Monarch profiles

Appearance details are the wiki's descriptions of the manhwa designs. The novel is prose, so treat every look below as manhwa canon.

### Antares, Monarch of Destruction, King of Dragons

- **Standing:** strongest and oldest of the nine, leader of the Monarchs and the final antagonist. [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- **Debut:** N ch. 228, M ch. 163. Not yet animated. [W][P] [Game Rant: Every God](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-anime-every-god/)
- **Human-shaped form:** a tall, muscular, middle-aged man with long red hair, a short red beard, glowing red eyes and dark red claws, in reddish-silver high-collared armour and a red-brown tailcoat. [W]
- **True form:** an enormous red dragon with burning scales and several huge horns. [W][P] [Game Rant: Antares](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-most-dangerous-rival-season-3/)
- **Hybrid form:** a human-dragon hybrid with serpentine eyes, reptilian armour and a white helmet with four brown horns. Game Rant's captions name a "hybrid transformation". So he has three visual states: armoured man, horned hybrid, full dragon. [W][P] [Game Rant: Antares](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-most-dangerous-rival-season-3/)
- **Personality:** relaxed and confident, enjoys destruction and despises humans, but has a sense of honour and will bargain with someone strong enough to impress him. He cares for his own minions and dislikes dragons of mixed blood. [W]
- **Vessel:** none. No human could hold his power, so he stayed behind until conditions let him cross in his raw form. The wiki calls him the only Monarch who entered the human world without a vessel. [W][P] [Beebom: Antares](https://beebom.com/who-is-antares-solo-leveling/)
- **Army:** ten million dragons in the Canada invasion (M ch. 173). Each dragon can badly hurt a top hunter. He threatens to feed Jinwoo to his drakes. [W][P] [Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/comics-solo-leveling-ending-manhwa-manga-explained-jinwoo-monarchs)

**The revised timeline**

- After the reset he keeps his memories, does nothing to help the other Monarchs, and waits. Jinwoo reaches his palace 27 years later, after every other Monarch is dead. Antares opens with Spiritual Body Manifestation, tries to burn him, and is killed "without too much trouble" (M ch. 178). [W][P] [Beebom: Antares](https://beebom.com/who-is-antares-solo-leveling/)
- The cost to Jinwoo is a permanent burn on the left hand (M ch. 178, 181), which he hides under a black glove (M ch. 181). Screen Rant ties the burn to a moment when Igris was saved from the breath; the research flags that detail as garbled. [W][P] [Screen Rant: Antares](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-antares-kill-jinwoo-explainer/)
- The manhwa does not show how this second fight went, which CBR lists among the complaints about the ending. [P] [CBR: ending](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ending-jinwoo-fate-explained/)
- A "what if" exists: in side story 10 and a dream in M ch. 189, Jinwoo is Antares' vessel instead of Ashborn's and Antares takes over his body. [W]
- Sequel material on Antares (his revival, his fangs as a weapon, his successor) is under "Ragnarok" below.

### Ashborn, Monarch of Shadows, King of the Dead

- **Standing:** the first Shadow Monarch and a former Ruler, the "Greatest Fragment of Brilliant Light", strongest of the eight. Feared by Rulers and Monarchs alike. [W][P] [Screen Rant: Monarchs ranked](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-monarchs-ranked-weakest-strongest-list/)
- **Debut:** N ch. 217, M ch. 128. Status: alive but asleep. [W][P] [Game Rant: Every God](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-anime-every-god/)
- **Form as a Ruler:** a burly white knight with large white wings, long silver hair and glowing white eyes, in silver robes, light tan armour and a grey-blue cloak, with a white sword. He is the only individual Ruler whose look is described. [W]
- **Form as a Monarch:** a large armoured shadow warrior with flame-like glowing purple hair, purple eyes, two curved black horns and claws, in jet-black armour and a smoky black cloak, with a black sword. [W]
- **Personality:** values life, is kindly toward humans and tired of the war, yet loyal to the Absolute Being to a fault (M ch. 163). [W]

**History**

- When seven Rulers rebelled, Ashborn alone defended the Absolute Being. The others stalled him, beat him and left him to die. Dying, he triggered a dark power the Absolute Being had placed in him and became the Shadow Monarch, healed and stronger, but too late: the Absolute Being was already dead. [W][P] [Game Rant: Rulers](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-who-are-the-rulers/)
- The Absolute Being gave him that power as a means to destroy his own creations if they ever rebelled (M ch. 192). [W]
- After the Rulers captured Legia, Ashborn judged the Monarchs would lose and offered them his help, which they took reluctantly. He kept the war balanced, and both sides feared him. [W][P] [CBR: Monarchs explained](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-monarchs-ashborn-explained/)
- **Betrayal (M ch. 129):** on Antares' order, Rakan and Baran ambush him with their armies just after he has cut down hundreds of the Rulers' soldiers. He beats both with ease, Rakan flees, and he crushes Baran's skull. His shadow army is nearly wiped out. [W][P] [Game Rant: Baran](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-season-2-baran-white-flames-monarch-explained/)
- Straight after, the Rulers come down and ask his forgiveness. He demands they kill him and end the war; they keep pleading, and he refuses them and leaves to rebuild his army. His absence lets the Rulers win and drives the Monarchs into the cracks between worlds. [W][P] [CBR: Rulers](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- He later rejoins the Monarchs under Antares and follows them to the human world, wanting a place to belong. [W]

**The vessel and the handover**

- No human could hold his power, so the mage Kandiaru (the Architect) built the System to find and grow a vessel, in exchange for immortality. Ashborn picked Jinwoo because he kept surviving and kept risking his life for his family; a fan article adds that Jinwoo was chosen because he did not follow the Architect's rules. [W][P][F] [CBR: timeline](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-complete-timeline/)
- At level 100 Kandiaru calls Jinwoo back to the Double Dungeon for the takeover. Ashborn instead locks Kandiaru out of the System and gives Jinwoo his black heart, so Jinwoo keeps his own will and becomes a successor, not a possessed host (M ch. 130). [W][P] [CBR: Monarchs ranked](https://www.cbr.com/every-solo-leveling-monarch-ranked/)
- When Rakan kills Jinwoo, a hidden passive skill shown as "(Unknown)" triggers (M ch. 161). Ashborn holds him in an illusion of his old life, then appears, explains the System, the Rulers, the Monarchs and the war (M ch. 163), and offers a choice: stay in the dream forever or go back and fight. Jinwoo returns with the full power (M ch. 161 to 162). [W][P] [The Outerhaven: vol. 12](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-12/)
- Having passed on the rest of his power, Ashborn returns to the World of Eternal Slumber (M ch. 164). [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- The Rulers did not know which side Ashborn was on, so they sent Sung Il-Hwan to kill his future vessel. They accepted Jinwoo after watching him kill Legia. [W]
- Readings of the research: Jinwoo's whole skill list is Ashborn's kit released in stages by the System, with Ruler's Hands as the early weak form of Ruler's Authority. The System is a programme for growing a vessel, not a gift.

**His army**

- Make-up of the original army as Jinwoo inherits it: Shadow Heavenly Infantry, Shadow Steeds, Shadow Ancestral Dragons, Shadow Ice Elves, high orcs, goblins, giants, rock golems and lizards. With it Jinwoo commands more than 100,000 shadows. A manhwa-based fan page says Ashborn commanded "thousands". [W][F] [Otakus Notes](https://otakusnotes.com/power-of-known-monarchs-in-solo-leveling/)
- The army arrives through the biggest gate yet seen, which opens over Seoul (M ch. 153), and pledges itself to Jinwoo (M ch. 166). [W]
- **Bellion** is its one named officer: Grand Marshal grade and the strongest shadow. He was a Heavenly Soldier born from the World Tree, Ashborn's lieutenant, and the first shadow soldier ever made (M ch. 168). He has two pairs of black wings, jagged black armour, a one-horned helmet and a centipede-like blade used as both whip and sword. He beats Beru when they spar (M ch. 167). Debut N ch. 226, M ch. 166. [W]
- **Shadow grades**, lowest to highest: Normal (E to C hunter level), Elite (B), Knight (A, and can be given a name), Elite Knight (basic S rank), two upper tiers, then Grand Marshal. The novel calls the two upper tiers Commander and Marshal; the webtoon calls them General and Commander. [W]
- Jinwoo's army over time: 20 shadows at the start, over 100,000 once he inherits Ashborn's, and "over 10,000,000" after the revised-timeline war, by the wiki's Shadows page. Another wiki page says ten thousand at M ch. 179. [W]

- Named dragons: Antares, Kamish, Kaisellin, Granodeh (one reference to N ch. 248, no description) and, in the sequel, Ragnar. No dragon general or individual elder dragon is named anywhere. [W][P] [Game Rant: Antares](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-sung-jinwoo-most-dangerous-rival-season-3/)
- Dragon bone takes magical energy unusually well (N ch. 211). [W]
- The wiki's Shadows page lists "Shadow Ancestral Dragons" in Ashborn's original army and "Shadow Dragons (Antares's Army)" among Jinwoo's later soldiers. [W]
- **Nagas** are typed Dragon and ranked A: good at both combat and magic, with scales thick enough to use as armour. They keep to damp places and move in packs (at least thirty, by one character's hearsay). They appear in the Ahjin Guild's first A-rank gate (N ch. 148, M ch. 122). In the sequel Antares says nagas hid in deep water so as not to become prey for dragons (R novel ch. 202), which reads as prey and not as soldiers of his. [W]
- In the revised timeline Jinwoo hunts every dragon to extinction, sparing one egg. [W]

### Insects

- Types: poison-toothed giant desert centipedes, and ants. Ants are B rank; the Ant Queen and Ant King are S. [W]
- The ant swarm that reached China after Jeju was killed by Liu Zhigang with a single attack. [P][W] [Screen Rant: strongest by country](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-each-country-strongest-s-rank-hunter-list/)
- Querehsha's own troops are corpses run by parasites. [W][F] [QTA](https://quotetheanime.com/all-9-monarchs-gods-in-solo-leveling-ranked-explained/)

### Beasts

- Ice bears and yetis are B rank and share the Red Gate with the ice elves, so one dungeon can mix two Monarchs' races. The wiki's table types them as Beasts; the Ice Bears page's own infobox types them Snow Folk. [W]
- Rakan has an army of his own, used in the ambush on Ashborn. [W][F] [QTA](https://quotetheanime.com/all-9-monarchs-gods-in-solo-leveling-ranked-explained/)

### Snow Folk

- Ice elves have blue skin, white hair, white eyes and long ears, and human-level intelligence. They fight with bows and daggers and ice-infused arrows, share a Stealth skill, and ambush, picking off the weakest first. They are sadistic. [W]
- Sillad can also build monsters and golems out of ice. [W][F] [QTA](https://quotetheanime.com/all-9-monarchs-gods-in-solo-leveling-ranked-explained/)
- Sequel lore (R): only adult ice elves served in the Frost Monarch's army; children stayed in a village with elders until old enough to join. Ice elves are a mutation of high elves (R ch. 256). The children and a few elders, in a village near Echo Forest, survive Jinwoo's revised-timeline war. [W]

### Monstrous humanoids

- **High orcs** are red-skinned, each a match for an A-rank hunter, with strong magic resistance. Some can speak to humans. Their tattoos count kills. [W]
- **Kargalgan**, their shaman-chief (S rank), uses a barrier that seals the gate, telepathic control of a soldier, and gravity magic. He panics on recognising Jinwoo as a possible Monarch. [W]
- Green-skinned orcs under **Groctar** (A rank) attack a Seoul high school (M ch. 119). [W]
- Goblins are E rank and stone golems D. [W]
- The link to Tarnak is by type, not by a direct statement: all of these are typed Humanoid, the final battle lists "Tarnak & Humanoids" (M ch. 173), and Jinwoo's later army includes "Shadow Humanoid Monsters (Tarnak's Army)". The wiki's Goblins page lists Tarnak himself under "Known Goblins" with no citation. [W]

### Giants

- Man-eating, building-sized, grey-skinned, horned, armoured and sword-carrying, and fast for their size. In the manhwa one breaks Yuri Orloff's barrier in a single swing. [W]
- There were thirty. They had no boss, so they were set against each other and the winner led. Each was meant to be the boss of a top A-rank gate, and all but one are rated at the level of an S-rank monster. They flood out of the Tokyo S-Rank Gate. [W]
- One crossed to China and was killed by Liu Zhigang alone. Jinwoo holds the other 29 as Elite Knight grade shadows numbered 1 to 29 (debut N ch. 180, M ch. 135). [W][P] [Game Rant: strongest by country](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-strongest-s-rank-hunters-every-country-ranked/)
- In the revised timeline Jinwoo wipes the giants out. [W]
- Titans, ranked at least S, appear at M ch. 184; the notes give no type for them. [W]

### Demons

- Common demons are animal-like and can be as big as small buildings. Demon nobles are human-sized, intelligent and organised into at least twenty ranked clans (Radiru, Garsh, Pathos, Ricado, Hershel). Demons are usually horned with red eyes. [W]
- Most demons died under Baran in the first war. Kandiaru moved a large group into the Demon Castle with orders to guard it (M ch. 82). Mindless demons cannot recognise intelligent demons as their own (M ch. 83). [W]
- Sequel lore (R): demons grow stronger by consuming souls, and this trait, studied from Baran's corpse, was the basis of the System's levelling (R ch. 240). Demon souls return to the Demon World and are reborn, so they cannot be shadow-extracted (R novel ch. 232). [W]

### Demonic spectres

- The race has no description in any source: not its looks, not how it fights, and no appearance as ordinary dungeon monsters in the original story. Yogumunt's title is the only use of the term on CBR, Screen Rant and Game Rant. [W][P] [Game Rant: Every God](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-anime-every-god/)
- **Kandiaru**, the Architect, is the first named member. In the original story he builds the System and the instance dungeons, makes the Baran copy, and fights Jinwoo in the Double Dungeon (M ch. 125 to 130); his real body is said to survive (M ch. 131). His shape is a hooded angel statue with six wings that can shift into extra arms (eight arms in that state); his real face has whitish-blue skin. He can use Ruler's Authority. That he is a demonic spectre is revealed only in the sequel. [W][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 236 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-236)
- Jinwoo's army list includes "Shadow Demonic Spectres (Yogumunt's Army)", absorbed during his war with the Monarchs. [W]
- The sequel's named spectres and their skills are under "Ragnarok" below.

### How army soldiers became dungeon monsters

- The Monarchs' surviving soldiers were refugees looking for a foothold after losing the war. [W]
- Kamish and Legia were both captured in the first war and fitted with an essence stone. Legia's stone caused his bloodlust toward humans, and Kamish calmed down once told he was no longer under the Rulers' control. [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)
- The same story is told of an ordinary dungeon tribe: Kargalgan and his high orcs fought in the Monarchs' war, were taken prisoner, were brainwashed by the Rulers, and waited until they were dropped into the human world. [W]
- The Rulers opened the gates on purpose after rewinding time, so that over ten years mana and monsters would turn humans into hunters before the Monarchs arrived. Much of the monsters' hatred of humans is the Rulers' doing. [W][P] [CBR: Rulers](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- Reading of the research: the sources state the capture story for Kamish, Legia and Kargalgan's tribe. That all gate monsters are captured soldiers under essence-stone control is a generalisation from those cases.

## The Rulers and their soldiers

### The Rulers

- An ancient race of angelic warriors made from light, also called Fragments of Brilliant Light and Messengers of God. The sequel's official translation describes them as six-winged angels who turned on their maker and became god-slayers. Debut: N ch. 160, M ch. 129. [W][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- There were eight. Seven rebelled and killed the Absolute Being once they realised he wanted the war to go on for his entertainment. The eighth and strongest, Ashborn, stayed loyal and was beaten and left for dead. [W][P] [Game Rant: Rulers](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-who-are-the-rulers/)
- **Names:** none. CBR calls them "mostly unnamed beings". Only two are singled out, both by epithet: the Greatest Fragment of Brilliant Light (Ashborn) and the Brightest Fragment of Brilliant Light, the Rulers' current leader. CBR says the second phrase describes how he looks and is not a name. Fiction Horizon counts the eight as Ashborn, the Brightest Fragment and six unnamed. [P] [CBR: Rulers](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- **Look:** winged humanoids who resemble angels. The only individual described is Ashborn before his change (see his profile). Their size is not stated. [W][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- **Strength:** the seven together are strong enough that Ashborn admitted he could not have fought them off. Their mana pressure would crush ordinary humans, which is why they do not fight on Earth in person. [W][P] [CBR: Rulers](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- **Methods:** morally doubtful. The gates they opened killed many people. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- **In the final battle** they enter in raw form through the crack in the sky, with armies, and kill Antares with spears from all sides (M ch. 177). [W][P] [Screen Rant: ending](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-ending-explained/)
- **After the final battle** CBR says they thanked Jinwoo and then turned on him: the Brightest Fragment gave him a choice between leaving Earth and being sealed away, and they backed down under his stare. CBR lists this among the reasons fans dislike the ending. So the protectors end as a threat to the hero. [P] [CBR: ending](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-ending-jinwoo-fate-explained/)
- After Jinwoo's 27 years of war they send him a human envoy (M ch. 179). [W]
- Reading of the research: the Rulers function as a faction, not as individual characters. The story gives them one collective voice, a shared skill and a shared look, and their vessels are the faction's readable faces.

### Ruler's Authority

- Telekinesis that costs no mana, works over a wide radius and on the surroundings, and can be used for recoil movement and mid-air dodges. At full strength it works even on Monarch-level fighters (M ch. 146, 59). Also called "Capture". [W][P] [Game Rant: Rulers](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-who-are-the-rulers/)
- Jinwoo first gets a weaker form, Ruler's Hands, from Igris's rune stone. First shown M ch. 40, anime episode 12. [W]
- Users: Ashborn, Jinwoo, Kandiaru and all seven vessels (each marked as before the time reversal). In the sequel Suho has it from infancy. [W]

### Heavenly Soldiers

- The Rulers' foot soldiers: angelic warriors with white skin, wings, armour-like body structures and ornate helmets, armed with spears (N ch. 175). CBR describes silver armour and feathered wings; the sequel's official translation calls them "silver soldiers". [W][P][O] [CBR: Rulers](https://www.cbr.com/what-are-rulers-in-solo-leveling/)
- Rank shows in the wings, which reflect magic reserves: two for ordinary soldiers, four for high-ranking ones, six for the Rulers themselves (N ch. 227). [W][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- All can fly, and each is at least as strong as an S-rank hunter. [W]
- They grow as fruit on the World Tree, shaped by the Absolute Being (N ch. 229, M ch. 168). The tree bore more whenever the Monarchs gained an edge, to keep the war even. After the Rulers' rebellion it stopped fruiting, so the Rulers cannot replace their dead (R novel ch. 231 and prologue). [W][O][S] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- **Bellion** is the one named Heavenly Soldier (see Ashborn's army). [W]
- No source gives a head count. [W]

### Tools of the Rulers

- **Cup of Reincarnation:** made by the Absolute Being. It rewinds time ten years per use and wears out. Its last use sends everyone back ten years at Jinwoo's request (M ch. 177). [W][P] [Screen Rant: ending](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-ending-explained/)
- **Capture tools:** devices holding fragments of the Absolute Being's power, used to take Legia; mana-absorbing chains to hold him. [W][F] [QTA](https://quotetheanime.com/all-9-monarchs-gods-in-solo-leveling-ranked-explained/)
- **Essence stones:** implanted in captured soldiers such as Legia and Kamish to control them. [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)

### National Level Hunters

- The title is the rank above S, created for the five S-rank hunters who killed Kamish, each given authority equal to a nation's (M ch. 115; the title is first mentioned in M ch. 78). [W][P] [Screen Rant: National Level Hunters](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-national-s-rank-hunters-list/)
- **Antoine Martinez** is the fifth: a healer and the only one of the five who is not a Ruler's vessel. Reed rates him the greatest healer, and TechWiser credits him with a key part in the Kamish kill. Screen Rant and Game Rant call him French, France's first and strongest S-rank; Beebom, TechWiser and CBR give his nationality as unknown. The novel and manhwa do not name him. ## Spiritual Body Manifestation

The Rulers could descend raw only once the Jinwoo and Antares fight had distorted space enough (M ch. 176 to 177). ## Kamish

- **What he was:** one of Antares' dragons, captured by the Rulers during the first war and fitted with an essence stone. He confirms his service to Antares himself when revived (M ch. 143). [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)
- **Debut:** N ch. 135, M ch. 115. Rank S alive; General grade as a shadow (webtoon grade names). [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)
- **Appearance:** an enormous red western dragon with five grey horns, glowing yellow eyes, a light brown belly and a long whip-like tail. As a shadow: black scales, glowing purple horns and belly. [W]
- **Abilities:** Dragon's Fear, Dragon's Breath (fire), speech. [W]

**The raid**

- He was the boss of the first S-rank gate ever recorded, which opened on the American West Coast. The United States government offered large rewards to bring in the best hunters from around the world. [W][P][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 175 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-175)
- "Over 100" S-rank hunters went in, or "hundreds" on other pages. Nearly all died. Civilian deaths are given as over one million or as "millions". He is called humanity's greatest calamity. [W][P] [Game Rant: First S-rank Gate](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-first-s-rank-gate/)
- Five survived and killed him: Thomas Andre, Liu Zhigang, Christopher Reed, Siddharth Bachchan and the healer Antoine Martinez. [P][W] [CBR: National Level Hunters](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-national-level-hunters-class-country-facts/)
- **When:** about eight years before the story's present. The series gives no calendar dates at all. Sung Il-Hwan had vanished before both the ranking system and the raid, and the ranking system is about eight to nine years old. [P][W] [CBR: timeline](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-complete-timeline/)
- **How the kill went is not told.** The raid is never shown in the manhwa or the novels and is only spoken of. The fixed details are that Thomas Andre's Capture pulled Kamish out of the sky, and that Kamish bit him with its sharpest fang, which he kept. [P][W] [Screen Rant: Kamish raid](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-anime-adapt-missing-manhwa-scene-kamish-raid-op-ed/)
- **What it changed:** the five survivors gained authority to rival nations, guilds gained sway over national gate policy, and no other S-rank gate was cleared until Jinwoo's Jeju Island raid. Thomas Andre later remarks that the gate over Seoul is far bigger than the one Kamish came out of (M ch. 153). [P][W] [Game Rant: First S-rank Gate](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-the-first-s-rank-gate/)

**The remains**

- **Corpse:** the US government kept it in its death pose behind mana-reinforced glass, in a basement under the headquarters of its hunter bureau, which was built over it. Kamish's essence stone powers the building. [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)
- **Rune stone:** found inside the corpse and left unused because no National Level Hunter was a mage. It holds Dragon's Fear. Jinwoo breaks it to learn the skill, which the wiki dates to M ch. 173. [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)
- **Kamish's Wrath:** Thomas Andre had the fang made into two daggers because it was too short for a sword. He never used them; they lay for eight years in the basement of the Scavenger Guild's headquarters. He gave them to Jinwoo in thanks for sparing him and his guild (M ch. 152 to 153). [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)
- The daggers give +1500 attack, scale with the wielder's mana, and let the wielder change their weight. Jinwoo uses them to cut and kill the lesser Monarchs (M ch. 159). They cannot scratch Antares. [W]
- Their look differs by version: large pale orange blades with glowing red edges, one thicker than the other, in the manhwa; ice-white blades in the novel. [W]

**Shadow extraction (M ch. 142 to 143)**

- About eight years after the raid Jinwoo raises Kamish on the second try. Kamish moves to breathe fire on the humans present, stops when told he is free of the Rulers, bows, warns that four humans hold Ruler power, regrets he cannot serve, and crumbles because he has been dead too long. Jinwoo's shadow list carries him as held "temporarily". [W][P] [Beebom: Kamish](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-kamish-explained)
- Reading of the research: the four he warns about match the four vessels among the National Level Hunters, which fits him having fought them.

### Ragnarok (the sequel)

Everything in this subsection is sequel material. *Ragnarok* is by a different writer, and by the reported Chugong interview its lore may not bind any sequel he writes. The only primary text reached was the official English translation's prologue and short chapter teasers; the rest is the wiki's. The web novel finished in July 2025 at 357 chapters by Wikipedia, and its webtoon has run since 31 July 2024. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)

**Setting**

- The Rulers, with their silver soldiers and Jinwoo's shadows, hold a front line against the Outer Gods (the Itarim). The army there is counted in the millions, and Jinwoo and his legion are called the only immortals left. The Rulers oppose sending forces back to Earth for fear of thinning the line. [O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok prologue](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-0)
- Kandiaru escaped the Shadow Monarch's extermination of his kind, fled to the outer universes, entered an Itarim's service and told it that his home universe had no god left, which started the war. [W]
- Jinwoo's army is put at "billions" by the epilogue (R novel ch. 374). The Apostles average out at Heavenly Soldier level. By high school Suho could be beaten one-on-one only by Jinwoo. [W]

**Succession**

- A dead Monarch can be succeeded by a magic beast chosen by the Absolute Being or by the dead Monarch's priest. The candidate must cut a head from Nidhogg, an eight-headed serpent (R ch. 248). The official teaser of R novel ch. 338 describes the ceremony: each successor picks out the head that holds their darkness and dives into its jaws, to be consumed or to come out a Monarch. [W][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 338 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-338)

**Antares and the dragons in the sequel**

- Suho meets Antares in the realm where dead Monarchs rest. Jinwoo strikes a deal to bring him back if he trains Suho (R ch. 197), then revives him inside Ragnar (Ragna), Kamish's child, a body he can hold only while the young dragon has enough mana. Game Rant calls this a trick on Antares. [W][P] [Game Rant: Suho](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-ragnarok-sung-suho-monarch-explained/)
- Antares beats Suho easily at their first meeting and calls him unworthy (R novel ch. 211). Suho's dragon skills come from "Draconic Trials": Storm of Black Flames after the first, at level 99 with Antares' Dragon Heart, and Breath of Destruction from fighting Baran in the third (R novel ch. 348). [W]
- **Ragnar** is the last dragon, Kamish's son and Suho's priest. The official teaser of R novel ch. 176 has Beru react to "Kamish's Egg" and describe Kamish as a red dragon who was one of the Rulers' war captives, with Suho wondering whether the hatchling will succeed the King of Dragons. [W][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 176 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-176)
- **Antares' Fangs:** six large daggers with curved dark-purple blades and black, gold-trimmed hilts shaped like dragon heads (webtoon design, M ch. 183). During the revised-timeline war one blade of Kamish's Wrath broke on Antares' skin and the pair wore out over nearly three decades, so Jinwoo had Antares' fangs made into daggers (R novel ch. 244). They rank above Kamish's Wrath as the strongest weapon in the world. [W]
- Suho gets round the rule that demon souls cannot be extracted by using Antares' power (R novel ch. 242). [W]

**Kandiaru and the demonic spectres in the sequel**

- Kandiaru began as a scholar of sorcery who wanted to outdo Yogumunt (R novel ch. 240). The official teasers call him "the Grand Spellcaster" and the great sorcerer who built the instance dungeons, and say he made Ammut through experiments with shadow power; Ammut's immortality comes from a contract with him that holds only inside the pyramid, now a training ground. His authority is called Echo of Despair. [W][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 321 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-321)
- **Harmakan** (S rank, R novel ch. 148) can open gates and has a skill called Mirage, used to project illusions of black pyramids over cities. The official translation calls him a "demonic spirit" and has him stop a fight and laugh instead of explaining himself; a search snippet calls him "Supreme Chief of the demonic spirits". [W][O][S] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 236 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-236)
- **Xavier** is tied to the Busan Haeundae Beach dungeon (R novel ch. 166). [W]
- The same arc has a "Death debuff", an energy filling a sea that drains the life of living beings; the source does not tie it to the spectres by name. [O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 321 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-321)

**Demons in the sequel**

- After the reset the demons were moved to the World of Eternal Slumber and later went back to the Demon World, where every noble except Esil died in an uprising of lower demons stirred up by an Apostle. [W]

**The vessels and hunters in the sequel**

- **Thomas Andre** regains his memories and part of his National Level power in the Glacier Dungeon and ends as Monarch of Conquest. [W]
- **Christopher Reed** is found possessed by an Apostle and Thomas kills him, for which Thomas is accused of murder. [W]
- **Siddharth Bachchan** keeps his memories of the erased timeline, believes he was chosen, sides with the Apostles of the Itarim and plots to hand Jinwoo over. He uses dragon eggs to turn India's hunters into dragonewts, and goes to clear a naga dungeon at Loktak Lake (R novel ch. 201). The official teaser of R novel ch. 213 shows him as a high priest of the Church of the Outer Gods and "Mutant No. 1": a stable blend of man and dragon with wings, large interlocking horns, shimmering scales and a glowing blue star fragment on his forehead, with a follower of the Itarim fused into his body. CBR says the hybrid form holds Suho even for a while, and Screen Rant says he ends as Suho's shadow soldier Sita. [W][P][O] [Wuxiaworld: Ragnarok ch. 213 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/solo-leveling-ragnarok/slrk-chapter-213)
- **Sung Il-Hwan** regains his memories when Jinwoo leaves, is not swayed by the Outer Gods as other former National Level Hunters are, and kills Hwang Dongsoo a second time. [W]
- **Liu Zhigang** is a six-star hunter in the sequel by Screen Rant. He revives the National Level title after seeing Suho in a dungeon break in India; it becomes official after Thomas beats the Apostle of Conquest (R novel ch. 342), and Suho is counted the first holder (R novel ch. 221). Counting Suho, six hunters hold the title across the franchise. [W][P] [Screen Rant: National Level Hunters](https://screenrant.com/solo-leveling-anime-manhwa-national-s-rank-hunters-list/)
- **Go Gunhee** is said to have paid for the Hunters Association himself (R novel ch. 228). [W]
