# Bestiary: monsters and bosses

**Covers:** the monsters ("magic beasts") and dungeon bosses of Solo Leveling: every type by race, every boss in story order, how they rank, what they leave behind, how they behave, how they are drawn and animated, and where the novel, manhwa, anime, sequel and games differ.

**Date:** 2026-11-16.

**Source markers:** `[O]` official or licensed text, including staff interviews. `[P]` press, explainer, recap or review site (Wikipedia is counted here). `[W]` the Solo Leveling Fandom wiki; chapter references carrying only `[W]` are the wiki's own and unchecked. `[S]` known only from a search-result snippet. `[F]` fan site, forum or fan opinion. `[low]` added where the fact is low confidence. A fact with `[W]` and `[P]` or `[O]` has independent support; one with `[W]` alone does not. The fan site daddyjim.com was found to be rewritten from the wiki, so `[F]` plus `[W]` is one source, not two. The wiki material was obtained through the wiki's API after the site refused automated reads, and the project owner chose on 2026-11-16 to keep it, labelled.

## At a glance

1. **One ladder.** Hunters, gates and monsters share six ranks, E to S, with a large gap between steps. The wiki sorts monsters into nine races, each under a Monarch. [W][S]
2. **One gimmick and one counter per type.** Ground-and-ceiling pincer (Razan and Briga), armour that needs a piercing dagger (knights), summoners who must die first (mages), arrows from group stealth (ice elves), an alpha-led stampede (ice bears), surge then withdraw to a stronghold (high orcs), a bodyguard room before the boss (royal guard ants). Section 1. [W][P]
3. **Three tiers in practice.** Ordinary monsters, an elite guard, a boss. Ranks mix inside one gate: C-rank jackals run with A-rank high orcs under an S-rank shaman. The series does not name the tiers. Section 3.3. [W][S]
4. **The boss outranks its gate, with no stated rule.** An E-rank dungeon holds D-rank beasts and a C-rank boss; a top-tier A-rank gate holds an S-rank boss. "Hidden bosses" sit far above even that. Section 3.2. [W][P]
5. **Boss fights share a shape.** The boss out-stats the hero and breaks or shrugs off his weapon, a timed or triggered power-up follows, and the win comes through a weak point at the eye, neck or face, a positional trick or an ally. Many are two-stage rooms: guards then boss, mount then rider, army then caster. Section 2.19.
6. **Three bosses are rules encounters,** not damage races: the Statue of God (obey three commandments), Legia (a pact under which neither side can lie) and the Architect (a ten-minute timer). Sections 2.1, 2.16, 2.17. [W][P]
7. **Name colour shows threat relative to the player:** white for weaker, orange for about equal, red for stronger. Section 3.5. [W][F]
8. **Monsters attack because they are ordered to.** A voice, traced to essence stones the Rulers planted, tells them to kill humans. Intelligent ones talk, taunt, bargain and flee; hive and animal types fight until the leader dies and then scatter. Section 5. [W][P]
9. **Timers.** A gate breaks after seven uncleared days and closes one hour after its boss dies. Raids kill the boss last and loot first. Sections 3.1 and 4.3. [W][P]
10. **What is left behind.** Essence stones (white for E and D, red for A, purple for S), blue mana crystals mined from the walls, corpses for crafting and rare rune stones that teach one skill. Named gear drops come only to the hero through the System. Section 4. [S][F][P]
11. **The look.** One flat body colour, glowing eyes, one accent or silhouette feature, and size given by comparison with buildings. Section 6.1. [W][F]
12. **Most cave-like material:** the Insects Dungeon (tunnels, holes in the walls, a crystal-filled boss chamber with a sleeping spider) and the Jeju ant nest (egg-covered floor, guard chamber, queen). Sections 1.4, 2.4, 2.13. [W][P]
13. **How firm this is.** Ranks, looks and chapter numbers for ordinary types rest mostly on the wiki alone. Boss fights in the anime have independent press support.

## 1. Monster types by race

### 1.1 How the source sorts them

- Monsters are called magic beasts, also the Denizens of Chaos. They live inside dungeons and come from other dimensions through gates. [W][S]
- The wiki sorts them into nine species groups: Beasts, Demons, Dragons, Giants, Humanoids, Insects, Snow Folk, Undead and Demonic Spectres. Its table adds "Titans" for one late entry (rated "at least S", cited to chapter 184, nothing else known). [W][S]
- Each group belongs to a Monarch. The ones named in the notes: Rakan for Beasts, Querehsha for Insects, Antares for Dragons, Legia for Giants, Ashborn for Undead. [W][S]
- The Beasts, Insects and Undead groups each span S-rank down to D-rank. Dragons as a species are "at least S". [W][S]
- Magic beasts hold mana, so only beings who wield mana, such as hunters, can kill them. [S]
- As a class they can forge their own gear, can be as intelligent as humans, and some speak human languages. [W]
- "Demonic Spectres" is a race label, not a common enemy. In the original story it has one member, the Architect (section 2.16). [F][W]
- The tables below follow the wiki's type column where it gives one. Where another source files a monster under a different race, the row says so and section 8 lists the conflict.

Notes on humanoids:

- "Lizards" on the wiki are not monsters. The word means hunters who commit crimes inside dungeons (wiki cites manhwa ch. 20 and 21). [W]
- CBR calls green orcs the weaker kin of high orcs. Groctar despises high orcs. [P][F] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-high-orcs-explained/)
- Groctar nearly kills Jinwoo's sister Jinah. He works out by scent that the shadow orcs guarding her are tied to her, senses Jinwoo approaching before he can strike, and orders a retreat. The same arc holds a crisis at her high school; the sources read do not say outright that the orcs are that crisis. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Groctar)
- High-orc tactics in the anime: draw the raid deeper, seal the only exit with a barrier the hunters cannot break (curses on it injure those who try), mock and toy with the party, then offer its leader a "fighting chance" against the shaman. The jackals that run with them wear collars, so they are trained. High orcs communicate and plan, and the shaman speaks through one of them. [P][S] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-high-orcs-explained/)
- The wiki says neon teal tattoos and a necklace of human skulls set the shaman Kargalgan apart from other high orcs. The anime staff say the background orcs share one CG model and were given varied tattoo patterns and colours to hide the repetition, so ordinary high orcs are tattooed on screen. [W][O] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/how-the-solo-leveling-anime-was-made-behind-the-scenes-at-a-1-pictures-ahead-of-the-season-2-finale/)
- As shadows, the job-change knights equal C-rank hunters and rise to B-rank level as elites. The Job Change dungeon forbids potions. [W]
- Stone Golem fight detail: the rest of the party were E-rank, nearly out of mana and stalling for higher-rank help. One review words the golem's defence as a "barrier" and credits the hunter Lee Joohee with the kill; that is probably loose phrasing for the same event. [W][P] [Otaku Orbit](https://otakuorbit.com/solo-leveling-season-1-episode-4-review/)
- CBR places the Earth Giant above Metus in its strength list. That ordering is CBR's own. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-strongest-magic-beasts/)

Notes on insects:

- Insects Dungeon setting: corridors blocked with spider silk, holes the insects travel through, mana crystals on the walls, and a cave-in set off by the betrayers in the party. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-every-dungeon-jinwoo-enters/)

- Jeju ants by the numbers: an official finding that it takes an A-rank hunter or better to kill one; a single ant that reached Japan drew 108 hunters and killed 294 people before it died; about 4,000 ants were alive after the raid and Beru was set to hunt them down. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-jeju-island-arc-explained/)
- A soldier ant lives about one year, which is why the colony was expected to die out soon after the Queen. Flight came after the island's food ran out, and the ants turned to cannibalism. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-what-happened-on-jeju-island/)
- The Queen is the last of the first generation. The King belongs to no generation because he carries traits of all three. [W]
- Fiction Horizon rates a single red ant about C and a white ant A with "excellent defence". Both are its own estimates. The anime prologue shows red ants and one giant white ant. [P][W] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-magic-beasts-monster-classes-from-the-anime-ranked/)
- The fourth raid's plan was to draw the swarm away so few defenders were left round the Queen. The anime adds extra fighting against the guards to show each hunter's skill. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-11-review-an-absolute-massacre/)

### 2.1 The Cartenon Temple statues and the Statue of God

- **Where and when:** a hidden second dungeon, the Cartenon Temple, inside a D-rank gate. Anime ep. 1 and 2; manhwa ch. 1 to 10. The rematch is in the Double Dungeon Arc, manhwa ch. 123 to 131. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-2)
- **Rank:** S for both the statue and the temple on the wiki. In the story the hunter Song Chi-Yul estimates A if not S because it kills instantly. CBR says the gate read as D-rank because the Double Dungeon's mana did not leak out to be measured. [W][S][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-every-dungeon-jinwoo-enters/)
- **Look:** a chamber lit by blue flames and ringed by ancient statues with inscriptions. The god statue is a colossal stone copy of the Absolute Being with glowing red eyes on black sclera and a cruel smile it shares with its maker. The rules are carved on a stone epitaph in a runic script. There are no ordinary monsters in the temple. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-1)
- **Statue types:** door guardians that cut down anyone forcing the exit; weapon holders (a sword statue splits a hunter in two, a spear statue impales Jinwoo, a shield statue cuts off his lower right leg); instrument players with trumpet, violin and drum, each beside a dormant partner. [F][W] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-2)
- **Attacks:** the god statue's eyes glow, then fire a heat beam that erases a running hunter down to his feet. It strides forward and stomps. The lesser statues move only while nobody is watching them, like a children's game. [F][W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-statue-of-god-explained/)
- **The fight, first visit.** It is a rules fight in three phases set by three commandments. [W][S][F][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-statue-of-god-explained/)
  1. The healer notices the god statue's eyes move. The doors shut and the statues attack; the armed statues along the walls cut down anyone who makes for the doors (wiki cites manhwa ch. 4).
  2. Worship. Heat vision kills everyone standing above a set height. It stops once every survivor is low enough, and the statue grins.
  3. Praise. Each survivor must stand in front of an instrument statue, which then plays. Weapon statues kill whoever stands in front of them, slow movers are crushed underfoot, and a hunter who tries praying is crushed. Jinwoo loses his lower leg to the shield statue before he reaches an instrument (ch. 7). When the rule is met the god statue sits back down and raises a central altar.
  4. Prove faith. On the altar an outer ring of blue flames goes out at one a minute as a timer, and an inner ring of red flames shows how many hunters are still on it (ch. 9). The doors open. The warrior statues wake with blue-white light in their eyes and markings and advance whenever they are not watched. Each person who runs puts out a flame and narrows the doors.
  5. Jinwoo stays, is impaled on the altar and receives the System's offer.
- **How it was beaten:** it was not, the first time. Six of thirteen hunters walk out in the anime, six of seventeen in the manhwa. The party's strongest member, the C-rank Song Chi-Yul, loses an arm. [F][W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-most-dangerous-raids/)
- **The rematch** (wiki cites manhwa ch. 125), after Jinwoo passes level 100 and is called back: [W][S]
  1. He dodges the heat vision and closes in with the Quicksilver skill.
  2. He dodges a stomp and a second heat vision.
  3. The Mutilation skill leaves only scratches on the face, even with the Demon King's Daggers.
  4. He drops the daggers and punches the face until the statue topples.
- **Drop:** none. Surviving the first visit grants the System itself (Player status). [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling_season_1)

### 2.2 Blue Venom-Fanged Kasaka

- **Where and when:** boss of the Hapjeong Subway Station instant dungeon. Instant Dungeon Arc; anime ep. 4; the wiki dates its death to manhwa ch. 16. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-4)
- **Rank:** C-rank boss of an E-rank instant dungeon whose ordinary beasts are D-rank. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/strongest-solo-leveling-dungeon-boss-ranking/)
- **Look:** a cobra about the size of a house with blue scales, glowing amber eyes, a long black forked tongue and venomous fangs. It lives in a flooded sewer or swamp area and is styled the swamp's ruler. [W][S][P] [Otakusnotes](https://otakusnotes.com/strongest-dungeons-in-solo-leveling/)
- **Attacks:** bites and an attempt to swallow Jinwoo whole; a tail sweep; body thrashing; a thrown train carriage; a charge that drives him through a wall. Scales too thick for his sword. It fights on land and in water. No source describes a venom spit or any attack built on the venom beyond the bite. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-most-dangerous-raids/)
- **The fight** (anime ep. 4): [W][F][P] [Otaku Orbit](https://otakuorbit.com/solo-leveling-season-1-episode-4-review/)
  1. Jinwoo survives a rushing wall of water on the way in.
  2. A sword blow does no damage; the sword shatters on the scales in one strike.
  3. The tail knocks him into water, where he barely dodges the head.
  4. He dodges a train carriage thrown at him, then is driven through a wall.
  5. He gives up on weapons, locks both arms round its neck, holds on by the scales as it thrashes and strangles it, relying on his strength stat.
- **Drops:** Kasaka's Venom Fang, a C-rank dagger with attack +25 and paralyse and bleed effects. Kasaka's venom gland, an A-rarity elixir and a "rare drop": drinking it gives 20% physical damage reduction and costs 35 strength. The item is "Casaka's Venom Gland" in the novel and "Rasaka's Venom" in the manhwa. The corpse and the dungeon dissolve afterwards. The arc also gives the Wolf Slayer title (manhwa ch. 13) and a Hearthstone. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/arc/Instant-Dungeon-Arc)

### 2.4 Giant Arachnid Buryura

- **Where and when:** boss of the C-rank Insects Dungeon. Dungeon & Lizards Arc; anime ep. 5 and 6; debut novel ch. 19, manhwa ch. 21. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-6)
- **Rank:** C. Jinho says soloing a C-rank boss normally takes at least B-rank power. [W][S]
- **Look:** a massive eight-legged spider with a grey carapace on the abdomen, black chitin armour with grey joints and several glowing red eyes. Typed "Insects – Arachnid". It is found asleep in a chamber glittering with mana crystals. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-5)
- **Attacks:** blows from the legs; corrosive acid spit; an exoskeleton that a C-rank dagger cannot pierce; one unnamed attack that takes Jinwoo by surprise. [W][P] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-magic-beasts-monster-classes-from-the-anime-ranked/)

  1. The venom dagger cannot pierce the shell, and speed alone does not help.
  2. Jinwoo dodges leg blows until his stamina runs out.
  3. He takes one hit and is nearly killed, then uses a stored Full Recovery reward.
  4. He leaps and drives the dagger into an eye; the paralysis and drain effects finish it.
- **Drops:** none. The wiki's item tables list nothing from it. The prize is the boss room's mana crystals, worth about a billion won to the hunters who tried to steal them, plus several levels. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-6)

### 2.5 Cerberus, gatekeeper of the Demon Castle

- **Where and when:** outside the gate of the S-rank Demon Castle (CBR counts it as floor 1). Dungeon & Prisoners Arc; anime ep. 7; debut novel ch. 28, manhwa ch. 26; the fight is manhwa ch. 27. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-7)
- **Rank:** A. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-most-dangerous-raids/)
- **Look:** an enormous black three-headed dog with six glowing red eyes and jutting fangs. The left and middle heads have fiery orange manes and brown horns; the right head wears a grey mask ringed with brown fur. The setting is a burning castle landscape. [W][P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-7-jinwoos-strongest-foe-yet/)
- **Signature ability:** Rage doubles its strength for up to three minutes at a time. It is too strong to be affected by the paralysis of Kasaka's Venom Fang, and its hide turns daggers. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-strongest-magic-beasts/)
- **The fight** (wiki's page for manhwa ch. 27): [W][F][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-most-clever-strategies-sung-jin-woo/)
  1. Jinwoo's whole kit barely scratches it, and it dominates for minutes.
  2. It bites off his arm and Rage triggers.
  3. He uses Full Recovery and a healing potion and still cannot gain ground.
  4. He drinks Kasaka's venom gland for its toughness buff, relying on his immunity to its drawback.
  5. As Rage runs out he starts to hurt it.
  6. Caught in its jaws on his last health, he stabs until it drops. In the anime's telling he stabs its large eyes over and over while it holds him, so the eyes are the weak point.
- **Drops:** Castle Door Key (A-rarity, opens the castle's door). Warden's Necklace ("Gatekeeper's Necklace" in the novel), A-rarity, agility +20 and sense +20. Cerberus' Fang ("Molar" in the novel), a material priced at 150,000 gold. The Elixir of Life recipe is among the rewards. [W][S]

### 2.6 Blood-Red Commander Igris

- **Where and when:** the throne room of the System's Job Change Quest Dungeon, entered at level 40 (the level comes from a search snippet). Job Change Arc; anime ep. 11 and 12. The wiki's table gives debut novel ch. 48, manhwa ch. 38; its arc page dates the win over Igris to ch. 40. [W][F][S][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-strongest-magic-beasts/)
- **Rank:** A. Jinwoo had judged he could not yet beat an A-rank. [W][P] [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/solo-leveling-job-change-who-is-red-knight-igris)
- **What he is:** one of Ashborn's lieutenants, set to test Jinwoo. This version was far weaker than the one in Ashborn's army. [W]
- **Look:** a tall, lean knight in blood-red plate with four horns (one broken), a Y-shaped visor slit over white eyes, a long red plume and a tattered crimson cape. He carries a bronze greatsword as tall as he is and daggers at the belt, and stands guard before an empty throne. His overhead name is as red as his armour. [W][S][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Blood-Red-Commander-Igris)
- **Dungeon rules:** no leaving until cleared, no potions, no store, no recovery on level-up. [S][W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-11)
- **Abilities:** strength, speed and durability above Jinwoo's; sword mastery Jinwoo cannot match; armour that turns dagger cuts; "Ruler's Hand", a weak telekinesis he uses to recall his sword from a distance. [W][S]
- **The fight** (anime ep. 11): [F][P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-11-a-legendary-battle/)
  1. Igris attacks at once. Jinwoo is overwhelmed by speed he did not expect; pillars break, and he is thrown and pummelled.
  2. Blade against blade: greatsword against quick daggers, with arcs of red and blue electricity where they meet.
  3. Jinwoo sheathes his useless dagger and fights bare-handed. Igris sets his own weapon aside, which the press reads as a knightly trait.
  4. In the fist fight Igris lands a kick the reviewer calls a "Manji kick", slams Jinwoo into the floor with a mid-air combo, hits him again on the rebound and hurls him onto the throne. A kick to the throat from Jinwoo enrages him.
  5. As the finishing blade comes down Jinwoo catches it in his palms, stabs a dagger into the left eye, then stabs repeatedly at the exposed flesh under the neck until Igris yields and takes a final blow to the head.
- **How he was beaten:** by the weak points at the eye and the neck gap. Sources differ on which one is the killing blow (section 8). All agree Jinwoo calls the win luck. [P][W] [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/review/solo-leveling/episode-11/.209230)
- **After the boss:** killing Igris only starts the real quest, a survival wave of knights and mages that lasts about four and a half hours (section 1.2). [W][P] [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/solo-leveling-job-change-who-is-red-knight-igris)
- **Drops:** Red Knight's Helmet, S-rarity armour with physical damage reduction +15%, strength +20 and stamina +20. A rune stone holding Ruler's Hand ("Ruler's Hands" in the novel, "Dominator's Touch" in the manhwa). A teleport stone ("Hearthstone" in the manhwa), a quest-only item that cannot be stored. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/arc/Job-Change-Arc)
- **Not a drop:** Knight Killer, the B-rank dagger Jinwoo uses on the knights (attack +75, +25% damage against armoured targets), is bought in the System's store at manhwa ch. 43. [W]
- **Shadow:** extracted on the third attempt as Jinwoo's first named knight, also called Igris. [S]

### 2.7 Baruka, leader of the Ice Elves

- **Where and when:** the Red Gate. Red Gate Arc; anime ep. 13 and 14 (season 2 ep. 1 and 2); debut novel ch. 63, manhwa ch. 52. [W][P] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-you-need-to-know-about-baruka/)
- **Rank:** S-rank boss. The gate was first measured as C-rank and then turned red; the wiki's Red Gate Incident page rates the dungeon A. One press piece estimates him at "around low S". [W][S][P] [AnimeHunch](https://animehunch.com/why-could-not-jinwoo-extract-barukas-shadow-in-solo-leveling/)
- **Look:** snow-white hair, blue skin, blank white eyes, black fingernails, a purple leaf or flower pattern across the forehead, a cruel grin, a fur-lined white and blue parka. [W][P] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-you-need-to-know-about-baruka/)
- **Abilities:** paired daggers with swordwork equal to Jinwoo and Igris together; Stealth; a style close to Jinwoo's own, so he reads Jinwoo's moves; he shrugs off shadow-mage fire and resists the poison of Kasaka's Venom Fang; he can twist away from a near-lethal strike while outnumbered. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-most-clever-strategies-sung-jin-woo/)
- **Mind and speech:** vain, sadistic and clever. He judges within minutes that Jinwoo is not fully human. He speaks only a monster language, which Jinwoo alone understands through the System. [W][S][P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/ice-elf-dungeon-boss-baruka-appears-in-solo-leveling-season-2-episode-2-preview/)
- **The fight** (anime ep. 14): [W][F][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-most-clever-strategies-sung-jin-woo/)
  1. His elves kill all of the A-rank hunter Kim Chul's squad except Kim.
  2. He offers to spare Jinwoo in exchange for the rest of the party.

[W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Kandiaru)

### 2.19 Patterns across the boss fights

These are readings of the entries above, not statements from the source.

- Most fights share a shape: the boss out-stats the hero and breaks or ignores his weapon; a timed or triggered power-up follows (Rage, stacked hymns, recall of the swarm, a quest timer); the win comes through a weak point at the eye, neck or face, a positional trick, or an ally arriving mid-fight; the drop is a named weapon or key.
- Several are two-stage rooms: bodyguards then boss (Royal Guards, Queen), boss then survival wave (Igris), mount then rider (Kaisellin, Baran), puppets then puppeteer (statues, Architect), minion army then caster (Metus).
- Three are rules encounters more than damage races: the Statue of God (three commandments), Legia (a pact that forbids lies) and the Architect (a ten-minute timer).
- The caster bosses, Kargalgan and Metus, are fragile once reached. The problem they set is getting through a shield or a horde.

## 3. How monsters rank and scale

### 3.1 The ladder

- One six-step ladder, E, D, C, B, A, S, is used for hunters, dungeons and magic beasts alike, with a large gap in power between steps. [W][S]
- About ten C-rank hunters are needed to match one B-rank. A-rank hunters usually lead raids. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-raids-explained/)
- Every source read uses the plain E to S ladder. The one "S+" seen is a search-snippet attribution to a single listicle (section 8). [P][S] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-gates-explained/)
- A gate's rank follows the amount of magical energy it gives off, and higher-ranked gates are physically larger: an S-rank gate "looks like a hurricane", a weak one is a doorway. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-gates-explained/)
- Measurement can fail. The Double Dungeon read as D-rank because its mana did not leak out. The Red Gate read as C-rank before it turned. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-every-dungeon-jinwoo-enters/)
- A gate breaks if it is not cleared within seven days, and closes for good one hour after its boss dies. [P][W] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-raids-explained/)
- Red Gates: the lowest difficulty one can present is B-rank. The gate seals until the boss dies, the party dies or a break happens. One day inside equals one hour outside. [S]

### 3.2 Boss rank against gate rank

No source states a rule that a boss sits a set number of ranks above its gate. What sources do say: [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-season-3-spotlight-strongest-magic-beasts-series/)

- Monster strength "usually" tracks the dungeon's rank.
- Every dungeon has a boss, its strongest beast, markedly stronger than the rest, which must be killed for the dungeon to clear.
- A typical gate holds weaker beasts led by one strong boss.
- Top-tier A-rank and S-rank gates can hold outliers harder than the rank suggests.

Worked examples from the notes:

- **Hidden bosses:** the Ant King (Jeju gate), Legia (Tokyo gate) and Kandiaru (Cartenon Temple) are each called the hidden boss of their dungeon and sit far above its visible boss. [S][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/arc/Double-Dungeon-Arc)

### 3.4 Growth

- **A colony:** left alone for four years, the Jeju ants multiplied through three generations, each more advanced. When food ran low they grew wings to find more. The Association had assumed they could neither fly nor swim. [W][S][P] [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/solo-leveling-jeju-island-arc-and-story-so-far/)
- **An individual, by eating:** the Ant King's Gluttony takes skills and knowledge from prey. Vulcan ate more of his own kin than any other noble (a novel detail, from the fan site). [W][S][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Vulcan)
- The wiki says all demons grow stronger by consuming souls, and that this trait, studied from Baran's corpse, was the model for the System's levelling. It cites the sequel for this (section 7). [W]

### 3.5 Threat relative to the player

- The System colours a monster's overhead name by its threat against Jinwoo's own level: white for weaker, orange for about equal, red for stronger. It is relative to the player, not absolute. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-4)
- In anime ep. 4 the wolves' names are red when first met; in the manhwa they are white. Igris's name and Vulcan's name show red. [W][S][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-most-clever-strategies-sung-jin-woo/)

### 3.6 What rank does to shadow extraction

- Extraction fails on targets stronger than the extractor and on things long dead. [P] [AnimeHunch](https://animehunch.com/why-could-not-jinwoo-extract-barukas-shadow-in-solo-leveling/)
- Cases: Baruka, three failures put down to his greater strength. Kargalgan resisted at first. [W][low] Kamish took two tries because he had been dead so long. Legia has no flesh body. Demons cannot be raised because their souls hold contaminated mana (the wiki cites the sequel for the reason; the press states the rule for the anime). [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-every-dungeon-jinwoo-enters/)
- Shadow grades give a second scale for the same monsters: job-change knights equal C-rank hunters (B as elites) and start at Normal grade; the alpha bear's shadow Tank started at Elite grade and at Knight grade is about A-rank; each shadow giant at Elite Knight grade is about S-rank; Jima at General grade is an advanced S-rank. [W]

## 4. What monsters leave behind

### 4.1 Essence stones

- Small glowing stones embedded in the bodies of magic beasts. They are hunters' main income. Larger beasts give larger stones. [S][W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Essence-Stones)
- Colours by rank: [S][F][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)

| Rank | Colour |
|---|---|
| E | white |
| D | white |
| C | not given by any source |
| B | not given by any source |
| A | red |
| S | purple |
| The very strongest (the Ant King, Kamish) | pure black, per the fan site only |

- Rule of thumb: lighter stones are commoner and darker ones worth more. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Shape: like a triangular diamond, some star-like, which is how hunters tell them from mana crystals. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Value: E-rank stones are nearly worthless because they flood the market; S-rank stones are "valued in the millions". Prices track the rank of the monster and dungeon and stay fairly stable. No source gives a figure per rank or a size per rank. [F][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-raids-explained/)
- Uses: forged into weapons and gear for tough monsters, and long-lived clean power for ordinary equipment. The US hunter bureau ran its headquarters on one for close to ten years. [F][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Tax example: Jinwoo, as an independent, faced 40% tax selling 49 C-rank stones. Guild members pay much less. [S]
- The Rulers planted essence stones in captured beings; Legia and Kamish are the cases given. [W]
- After the Jeju raid, essence stones were retrieved from the island, and Japan's association offered its government's share in exchange for hiring Jinwoo. [W]

### 4.2 Mana crystals and glow stones

- Mana crystals are blue minerals embedded in dungeon walls. They hold less mana than essence stones and are worth far less, but are a steady income. [S][F][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-essence-stones-explained/)
- Electricity does not work inside dungeons, so low-rank mining teams, usually D-rank hunters, dig them out by hand with pickaxes. [S][F][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-raids-explained/)
- Boss rooms can be rich in them. Buryura's chamber held about a billion won of crystals, which motivated the betrayal. In the anime the betrayers plan to let the spider feed, wait for it to sleep and come back through a side tunnel; in the manhwa they blast their way back in. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-6)
- Jinho gathers crystals while Jinwoo fights in the C-rank gates. A guild mining team enters after the strike squad to dig crystals out before the gate closes. [W][low]
- Fiction Horizon ranks four kinds of stone by worth: glow stones (common, only good for light, usually missing from insect dungeons because insects dislike light), mana crystals, rune stones, then essence stones. [P] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-stones-ranked-by-worth/)

### 4.3 Corpses and body parts

- Corpses are material. Guild collecting teams haul bodies out by hand once the strike team has cleared everything but the boss. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/group/Collecting-Team)
- Raid procedure: kill the ordinary monsters fast and leave the corpses intact, spend most of the dungeon's timer gathering and mining, and kill the boss at the last possible moment. [P][F] [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/review/solo-leveling-season-2-arise-from-the-shadow/episode-16/.220493)
- Known uses: naga scales are steel-like and are made into armour, shields and other protective gear (wiki cites novel ch. 149). Kamish's fang became the daggers Kamish's Wrath; his body was kept whole by the US government. Nothing is given for other species. [W]
- Body-part items in the System's tables: Raikan's Fang, junk, 20 gold. Cerberus' Fang, a material, 150,000 gold. Low-Grade Demon's Horn and Vulcan's Horn, materials for high-grade magic that fetch a high price. World Tree's Fragment, an ingredient for magic items of the highest quality. Kasaka's Venom Fang is itself a weapon. [W]
- System instant dungeons dissolve with their corpses. The Job Change dungeon is destroyed automatically when its quest ends. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-4)
- Meat: the wiki calls magic beast meat unappetising and toxic, with effects from indigestion up to organ damage; the toxin can be neutralised but the taste cannot (cited to the sequel). Against that, Jinwoo's party eats Ice Bear meat at camp in the anime, where the manhwa has them eat bread. [W]

### 4.4 Rune stones

- Rune stones drop from slain beasts. Each holds one skill of the creature, learned by breaking the stone. [W][S][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Rune-Stones)
- Known stones: Ruler's Hand from Igris; Dragon's Fear from Kamish; Stealth from a human target, the hunter Kang Taeshik, whom the System treated as a quest target; Shadow Exchange, which was a quest reward for beating Baran and not his own skill. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Rune-Stones)
- In the manhwa the Stealth stone is found in Kang's corpse; in the anime the System hands it over as a reward. [W]
- A body can hold only so many skills (the wiki cites the sequel for the cap). Buy and sell prices "vary". No source gives drop rates or class restrictions. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/item/Rune-Stones)

## 5. Instinct, orders, speech and behaviour

### 5.1 The compulsion to kill humans

- The vast majority of magic beasts are hostile to humans because of the Rulers' manipulations. [W][S]
- Jinwoo learns from the ice elves that every magic beast is driven to kill humans, with himself the exception (anime ep. 14). Baruka says beasts hear a voice ordering it, and that the voice says nothing about Jinwoo. [W][P] [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/review/solo-leveling-season-2-arise-from-the-shadow/episodes-13-14/.219982)
- Kargalgan remembers a voice repeatedly ordering him to kill humans when he first woke in the dungeon (manhwa ch. 74). The wiki says he and his orcs were captured in the Monarchs' war, "brainwashed by the Rulers" and left waiting for the day of release. [W][S]
- The mechanism is the essence stone. The stone inside the orc chief Groctar whispers constantly and grows louder as he moves to kill. The Rulers buried a stone in the back of Legia's neck to give him a thirst for human blood, and planted one in Kamish. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Groctar)
- Why: the Rulers made the stones and embedded them during their war with the Monarchs. Driving beasts at humans soaked the human world in mana so that humans could awaken and survive the rematch. Wikipedia's summary agrees that the Rulers are indirectly responsible for the deaths. [F][P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Not every order is "kill humans". A voice told the demons moved into the Demon Castle to protect it from intruders at any cost (wiki cites manhwa ch. 82). [W]
- Freed of the compulsion a monster can be reasonable: Kamish's shadow is courteous and stops attacking once told the Rulers no longer control him. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Kamish)
- Monsters are stopped from explaining themselves. Baruka's and Esil's speech and image "glitch" when they try (sections 2.7 and 2.12). [W][P] [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-glitch-red-gate-jinwoo-secret)

### 5.2 Speech and intelligence

- Beasts have a runic alphabet and a language of their own, which humans call beast language or monster tongue. The original Baran answers Ashborn in it as an insult (wiki cites manhwa ch. 129). [W]
- Baruka speaks only the monster tongue; Jinwoo understands it through the System and answers fluently. [S][W]
- Kargalgan speaks human language through a possessed soldier, which the hunters find abnormal. [S][W]
- The Ant King learns Korean and Japanese by eating a human. [S][W]
- Speech tracks rank: D and C-rank types behave as animals; A-rank humanoids (high orcs, ice elves, demons) talk, plan and taunt; S-rank bosses negotiate. (A reading of the notes.)
- Fear and self-preservation are shown: Kargalgan goes from shock to terror, the Ant King flees, Groctar orders a retreat to the gate, Esil begs. Anime ep. 19 ends with Jinwoo asking whether magic beasts can feel fear. [F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-19)

### 5.3 Behaviour inside a dungeon

- Dungeons are pockets of the chaos world. [W]
- Bosses wait in a seat of power: Buryura asleep in a crystal room, Igris before a throne, Groctar in a throne room, Kargalgan in a stronghold from which he detects intruders and sends troops, the Ant Queen deep in the nest with guards. [W][S][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Kargalgan)
- Intelligent races play with prey. Ice elves pick off the weakest first and toy with the strongest. Kargalgan lures a strike team to his castle for sport and tortures its captain. High orcs lure, trap, taunt and offer duels, and their shaman breaks a promised truce. [W][S][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-manhwa-strongest-magic-beasts/)
- Ice elves and Jeju ants share one tactic: go for the weakest enemies first. [W]
- Territory between species: in the Red Gate no other beast enters the ice bears' forest, and the elves, wolves and others keep to the snow plains. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-most-clever-strategies-sung-jin-woo/)
- Rule and rivalry between monsters: hobgoblins lead goblins and carry them; high orcs keep collared jackals as trained animals; the green-orc chief despises high orcs; mindless demons cannot recognise intelligent demons as their own; demon clans feud over rank; giants chose leaders by making soldiers fight; the Ant King ate part of the dead Queen for her memories. No source shows two species fighting each other inside a gate. [W][P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-high-orcs-explained/)
- Hive command and morale: the Queen's guards never leave her, even when the rest of the swarm goes out to fight; the Ant King calls the swarm to him as a last resort; when he dies the remaining soldiers run. [P] [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/solo-leveling-jeju-island-arc-explained/)
- Some bosses are placed, not native. The Demon Castle was created by Kandiaru and its demons were moved there by him; its Baran is a copy he made to be the final boss; the temple statues are his puppets; Igris was posted by Ashborn to test Jinwoo. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/character/Baran)

### 5.4 Behaviour after a dungeon break

- A gate must be cleared within seven days or its monsters cross into the human world. Gates stay open until the boss is killed. Unchecked high-rank breaks can destroy cities or countries. [W][S][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-raids-explained/)
- Small scale: a single D-rank Stone Golem, a boss, walks the streets of Seoul. A scratch team of E and D-rank hunters fails to dent it while bystanders jeer. It was the last break monster left standing. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-4)
- Large scale: the Jeju gate could never be closed. The ants killed everyone on the island and three raids failed. The island was quarantined in the hope the ants would starve; instead they evolved wings and reached the mainland and Japan. Attacks on cities along Japan's south coast are what brought Japan's top hunter to offer help with the fourth raid. [W][S][P] [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-jeju-island-arc-explained/)
- After a boss dies outside a gate: leaderless ants go berserk and attack ships and beaches until hunted down. [F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-25)
- Whether the boss leaves in a break varies, and no source states a rule: [W][P] [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/solo-leveling-all-gates-ranked-by-rank/)
  - Left: the Stone Golem; Kamish, who came out of the first S-rank gate.
  - Stayed: the Ant Queen, who kept to her nest and sent the King out to lead the colony; Legia, chained inside while the giants poured out and one stood guard at the gate.
  - Waited: Kargalgan kept the strike team as entertainment "until the dungeon break occurred", so an intelligent boss can know a break is coming.
- A break changes the whole dungeon's state: territory limits vanish and the population starts adapting to the outside. (A reading of the notes.)

### 6.2 The anime production

- Studio A-1 Pictures. Season 1 aired January to March 2024 and season 2 January to March 2025, 25 episodes in all. Season 2 won Best Animation and Best Action at the 2025 Crunchyroll Anime Awards. [P] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Core staff (season 1 list): director Shunsuke Nakashige; character design Tomoko Sudou; sub-character design Chiaki Furuzumi; monster design Hirotaka Tokuda; prop design Soutarou Shiraishi; art director Yasuhiro Okumura; colour design Naomi Nakano; director of photography Daichi Iseki; CG director Toshitaka Morioka; music Hiroyuki Sawano. Yoshihiro Kanno is the series' action director and Atsushi Kaneko its animation producer. [P][O] [MyAnimeList news](https://myanimelist.net/amp/news/69823858)
- Scale: pre-production from April 2021 and animation from October 2022; a core A-1 team of 12 and an estimated 500 to 600 people in all; a TV episode normally has 20 to 30 key animators and 8,000 to 10,000 frames; season 2's main gain was more time for the creative staff. [O] [Cartoon Brew](https://cartoonbrew.com/anime/from-light-novel-to-anime-sensation-solo-leveling-producer-and-action-director-discuss-the-breakout-hit-242140.html)
- How the action is built, from staff statements: [O] [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/?p=13145822)
  - Named characters are hand-drawn and mass enemies are 3D, a choice of the director and the CG director.
  - The shadow army was planned in 2D and moved to 3D after tests, because its plain colours and lines gave too few visual cues in 2D.
  - Several animators often work on one shot, and key animators improvised much of the choreography under Nakashige and Kanno.
  - Jinwoo's early style was meant to look brutal but amateur. Kanno studied live-action fight footage for its flow.
  - City settings were used so that scenery can be thrown, broken or smashed into.
  - The gore was kept, within broadcast limits.

How each is staged:

- **Kasaka (ep. 4).** The snake is not CG; its body is animated a section at a time, which the reviewer found fluid. Blade-on-scale hits carry a sawblade-like sound. The music is flamenco-styled. Jinwoo's thoughts are voiced over the action without pauses. The storyboards stressed the monster's scale. The anime smears blood on Jinwoo's face, which the manhwa does not have. [P][O][W] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-4-review-true-strength/)
- **Cerberus (ep. 7).** A reviewer calls it the best individual combat cuts of the series to that point. Blood spray stays fluid at high speed with every hit Jinwoo takes, and the music sells the "under-levelled" feeling. The same reviewer ranks it just under the spider fight for animation. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-7-jinwoos-strongest-foe-yet/)
- **Igris (ep. 11).** The white-on-black look of the last moments was decided early. Drawing digitally let Kanno bend the linework and handle the backgrounds and colouring of those shots himself. Reviewers note alternating normal speed and slow motion, a switch to Jinwoo's point of view when he is thrown, and few cuts when a body can simply be shown moving fast. Fans credit Kikuchi's camera work. ANN's reviewer rates the fight as style over substance. Igris bleeds white in the manhwa and purple in the anime. [O][P][W] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-episode-11-a-legendary-battle/)
- **Baruka (ep. 14).** Called the best-looking action of the series to that point. [P] [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/review/solo-leveling-season-2-arise-from-the-shadow/episodes-13-14/.219982)
- **Vulcan (ep. 15).** Most of Jinwoo's inner monologue is dropped to keep the action moving. The giant demon's quick, slightly comic way of moving came out well with some CG. The fight is reported as cut short against the manhwa. [P][S] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-3-review-a-whirlwind-of-scenes/)
- **High orcs and Kargalgan (ep. 17 and 18).** Several planned cuts were merged into one 26-second cut of Igris and Iron because Kanno was assigned to it. The CG orc models were made to read as 2D, and the background orcs share one model with varied tattoos. Crunchyroll's producer names this battle the standout action scene of the series. A reviewer notes strong running and jumping animation from the first missed attack, choreography as the highlight, more surface texture on the magic shield than in the manhwa, heavy CG for crowds of orcs and shadows, none of the manhwa's cartoonish comedy faces, simple music that leaves blade and impact sounds prominent, and Kenta Miyake's gravelly voice carrying into the chanted spells. Kargalgan fights bare-handed only in the anime. A wiki trivia line, sourced to a forum thread, says the strike captain's orc kill is animated like a well-known finishing blow in Sword Art Online, another A-1 Pictures show. [O][P][W][F] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-6-review-absolute-cinema/)
- **Baran (ep. 20).** The fight takes less than half the episode. [P] [Epicdope](https://epicdope.com/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-8-anime-vs-manhwa)
- **Ant King's arrival (ep. 23).** Sold through sound: a high-pitched whine on arrival, a scream that is half beast and half human, and a voice (Akira Ishida) that matures as he absorbs people. Animation stays consistent with only brief 3D. The Queen's beheading is staged as graceful and "divine" with swelling music. The reviewer compares the King to Meruem from Hunter x Hunter. [P] [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-season-2-episode-11-review-an-absolute-massacre/)
- **Ant King duel (ep. 24).** About 17,000 frames against 8,000 to 10,000 for a normal episode. Kanno lengthened the King's transformation at the start of the "second round" beyond what the manhwa shows, wanting impact in a short time, and said the music and sound effects changed the scene's mood more than he expected. The colour idea is red against blue on a purple background. A scale study comparing Jinwoo with the Ant King was drawn in pre-production. The opening exchange of punches plays almost without music so the blows carry; the fast claw-and-dagger exchanges stay trackable; the escape is shown from the King's point of view. [O][P] [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-creators-reveal-most-challenging-episode)
- A reading across these: the strengths reviewers report are readability devices (blood, sound cues, texture on shields, serious faces) more than spectacle.

### 7.3 Anime against manhwa

- **Episode 1.** The anime opens on Jeju with red ants and a giant white ant attacking wounded hunters, adapted from the web novel; the manhwa reaches the island much later. It adds a goblin fight (Jinwoo's dagger breaks and a teammate saves him) and a werewolf boss kill, which the manhwa shows only in an exposition panel. Jinwoo carries a cheap dagger in the anime and goes in unarmed in the manhwa. The raid party is 13 in the anime and 17 in the manhwa. The anime drops the fireball sent down the tunnel to judge its length and adds a speech presenting essence stones and mana crystals as a clean power source that governments want. [W][F][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-vs-manhwa-biggest-changes/)
- **Statue of God.** The anime stretches Jinwoo's last stand: he parries the first blow with a borrowed sword, loses his right arm at the elbow and faces a final statue holding a longsword. In the manhwa he is impaled at once, takes a deep cut from shoulder to chest, and the blade is a scimitar. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-2)
- **Early money and weapons.** In the anime Jinwoo's weapons break in his first dungeons; CBR says the manhwa has him fight bare-handed because he cannot afford replacements. The anime treats even one small essence stone as a real win for him, where the manhwa tracks his money more closely. [P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-vs-manhwa-biggest-changes/)
- **Instant dungeon.** Jinwoo arrives armed in the anime. The wolves' names are red on first meeting in the anime and white in the manhwa. Blood on Jinwoo's face is added to the Kasaka fight. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-4)
- **Stone Golem.** Lee Joohee's healing spell breaks during the fight in the anime; in the manhwa she holds it. [W]
- **Buryura.** Manhwa: eight betrayers killed, and they blast their way back in. Anime: six, and they wait outside. [W][S]
- **Cerberus.** The manhwa has Jinwoo test his poison immunity at home with Kasaka's venom gland; the anime swaps that for beer and cuts the scene. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-7)
- **Goblins Dungeon.** The manhwa's boss-room sequence with the prisoner is cut from anime ep. 9; ep. 10 shows a Hobgoblin boss killed in one slash. [W][P] [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/solo-leveling-anime-vs-manhwa-biggest-changes/)
- **Igris and the job change.** In the manhwa he drags his sword down the throne steps and attacks at once; in the anime he enters from the side and pauses to point the sword at Jinwoo. The hammer knight's blow is in both: in the anime Jinwoo drops the teleport stone and recovers, in the manhwa he is knocked flat and surrounded. Jinwoo uses Stealth from the start of the survival quest in the manhwa and only after some kills in the anime. The manhwa's torch-lit approach is cut. Igris bleeds white in the manhwa and purple in the anime. [W][F] [daddyjim](https://daddyjim.com/solo-leveling/episode/Episode-11)
- **Red Gate.** The anime removes the Frostmen, leaving Ice Elves and Ice Bears. It adds elves hunting fleeing hunters and Baruka inviting Jinwoo and Igris to attack together. It cuts Iron's Taunt skill and Igris stalling Baruka alone, and changes the angle of Igris's killing stab on Kim Chul. The anime party is four hunters smaller; the camp eats bear meat instead of bread; Kim Chul charges openly instead of attacking by surprise; the guild's missing members number six in the anime and nine in the manhwa. Baruka's cut-off speech is staged as a visible game-style glitch and rewind; no source says how the manhwa draws that moment. [W][F][P] [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-glitch-red-gate-jinwoo-secret)
- **Vulcan and Metus.** Cut from the anime: the club swing that wipes out shadow soldiers, Igris presenting demon heads, Iron dragging Vulcan's head, and a shadow mage test-firing the Orb of Avarice. Jinwoo uses Stealth during the demon fights only in the manhwa. He attacks Metus from the front in the manhwa and from behind in the anime. The anime adds a montage of the climb and a scene of Iron mining, strips most of Jinwoo's inner monologue from the Vulcan fight, and shows him more decisive than the manhwa's improvising Jinwoo. ## 10. Touches in DungeonRaze

Pointers only. Names in code font are from the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Name tags.** The source colours a monster's overhead name white, orange or red by its threat against the player's level (section 3.5). The game's `.tag.t0`, `.t1`, `.t2` classes and `threatOf(e)` do the same thing.
- **Enemy definitions.** `EDEF` entries, their `threat`, and the attack `kind`s (`slam`, `charge`, `shot`, `volley`, `unb`) relate to the type tables in section 1 and the attack lists in section 2.
- **Packs.** `PACKS`, the two-attacker token rule, `callPack` and the `PURSUIT` table relate to pack tactics, mixed ranks in one gate and elite tiers (sections 1, 3.3 and 5.3).
- **Posture, poise and executions.** These relate to armour that turns blades and to the eye, neck and face weak points that end most boss fights (section 2.19).
- **The boss and its chamber.** `G.inBoss`, the boss's limits and its heal on return relate to bosses waiting in a seat of power, bodyguard rooms and two-stage fights (sections 2.19 and 5.3).
- **Giving up and territory.** `giveUp`, `huntOver`, the leash and `inTerritory` relate to morale, retreat and territory between species (sections 5.2 and 5.3).
- **Portal grades E to S** and backlog item 2 (difficulty and kill rewards by grade) relate to sections 3 and 4.
- **Loot** (backlog item 3) relates to section 4. **More enemy variety and a second boss** (backlog item 4) relate to sections 1 and 2.
- **Caves, crystals and lights.** The cave world and its glowing crystals relate to mana crystals, crystal-filled boss rooms and the Insects Dungeon tunnels (sections 1.4 and 4.2).
- **Primitive-built figures** with `f.rim` and `f.eye` relate to the design language in section 6.1.

Two project rules apply to anything taken from this chapter. Names, characters and signature moves from the series are deliberately not used in the game. The shadow-raising class was dropped by the owner and is not to be reintroduced unless asked.
