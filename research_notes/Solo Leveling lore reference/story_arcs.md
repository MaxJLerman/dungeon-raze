## 1. What are the story arcs in order, and which manhwa chapters, novel chapters, volumes and anime episodes does each cover?

### Takeaway
Three news-site listings agree on 22 arcs: 21 for the main story and epilogue (manhwa chapters 1 to 179, novel chapters 1 to 270) plus a manhwa side-story block at chapters 180 to 200. The anime's 25 episodes cover the first 13 arcs, ending with Jeju Island at manhwa chapter 107.

- **[Fandom-API]** Second-pass note on this takeaway: the wiki's Story Arcs page lists the same 22 arcs with the same manhwa ranges, and the wiki's episode pages give a manhwa chapter range for each of the 25 episodes (last lines of Cited Findings). The wiki counts episode 25 as running on into the Recruitment arc, to chapter 110 — [wiki: Story Arcs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Story_Arcs)
- **[Web-2]** Third-pass note on this takeaway: a normally served episode guide now gives a chapter range for all 25 episodes, and the "107, 108 or 110" question has an answer. The finale adapts chapters 104 to 108 in sequence, skips 109 and borrows its last scene from 110. The English print manhwa is complete in 15 volumes (the last on March 17, 2026), and the publisher's contents pages give a volume-by-volume map (Cited Findings below) — [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/solo-leveling-season-3-what-to-expect-explainer/); [Yen Press](https://yenpress.com/series/solo-leveling-comic)

**Premise (all versions)**
- Hunters are humans with supernatural abilities who fight monsters. Jinwoo is a notoriously weak E-rank who survives an extremely dangerous double dungeon that wipes out most of his party. ## 4. The ending: what Jinwoo asks for, the new timeline, the epilogue and side stories

### Takeaway
After Antares falls, Jinwoo asks the Rulers to turn time back once more so he can fight the Monarchs alone before they reach Earth. He does so for 27 years, returns to a world with no gates where almost nobody knows what he did, and in that world marries Cha Hae-In and has a son, Suho.

- **[Fandom-API]** Second-pass note on this takeaway: the wiki agrees with this outline and has him keep and use his powers in the new timeline. Its coverage of the side stories is fragments from character pages only — [wiki: Sung Jinwoo](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sung_Jinwoo)
- **[Web-2]** Third-pass note on this takeaway: normally served pages now cover the last two chapters and most of the side stories. In short: he is sent back to his middle-school years, is gone for two of them, comes home looking like a teenager, turns down the Rulers' demand that he leave Earth, and the manhwa's last page is a first meeting with a younger Cha Hae-In. The track meets, Woo Jinchul the detective, the Titans, the police job, the marriage and Suho are all side-story material. No page read says Cha Hae-In gets her first-timeline memories back — [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/solo-leveling-ending); [The Outer Haven, volume 14](https://www.theouterhaven.net/comic-review-solo-leveling-vol-14/)

### Cited Findings
- The Rulers hold the Cup of Reincarnation, a relic of their maker, the Absolute Being, and have used it to rewind time before. ## 6. Where the novel, manhwa and anime tell the story differently

### Takeaway
The anime follows the manhwa closely but adds scene-setting, moves a few scenes, cuts side detail in the Season 2 finale, and softens the thread in which Japan sets Korea's hunters up to die. The Japanese-language release goes further and moves the whole story to Japan under Japanese names. I found almost nothing sourced on novel-only content.

- **[Fandom-API]** Second-pass note on this takeaway: the wiki's arc pages list novel-against-webtoon differences for arcs 2 to 7 and 12, and its episode pages list anime-against-manhwa differences for several episodes. Both are added below — [wiki: Story Arcs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Story_Arcs)

### Cited Findings

**Japanese localisation**
- The Japanese title is romanised *Ore dake Reberuappu na Ken*. Names are Japanese: Sung Jin-woo is Shun Mizushino, Sung Jin-ah is Aoi Mizushino, Park Kyung-hye is Satoko Mizushino, Sung Il-hwan is Junichiro Mizushino, Cha Hae-in is Shizuku Kousaka, Yoo Jin-ho is Kenta Morobishi, Baek Yoon-ho is Taiga Shirakawa, Hwang Dong-soo is Masato Ukyo.
