## 1. Profiles of the nine Monarchs

### Takeaway
There are nine Monarchs, each the king or queen of one monster race, and each has a human-looking form plus a "true" form released by Spiritual Body Manifestation. Antares and Ashborn sit far above the other seven; the rest are profiled in the source material mostly through one or two fights each, and three of them (Legia, Yogumunt, Baran) have very little documented power set.

### Cited Findings

**Shared facts**
- The Monarchs were made from darkness by the Absolute Being, born to destroy the world, with the Rulers made from light to protect it. — [Fandom: Monarchs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Monarchs)
- The full roster and titles: Antares (Destruction, King of Dragons); Ashborn (Shadows, King of the Dead); Baran (White Flames, King of Demons); Rakan (Fangs, King of Beasts); Sillad (Frost, King of Snow Folk); Tarnak (Iron Body, King of Monstrous Humanoids); Legia (the Beginning, King of Giants); Querehsha (Plagues, Queen of Insects); Yogumunt (Transfiguration, King of Demonic Spectres). Sung Jinwoo is the second Shadow Monarch. — [Fandom: Monarchs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Monarchs)
- Every Monarch can use Spiritual Body Manifestation and can open gates to travel between worlds. Each is said to command millions of soldiers. — [Fandom: Monarchs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Monarchs)
- Wikipedia's character list uses the same names and adds that each Monarch rules a monster race and seeks to wipe out humanity. — [Wikipedia: Solo Leveling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Naming varies by source. Beebom (updated 15 Oct 2025) and Dafunda (19 Jan 2024) call the Frost Monarch "Hochwan"; the wiki names him Sillad and lists "Hockwan" as an epithet. — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-monarchs/); [Dafunda](https://dafunda.com/en/otaku/monarchs-in-solo-leveling-whos-the-strongest); [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Tarnak's title is rendered "King of Monsters, Monarch of Steel" in the Tappytoon English webtoon (ch. 172), which the wiki calls a mistranslation. Antares is "King of Berserk Dragons" in some translations. — [Fandom: Tarnak](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Tarnak); [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)

#### 1a. Antares, Monarch of Destruction, King of Dragons
- Strongest and oldest of the nine and the final antagonist. Debut: [N] ch. 228, [M] ch. 163. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- Human-shaped form: tall, muscular, middle-aged man with long red hair, short red beard, glowing red eyes, dark red claws, reddish-silver high-collared armour and a red-brown tailcoat. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- Personality: relaxed and confident, enjoys destruction, contemptuous of humans, but has a sense of honour and will bargain with someone strong enough to impress him. He cares for his own minions and dislikes dragons of mixed blood. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- No human vessel: he could not find a human able to hold his power, so he stayed behind until conditions let him cross in his raw form. He is the only Monarch who entered the human world without a vessel. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- Powers listed: extreme strength, speed and near-invulnerability, Dragon's Fear, Breath of Destruction, fire magic (explosions from streams of fire and claw slashes), levitation, telepathy with other Monarchs, shapeshifting, enormous red-and-white gates, Spiritual Body Manifestation. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- Death, original timeline [M] ch. 176 to 177: cut nearly in half by Jinwoo, then speared by the Rulers. Death, revised timeline [M] ch. 178: killed by Jinwoo in his palace 27 years after the reset. Full detail in section 6. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)

#### 1b. Ashborn, Monarch of Shadows, King of the Dead
- Also "Greatest Fragment of Brilliant Light": he began as the strongest Ruler. Debut: [N] ch. 217, [M] ch. 128. Status is given as alive but asleep. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)
- Monarch form: large armoured shadow warrior with flame-like glowing purple hair, purple eyes, two curved black horns, claws, jet-black armour, a smoky black cloak and a black sword. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)
- Vessel: Sung Jinwoo, who becomes his successor instead of a possessed host. Full detail in section 7. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)

#### 1c. Baran, Monarch of White Flames, King of Demons
- Debut: [N] ch. 107, [M] ch. 85. Japanese voice in the anime is listed as Shota Yamamoto. — [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- Appearance: large muscular grey-skinned demon, glowing white eyes, flame-like white hair, long tail, four grey horns (two long and curved), ornate grey helmet, pale yellow shoulder and groin armour. The Demon Castle copy has red eyes and no helmet ([M] ch. 85). — [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- Personality: cruel and brash, not a coward. He refused to tell Ashborn who ordered the betrayal and mocked him in monster language. — [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- Powers: Hell's Army (summons his demon army through portals); Lightning Breath (area or single target, applies a stun); White Flames (turns into a lightning bolt and charges, heavy damage plus stun; [R] ch. 241); Doppelganger (copies a target's appearance after consuming its blood and flesh); Blood Crystal (absorbs the souls of fellow demons to grow in size and strength; [R] ch. 243). — [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- The real Baran died in the ancient war. Antares ordered him and Rakan to ambush Ashborn; Ashborn beat both, Rakan fled, and Ashborn crushed Baran's skull one-handed ([M] ch. 129; ordered by Antares per ch. 177). Ashborn then absorbed the corpse into his shadow ([M] ch. 163). — [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- The Baran that Jinwoo fights is a clone made by Kandiaru as the boss of the Demon Castle's 100th floor. It overpowered Jinwoo until Esil Radiru distracted it; Jinwoo tore off its left arm and destroyed its head with a punch ([M] ch. 87). The wiki judges the clone much weaker than the original. — [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- Baran is the only Monarch not revived by the time reset. — [Fandom: Monarchs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Monarchs)
- Clearing the Demon Castle gives Jinwoo a rune stone with Shadow Exchange, a mission reward, not Baran's own skill. — [Fandom: Rune Stones](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rune_Stones)

#### 1d. Rakan, Monarch of Fangs, King of Beasts
- Debut: [N] ch. 213, [M] ch. 107, [A] episode 25. Voices: Hiroshi Shirokuma (JP), Ray Chase (EN). Novel epithet: "Greatest Hunter in the Chaos World". — [Fandom: Rakan](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rakan)
- Appearance: large muscular young man, tan skin, glowing yellow eyes (red when using power), sharp teeth, wild black hair, silver fur pelts, a ragged fur-belted skirt, gold-trimmed white armour on the right shoulder and groin. — [Fandom: Rakan](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rakan)
- Personality: aggressive, arrogant, eats a human who bumps into him, but runs from anyone stronger. Cares for his underlings. — [Fandom: Rakan](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rakan)
- Powers: strength, speed and durability enough to shrug off Thomas Andre breaking his neck; claws that tear red, claw-shaped gates in space; telekinesis (lifts tons of rubble); fast regeneration (regrew an arm in seconds); Spiritual Body Manifestation into a gigantic white wolf, with partial transformation of chosen body parts. — [Fandom: Rakan](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rakan)
- Key fights [M]: helps murder Christopher Reed (ch. 140); rampages through Seoul (ch. 156); beats Thomas Andre after transforming (ch. 157); is briefly blinded by Lennart Niermann and axe-kicked by Jinwoo (ch. 158); stabs Jinwoo through the chest from behind while Sillad holds his attention (ch. 160); is overpowered by Sung Il-Hwan and flees through a portal (ch. 162 to 164). — [Fandom: Rakan](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rakan)
- Death [M] ch. 165: cornered in a forest, he begs forgiveness; Jinwoo says he must survive five attacks; Rakan takes his full-size form and dies in four. Jinwoo carries some of his teeth to Sillad as proof. — [Fandom: Rakan](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rakan)
- [R]: leaves a wolf-cub heir, Gray, a servant named Brocky, and a weapon, the Fang of Rakan, made from a baby tooth holding a piece of his soul. — [Fandom: Rakan](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rakan)

#### 1e. Sillad, Monarch of Frost, King of Snow Folk
- Debut: [N] ch. 200, [M] ch. 107, [A] episode 25. Voices: Mitsuru Miyamoto (JP), Bruce DuBose (EN). — [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Appearance: muscular elderly ice elf, blue skin, long silver hair with one strand over the right side of the face, blank white eyes, round ears, ragged white cloak with black collar, tattered shirt, black trousers and boots. — [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Personality: cocky and condescending but cautious, avoids needless fights (he puts bystanders on Jeju to sleep instead of killing them). Turns anxious and reckless against a stronger foe. — [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Powers: ice breath that freezes everything in its path (stopped if his mouth is forced shut); ice magic (waves, walls, weapons including a trident, ice monsters, huge ice storms, an ice "meteor" large enough to threaten a city); blue energy blasts; sleep by finger snap; blue gates opened with a flick of the wrist. — [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Spiritual Body Manifestation: a younger form with much longer, glowing flame-like hair and longer elf ears, stripped to trousers and boots. — [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Key fights [M]: with Yogumunt, ambushed by Sung Il-Hwan in America to no effect (ch. 111); kills Go Gunhee in his office (ch. 150), then escapes Jinwoo by raising an ice wall and firing an ice spear at the dying Go Gunhee as a distraction (ch. 151); proposes killing Jinwoo before he matures (ch. 152); in Seoul his transformed state pushes Jinwoo to his limit and sets up Rakan's killing blow (ch. 160); cuts off Il-Hwan's left arm with an ice storm (ch. 164). — [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Death [M] ch. 165: Beru shatters the ice meteor in one blow, the awakened Jinwoo breaks the trident with one hand and slashes him across the chest; the vessel gives out and the body dissolves into shards of light. — [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)

#### 1f. Querehsha, Monarch of Plagues, Queen of Insects
- Debut: [N] ch. 215, [M] ch. 140. The only one of the original nine titled Queen. — [Fandom: Querehsha](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Querehsha)
- Appearance: tall young woman, glowing red eyes, pale skin, long black hair, dark purple lips, insect features on arms and legs, greenish-black armour. — [Fandom: Querehsha](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Querehsha)
- Personality: bloodthirsty, but motherly toward her minions, whom she calls her children. She is possessive of Beru, the Jeju ant king. — [Fandom: Querehsha](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Querehsha)
- Powers: giant glowing green hands made of mana; a concentrated venom secreted from her body; necromancy through parasites that reanimate and control corpses; near-instant regeneration (regrew her whole body after being cut in half, but could not do it a second time after being cut into dozens of pieces); Pledge of Trust ([R] ch. 154). — [Fandom: Querehsha](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Querehsha)
- Spiritual Body Manifestation: an insect monster with many appendages growing from her back. — [Fandom: Querehsha](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Querehsha)
- Fights and death [M]: joins the murder of Christopher Reed (ch. 140); in Seoul she is distracted trying to make Beru submit, is cut in half from behind, regenerates, finds Jinwoo immune to her venom, transforms and charges, and is diced by his Mutilation skill (ch. 159 to 160). Her body turns to dust. — [Fandom: Querehsha](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Querehsha)

#### 1g. Tarnak, Monarch of Iron Body, King of Monstrous Humanoids
- Debut: [N] ch. 182, [M] ch. 140. — [Fandom: Tarnak](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Tarnak)
- Appearance: tall muscular young man with slick blond hair and green eyes (lime-green glow when using power), long white coat with a blood-red collar, gold shoulder pads, white gloves, grey T-shirt, black trousers and shoes. — [Fandom: Tarnak](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Tarnak)
- Personality: egotistical, thinks a human cannot be a fit Shadow Monarch, yet rational enough to refuse to fight Jinwoo without Antares. — [Fandom: Tarnak](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Tarnak)
- Powers: Iron Body Reinforcement (large boost to attack and defence, and a strengthened soul that resists life-draining debuffs); telepathy with other Monarchs; gates. — [Fandom: Tarnak](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Tarnak)
- Spiritual Body Manifestation: a monstrous golem with flaming lime-green hair and dark grey armour. — [Fandom: Tarnak](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Tarnak)
- Fights and death [M]: joins the murder of Christopher Reed (ch. 140); refuses Sillad's plan (ch. 152); in the Canada invasion he confronts Thomas Andre, and Bellion and Beru come out of Thomas's shadow while Tusk blocks his telepathic call to Antares (ch. 172); he transforms and the three kill him (ch. 173). — [Fandom: Tarnak](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Tarnak)

#### 1h. Legia, Monarch of the Beginning, King of Giants
- Debut: [N] ch. 174, [M] ch. 137. Hidden boss of the Tokyo S-Rank Gate. — [Fandom: Legia](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Legia)
- Appearance: a muscular man about twice the size of an adult human, bluish-grey skin, long messy black hair, scraggly beard, glowing red eyes, wearing only a tattered white loincloth. — [Fandom: Legia](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Legia)
- Personality: odd humour (laughs wildly on meeting a fellow Monarch) and cunning. — [Fandom: Legia](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Legia)
- History: captured alive by the Rulers after the Absolute Being's death and held in mana-absorbing chains ([M] ch. 163). The Rulers later put an essence stone in the back of his neck, which gave him a bloodlust toward humans. — [Fandom: Legia](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Legia)
- Powers: great strength; Pledge of Trust, a spell binding two parties so neither can lie to the other. — [Fandom: Legia](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Legia)
- Death [M] ch. 137 to 138: he asks Jinwoo to free him and promises alliance under the pledge; Jinwoo finds the essence stone, Legia attacks, and Jinwoo beheads him in one strike. The body breaks into shards of light. — [Fandom: Legia](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Legia)
- Legia tells Jinwoo he cannot be made a shadow soldier because he is a spiritual organism without an organic body. — [Fandom: Legia](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Legia)
- The Giants page adds, without a clear citation, that the king of giants could disturb the fabric of the universe just by moving his shackles. — [Fandom: Giants](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Giants)

#### 1i. Yogumunt, Monarch of Transfiguration, King of Demonic Spectres
- Debut: [N] ch. 239, [M] ch. 111. Novel epithet: "Greatest Sorcerer in the Chaos World". — [Fandom: Yogumunt](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Yogumunt)
- Appearance: elderly man with grey hair, light red eyes (pink-purple glow when using power), a red blob-like mark on the torso, a tattered black-purple hooded cloak, a pale yellow bone-like mask over the mouth, and two metallic horn-like spikes rising from the upper back. — [Fandom: Yogumunt](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Yogumunt)
- Personality: looks down on humans (dismisses Il-Hwan as not worth fighting) but knows he cannot face Jinwoo without Antares. — [Fandom: Yogumunt](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Yogumunt)
- Powers documented: levitation and mass creation of purple gates to move himself and armies between worlds. — [Fandom: Yogumunt](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Yogumunt)
- Role [M]: tells Sillad a Ruler's vessel is in Korea (ch. 111); refuses Sillad's plan for fear of sharing Baran's fate (ch. 152); reports the Shadow Monarch's betrayal to Antares (ch. 167); opens countless portals in Canada to bring through the dragon army and is frozen along with it by Jinwoo's Dragon's Fear (ch. 173). — [Fandom: Yogumunt](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Yogumunt)
- Fate: the Yogumunt page says his fate in the original timeline is unknown; the Monarchs page says Jinwoo "defeated" him and Tarnak without fighting them in person. In the revised timeline Jinwoo kills him two years in, after every Monarch except him and Antares was already dead ([M] ch. 178). — [Fandom: Yogumunt](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Yogumunt); [Fandom: Monarchs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Monarchs)

**Human vessels of the Monarchs**
- Monarchs take over their human vessels completely, which lets them use full power in the human world but means they die if the vessel is killed. — [Fandom: Monarchs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Monarchs)
- None of the sources read names the humans whose bodies the Monarchs took. Beebom says only that Rakan possessed unnamed humans. — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/solo-leveling-monarchs/)

## 2. The armies, and how their races appear earlier as dungeon monsters

### Takeaway
Each Monarch's race turns up long before the Monarch does, as the stock monsters of earlier dungeons: insects and beasts in low-rank gates, ice elves in the Red Gate, high orcs in an A-rank gate, demons in the Demon Castle, giants in Tokyo, a dragon as the first S-rank boss. Coverage is uneven: the dragon, insect, beast, ice-elf, demon, orc and giant races are documented, while the demonic spectres are not.

### Cited Findings
- **Dragons (Antares).** Types named in the novel: winged four-legged western dragons, smaller two-legged dragons, drakes (giant wingless behemoths), ancient-class dragons, sky dragons, and humanoid dragonewts ([N] ch. 228, 239). Rank is "at least S". Named dragons: Antares, Kamish, Kaisellin, Granodeh, Ragnar. Race debut: [N] ch. 108, [M] ch. 85. — [Fandom: Dragons](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Dragons)
- Dragon bone takes magical energy unusually well ([N] ch. 211), and Dragon's Fear is described as the dragons' cruellest skill ([N] ch. 240). — [Fandom: Dragons](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Dragons)
- Antares' invasion force is put at ten million ([M] ch. 173). — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- **Insects (Querehsha).** Types: poison-toothed giant desert centipedes and ants. Named: Ant Queen, Ant King, Arsha, Giant Arachnid Buryura. Ranks D to S. Debut: [N] ch. 10, [M] ch. 11. — [Fandom: Insects](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Insects)
- **Beasts (Rakan).** Types: Steel-Fanged Raikans, Black-Shadow Razan, Razor-Clawed Briga, Blue Venom-Fanged Kasaka, Cerberus, werewolves, ice bears, dungeon jackals. Ranks D to S. Debut: [N] ch. 15, [M] ch. 13. — [Fandom: Beasts](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Beasts)
- **Snow Folk (Sillad).** Ice elves: blue skin, white hair, white eyes, long ears, human-level intelligence, bows and daggers, ice-infused arrows, ambush tactics that pick off the weakest first, shared Stealth, sadistic. At least B-rank. Seen in the Red Gate: [N] ch. 59, [M] ch. 48 to 51, [A] episode 13. Sillad can also build monsters out of ice. — [Fandom: Ice Elves](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Elves); [Fandom: Sillad](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Sillad)
- Only adult ice elves served in the Frost Monarch's army; children stayed in a village with elders until old enough to join ([R]). Ice elves are a mutation of high elves ([R] ch. 256). — [Fandom: Ice Elves](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Elves)
- **Monstrous humanoids (Tarnak).** High orcs: red-skinned, each a match for an A-rank hunter, strong magic resistance, some can speak to humans; tattoos count kills. First seen in the Hunters Guild Gate: [N] ch. 85, [M] ch. 69 to 74, [A] episode 17. - **Giants (Legia).** Man-eating, building-sized, grey-skinned, horned, armoured, sword-carrying, and fast for their size. One breaks Yuri Orloff's barrier in a single swing. They usually appear as bosses of top A-rank gates and flood out of the Tokyo S-Rank Gate: [N] ch. 155, [M] ch. 131 to 132. — [Fandom: Giants](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Giants)
- **Demons (Baran).** Common demons are animal-like and can be as big as small buildings; demon nobles are human-sized, intelligent and organised into at least twenty ranked clans (Radiru, Garsh, Pathos, Ricado, Hershel). Usually horned with red eyes. First seen [M] ch. 57 in the Demon Castle. — [Fandom: Demons](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Demons)
- Most demons died under Baran in the first war; Kandiaru moved a large group into the Demon Castle with orders to guard it ([M] ch. 82). Mindless demons cannot recognise intelligent demons as their own ([M] ch. 83). — [Fandom: Demons](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Demons)
- Demons grow stronger by consuming souls, and this trait, studied from Baran's corpse, was the basis of the System's levelling ([R] ch. 240). Demon souls cannot be shadow-extracted ([R] ch. 232). — [Fandom: Demons](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Demons)

## 3. The Rulers and their human vessels

### Takeaway
There were eight Rulers; Ashborn was the eighth and strongest, and the seven who rebelled are the ones acting in the story. They are winged, angelic warriors who are not individually named in the sources read, they share the telekinetic Ruler's Authority, and they lent power to seven humans, four of whom the Monarchs killed.

### Cited Findings
- The Rulers are an ancient race of angelic warriors made from light, also called Fragments of Brilliant Light and Messengers of God. Debut: [N] ch. 160, [M] ch. 129. — [Fandom: Rulers](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rulers)
- Wikipedia describes them as ancient winged humanoids who resemble angels, and notes that their methods are morally doubtful because the gates they opened killed many people. — [Wikipedia: Solo Leveling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling)
- Seven of the eight Rulers rebelled against the Absolute Being once they realised he wanted the war to go on for his entertainment; Ashborn alone stayed loyal and was beaten and left for dead. — [Fandom: Rulers](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rulers)
- The only individual Ruler whose look is described is Ashborn before his change: a burly white knight with large white wings, long silver hair, glowing white eyes, silver robes, light tan armour, a grey-blue cloak and a white sword. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)
- In the final battle the Rulers arrive with armies in the sky and kill Antares with spears from all sides ([M] ch. 177). — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- **Ruler's Authority** ("Capture"): telekinesis that costs no mana, works over a wide radius and on the surroundings, and can be used for recoil movement and mid-air dodges. At full strength it works even on Monarch-level fighters ([M] ch. 146, 59). Jinwoo first gets a weaker form, Ruler's Hands, from Igris's rune stone. First shown [M] ch. 40, [A] episode 12. — [Fandom: Ruler's Authority](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ruler%27s_Authority)

## 4. Spiritual body manifestation, and true form versus vessel form

### Takeaway
Monarchs and Rulers are mana-based beings without organic bodies, so in the human world they act through human vessels; Spiritual Body Manifestation is the skill that drops the human shape and shows the being's true form with its full power. For a boss design this reads as a guaranteed second phase.

### Cited Findings
- The skill lets high-ranking beings show their true spiritual form with a surge of mana and raw power; the look changes to match the user, such as a beast or dragon, with a radiant or shadowy aura depending on side. — [Fandom: Spiritual Body Manifestation](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Spiritual_Body_Manifestation)
- Users listed: Baran, Legia, Querehsha, Rakan, Sillad, Tarnak, Yogumunt, Antares, all seven Ruler vessels, and Sung Suho. — [Fandom: Spiritual Body Manifestation](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Spiritual_Body_Manifestation)

## 6. Antares in detail

### Takeaway
Antares is written as an unbeatable final boss who is not beaten in a fair fight the first time: Jinwoo strips him of his army with a fear roar, isolates him on an island, survives long enough to tear space open, and the Rulers finish him. The real one-on-one win comes only in the rewritten timeline.

### Cited Findings
- In the ancient war he feared Ashborn's power and ordered Rakan and Baran to kill him ([M] ch. 163, 177). When Ashborn later asked to return, Antares welcomed him because two Monarchs were lost and the rest were weak. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares); [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- [M] ch. 167: Yogumunt tells him the Shadow Monarch has turned and most of their forces are gone. He chooses to deal with Jinwoo himself and readies the dragon army. — [Fandom: Antares](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Antares)
- [M] ch. 170 to 171: he arrives through a gigantic gate in Canada, burns the Canadian S-rank Jay Mills to ash with his breath, and has his army cut a path across the country to draw Jinwoo out. - [M] ch. 173: when Jinwoo appears, Antares sends the ten-million army to corner him. ## 7. Ashborn in detail

### Takeaway
Ashborn is a Ruler who became a Monarch: the loyal eighth Fragment of Brilliant Light, struck down by his own kind, who survived by awakening a death power the Absolute Being had hidden in him. He ends by handing that power to Jinwoo as a successor instead of taking his body.

### Cited Findings
- When the seven rebelled, Ashborn alone defended the Absolute Being, was beaten and left to die. Dying, he found and triggered a power the Absolute Being had placed in him and became the Shadow Monarch, healed and stronger. He arrived too late: the Absolute Being was already dead. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)
- The Absolute Being gave him that power as a means to destroy his own creations if they ever rebelled ([M] ch. 192). — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)
- After the Rulers captured Legia, Ashborn judged the Monarchs would lose and offered them his help, which they took reluctantly. He kept the war balanced, but both sides feared him. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)
- Betrayal [M] ch. 129: Rakan and Baran ambush him with their armies just after he has cut down hundreds of the Rulers' soldiers. He beats both and kills Baran, but his shadow army is nearly wiped out. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn); [Fandom: Baran](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Baran)
- Straight after, the Rulers come down and ask his forgiveness. He demands they kill him and end the war; they keep pleading and he cannot answer. He leaves to rebuild his army, which lets the Rulers win and drives the Monarchs into the cracks between worlds. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)
- He later rejoins the Monarchs under Antares and follows them to the human world, wanting a place to belong. — [Fandom: Ashborn](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Ashborn)

## 8. Kamish

### Takeaway
Kamish was one of Antares' dragons, captured by the Rulers in the first war and released through the first S-rank gate, where he became the worst disaster in human history. His corpse yields the series' best dagger set and a rune stone holding Dragon's Fear.

### Cited Findings
- Kamish was a servant of Antares who was captured during the first war and had an essence stone implanted. He confirms his service to Antares himself when revived ([M] ch. 143). — [Fandom: Kamish](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Kamish)
- He was the boss of the first S-Rank Gate, which opened in America. He devastated the US west coast and killed all but five of the S-rank hunters sent against him; the five survivors killed him ([M] ch. 115). He is called humanity's greatest calamity. — [Fandom: Kamish](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Kamish)
- Casualty figures differ by page: "hundreds of S-Rank Hunters" on the Kamish page; millions of deaths and thousands of hunters on the novel-based Dragons page. — [Fandom: Kamish](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Kamish); [Fandom: Dragons](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Dragons)
- Appearance: enormous red western dragon, five grey horns, glowing yellow eyes, light brown belly, long whip-like tail. As a shadow: black scales, glowing purple horns and belly. — [Fandom: Kamish](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Kamish)
- Abilities: Dragon's Fear, Dragon's Breath (fire), speech. Rank S alive, General grade as a shadow. Debut: [N] ch. 135, [M] ch. 115. — [Fandom: Kamish](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Kamish)
- The US government kept the corpse in its death pose behind mana-reinforced glass, in a basement under the headquarters of its hunter bureau, which was built over it. — [Fandom: Kamish](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Kamish)

## 9. Differences between the novel, the manhwa and the anime

### Takeaway
The anime has barely reached this material: its 25 episodes end with the first glimpse of Rakan and Sillad, so nearly everything about the Monarchs and Rulers exists only in the novel and manhwa. Documented novel-versus-manhwa differences are small (designs, epithets, where characters first appear), and the larger claimed differences could not be verified.
