## Shadow Extraction: the rules, successes and failures

### Takeaway
Extraction raises a dead enemy as a loyal shadow soldier on the command "Arise". Normally served sources agree on three tries per target, failure against much stronger or long-dead targets, and the exclusion of Monarchs, Rulers and demons.
[Fandom-API] The wiki adds the skill's own text (no mana cost, failure proportional to the target's stats and time since death), an area effect, a starting cap of 30, the ability to cancel an extraction, and the detail of each notable success and failure.
