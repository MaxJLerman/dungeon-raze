How to play

Enemy names

A name appears above an enemy when it notices you or when you look at it. The colour of the name compares that enemy with you.

  • Husk White name: weaker than you.
  • Brute Orange name: around your level or slightly above.
  • The Ossuary Warden Red name: much stronger than you.

Combat

  • Pale flash: parry it. Red glow: you cannot block it, so dodge.
  • Orange bar full: the enemy kneels. Attack beside it to execute.
  • Out of sight: enemies chase what they can see. Break their line of sight and they search where they last saw you, then go back to their post. They will not follow you into the boss chamber.
  • Shrines are checkpoints and refill everything. EscThe pause buttonOptionsMenu pauses.
