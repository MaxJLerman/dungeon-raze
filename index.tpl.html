The Ossuary Warden

DungeonRaze

Six gates stand in the hall, graded E to S. Only the first is open. Pick a class, step through it, and come back alive.

This needs a keyboard and a mouse.

  • Pale flash: parry it. Red glow: you cannot block it, so dodge.
  • Orange bar full: the enemy kneels. Attack beside it to execute.
  • Shrines are checkpoints and refill everything. Esc pauses.

Paused

Resume takes the mouse back.

Choose a class

Switching mid-run keeps your place in the cave and your share of health.

Controls

Click a binding, then press the key or mouse button you want. Esc cancels. A key already in use swaps places.

You fell

You wake at the last shrine you kindled. Everything you killed is back on its feet.
