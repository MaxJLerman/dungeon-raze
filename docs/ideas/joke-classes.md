# Joke classes

Ideas kept for later, from a brainstorm with the owner on 2026-11-17. None of
these is built. The unlock system (plaques, Inspect, codes) comes first, with
the three hidden classes: Sapper, Enforcer and Riftwalker.

Joke classes are unlocked only by codes, never by feats. They are Easter eggs.
The hall's left wall holds the three hidden-class plaques, so joke-class codes
need a second spot in the hall (a graffiti wall or a vending machine, say).
That spot is not decided.

Names, looks and signature moves must be original. Nothing here may copy a
character from the series that inspired the game, or anything else owned.

## Chosen

### Nan on a Mobility Scooter

The owner's favourite.

- **Light chain:** handbag swings and walking-stick hooks, mixed in one chain.
- **Heavy:** a torrent of verbal abuse at the enemy, shown as floating symbols
  in the style of comic-strip swearing (`#@$%&!`). No actual words. While the
  symbols float toward the enemy, angry noises play: "rah, rah, rah, rah".
  Audio is synthesised in code, so this is a voice-like burst, not a sample.
- **Tool:** boiled sweets. Enemies stop to unwrap them.
- **Ability:** "Back in my day…", a monologue that sends nearby enemies to
  sleep.
- **Moves like:** a mobility scooter. Details open.

### RPG Dude

- Fires rockets for everything: light, heavy and tool.
- **Ability:** a rocket at his own feet that launches him.
- The cave walls cannot be destroyed (the field is built once). Explosions can
  throw rubble and hurt enemies but do not carve rock.

### Kerbside Kev

- Throws things found on a busy city street: bins, fire hydrants, street
  signs, roadblock signs, traffic cones, bollards, and anything else from a
  street, spread across his attacks, tool and ability.
- **Look:** a regular man with no clothes on, wound neck to ankle in
  red-and-white barrier tape, with a traffic cone on his head. The owner first
  suggested a body covered in layered receipts; that is a known character's
  signature look, so it was swapped for the tape.

### Flailin' Phil, the Inflatable Tube Man

A car-dealership tube man who came alive in a closing-down sale.

- **Health is air pressure.** He droops as he is hurt. The flask is a foot
  pump he stamps on.
- **Light chain:** flailing slaps, each swing's arc chosen at random.
- **Heavy:** puffs to three times his size and knocks everything back.
- **Dodge:** deflates flat into a nylon puddle. Enemies trip on him.
- **Tool:** Free Balloon With Every Purchase. Ties a balloon to an enemy,
  which floats out of the fight, then drops and takes fall damage.
- **Ability:** CLOSING DOWN SALE. An air horn; nearby enemies stop to stare
  at a spinning price tag for 4 seconds.
- **Execution:** sucks the enemy in at his base; it bulges up the tube and is
  fired out of the top like a cannonball.
- Built from a stack of swaying tube segments.

### Bathtub Barry

- Fights from a clawfoot bathtub on wheels. Drives like a vehicle: momentum,
  no strafing.
- **Light chain:** loofah slaps.
- **Heavy:** pulls the plug; a whirlpool drags nearby enemies in.
- **Dodge:** ducks underwater, leaving bubbles.
- **Tool:** a fizzing bath bomb that slows enemies.
- **Execution:** shampoos the enemy; the bubbles carry it off.

### The Flat-Pack

A sentient wardrobe.

- **Light chain:** door slams.
- **Tool:** a thrown Allen key that comes back.
- **Ability:** builds furniture mid-fight. A table gives cover, a bookcase
  blocks a tunnel, a bunk bed traps enemies. One part is always left over, so
  each build falls apart early.
- **Execution:** folds the enemy into a flat box with an unpronounceable name.

### The Mime

- Invisible walls that really block a tunnel, an invisible box that traps an
  enemy.
- The game goes completely silent while he is played.

### Three Raccoons in a Trench Coat

- Each hit taken knocks a raccoon loose: smaller, faster, weaker.
- Pick the raccoons back up to rebuild.

### The Intern

- Stapler, hole punch. Executes with a laminator.
- **Ability:** Reply All. Chips damage off every enemy in the cave and alerts
  them all. A gamble.

### The Opera Singer

- Holds a high note that shatters the caves' glowing crystals into bombs.
- The longer the note, the bigger the blast and the faster stamina drains.

### The Vending Machine

- **Light chain:** fires cans.
- **Heavy:** falls over onto enemies, then has to get back up.
- **Tool:** drops a snack that enemies fight over.
- **Ability:** OUT OF ORDER. Immune to damage but cannot move.

### Robot Vacuum With a Knife Taped On

- Most attacks swing over his head.
- Picks up drops by driving over them.

### The Wedding DJ

- Hits on the beat do double damage.
- **Ability:** drops the bass; enemies join a conga line and dance off.
