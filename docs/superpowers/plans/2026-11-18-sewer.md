# The Sewer (D gate, project 1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A sewer zone behind the D portal, with water that slows movement, D opening once E's boss is beaten, and a renderer that draws only the pieces of the sewer the camera can see.

**Architecture:** The cave's room-graph code is pulled out of `genWorld` into shared helpers. A new `genSewer` (in `src/03c_sewer.js`) uses those helpers with sewer shapes: polyline tunnels, rounded-rectangle rooms, a circular boss cavern, a water grid beside the walkable field, and a mesh split into visibility pieces. Water is read only through `world.waterAt`, which all three worlds return, and slows movement in the two places that integrate motion. A per-vertex material byte lets the world shader draw brick and moving water without changing the cave or hall.

**Tech Stack:** Plain JavaScript concatenated by `build.mjs`, WebGL2, Web Audio, Playwright tests through `test.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-sewer-d-gate-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Everything generated in code; no images, models or data loaded at run time.
- All `src/*.js` share one scope; declare each name once across `src/`.
- `node build.mjs` after every source change; the page that runs is `dist/index.html`.
- Collision, sight, routes, the camera and rubble read one walkable field (`fieldFns`). Water is not rock and blocks nothing.
- `genHub()`, `genWorld()` and `genSewer()` return the same shape. Every new field (`waterAt`, `bodyAt`, `bodies`, `grates`, `wallTunnels`, `shafts`, `pieces`) exists on all three.
- No reader tests "am I in the sewer" to find water. Water is read only through `world.waterAt(x, z)`.
- E's enemies never appear in a published D: `LIVE_GRADES` holds only `"E"` on this branch.
- `genWorld(seed)` output for a given seed is byte-identical before and after this work (tests t5, t12, t20, t21 rely on cave 99).
- `G.zone` stays `"cave"` for every grade's run; `G.grade` says which.
- Per-run state goes in `resetRun()`.
- Commit messages: no `Co-Authored-By` or `Claude-Session` trailers.

## Review Focus

1. **Old or odd saves.** A save with no `c` key, or a `c` that is not an object, loads with nothing cleared and D sealed, and the rest of the save still loads. Test in Task 3.
2. **Respawning in the sewer.** `respawn()` puts the player 1.6 m off the shrine. At every sewer rest point that spot must be dry, walkable ground, not water or wall. Test in Task 4.
3. **The camera outside every piece.** When the camera is pushed into rock or off the grid, `pieceAt` finds nothing. Drawing must then fall back to the player's piece list, never draw nothing. Test in Task 10.
4. **Class tools in the sewer.** A Sapper cave-in in a sewer tunnel still seals it. A rift end opened over water still carries the player. Test in Task 6.
5. **Reduced-motion entry.** `startTrans("D", true)` (the cut used under reduced motion) lands in the sewer with the sewer air and echo, the same as the fade. Test in Task 6.

### Task 4: `genSewer` layout and field

**Files:**
- Create: `src/03c_sewer.js`
- Modify: `src/09_main.js` (`DF.genSewer`, `DF.SEWER`)
- Test: `tests/t22.mjs`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `planGraph`, `linkGraph`, `fieldFns`, `meshSink`, `marchWalls` (Task 1).
- Produces:
  - `const SEWER = { GX: 5, GZ: 4, SP: 50, tunR: 3.4, bossR: 26, ... }`. Every layout number lives here; the builder adds the rest.
  - `genSewer(seed)` returns the world shape plus `sewer: true`, `F` (the field), `Wt` (the water grid, all 0 until Task 5), `bodyIds` (`Int16Array`, all -1 until Task 5).
  - **Nodes.** Every room node has `hw` and `hd` (half width in X and half depth in Z) and `r = min(hw, hd)`. Kinds are `"start"`, `"pool"`, `"brood"`, `"shrine"` and `"boss"`. The start is an extra node outside the grid, joined only to grid corner (0, 0) by one tunnel, so the arrival hall has one exit at its far end. It is a rounded rectangle about 9 by 7 m half-size. The boss is the far corner, a disc of radius `SEWER.bossR`, with `hw = hd = r = SEWER.bossR`. Medium rooms are rounded rectangles of half-size 10 to 15 by 8 to 12, with corner radius 3, axis-aligned, and larger than E's chambers. A room with both half-sizes of at least 12 gets one or two pillars (negative discs of radius 0.8 in the field).
  - **Edges.** Each tunnel is `e.pts`, a polyline from A's centre to B's centre with 3 to 6 bends. It is built of straight runs with lateral offsets, each bend turning at most 70 degrees, with `r = SEWER.tunR` on every point. Its field is `tunR` minus the distance to the polyline. No noise is added.
  - **Crumble.** 3 to 6 spots on tunnel walls, each adding `vnoise` roughness to the field within 2.5 m. The spots are listed as `w.crumble: [{ x, z }]`.
  - **Rest points.** `w.shrines` holds 4 entries: the start, and the path nodes at `round((L-1)/3)`, `round(2(L-1)/3)` and `L-2`, where `L = path.length`. These are deduplicated, with the graph sized so that all four are distinct. Rest rooms get kind `"shrine"` and no spawns.
  - **Kinds.** The other rooms alternate pool and brood by a seeded shuffle, so that both appear. Every non-start, non-shrine, non-boss room is pool or brood.
  - **Spawns.** Each spawn is `{ type, x, z, yaw, node, kind }`, with `kind` the room kind or `"tunnel"`. The stand-ins use E's types (pool: husk and spit; brood: brute and husks; tunnel: husk, as in `genWorld`), and the boss is `"warden"` on the dry ring (Task 5 makes the ring dry), facing the entrance. No spawn stands in a rest room.
  - **Ceiling.** `ceilAt(x, z)` comes from `fieldAt`, so rubble lowers it. It is at least 3.2 wherever `fieldAt >= 0.6`, at least 6 at a medium room's centre, and at least 12 in the boss cavern beyond 6 m from its wall.
